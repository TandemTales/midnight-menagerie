# Midnight Menagerie: Mateo Alvarez and Pepper Complete Personal Investigation Design
## 1. Purpose of Mateo's investigation
Mateo Alvarez's missing pet story should reveal a different part of the mansion's behavior than Maya and Orbit's story.
Maya discovers that the House knowingly keeps animals that have homes.
Mateo discovers something more troubling:
Sometimes the House does not merely wait for lost animals to arrive. It reaches out to animals it believes need it.
Pepper heard the mansion before any of the Kids understood that the mansion was supernatural.
The phrase Pepper repeatedly said before disappearing was not random mimicry.
"Open the little door."
Someone inside the House was talking to him.
Mateo's investigation therefore revolves around:
Repeated phrases
Mimicked voices
Supernatural recordings
Clues
Ghost Writing
Mansion bells and announcements
Animal intake records
Curiosities involving communication
Persistent Blueprint annotations
Distinguishing genuine Pepper speech from supernatural imitation
Determining who was speaking to Pepper
Understanding what Pepper himself was trying to communicate
Mateo's story should make players pay close attention to words they have already heard.
Information discovered early can acquire completely different meaning later.
2. Mateo AlvarezAge: 12
Gender: Boy
Background: Mexican American
Appearance: Round glasses
Missing pet: Pepper
Pet: Green cheek conure
Kid gameplay identity: Clues, persistent investigation, and interpretation of previously discovered information
### Personality
Mateo is curious, enthusiastic, talkative, easily fascinated, and incapable of accepting "probably nothing" as an explanation.
He takes notes about almost everything.
He likes patterns.
He asks follow up questions.
He remembers strange details from Curiosities that everyone else has already forgotten.
He sometimes reaches conclusions too quickly because he is so excited to have found a pattern.
Part of his character development involves learning the difference between:
Finding a connection
and
Proving a connection.
This makes him a natural representation of the game's persistent investigation system.
3. Pepper before the disappearancePepper is a green cheek conure with green feathers, a dark gray head, a reddish tail, and a particularly expressive face.
He is clever, social, noisy, possessive of Mateo, and prone to repeating sounds at inconvenient times.
Pepper knows a modest collection of actual words and a much larger collection of sounds.
Normal Pepper phrases include:
"Mateo!"
"Hi Pepper!"
"Snack?"
"Come here."
"No!"
"Good bird."
Pepper also imitates:
Mateo's phone alarm
Microwave beeps
The front door chime
Mateo laughing
A squeaky cabinet hinge
The family dog's bark from next door
Mateo's mother's call for dinner
Pepper often changes the tone or context of sounds.
Mateo therefore knows that simple repetition does not necessarily mean Pepper understands what he is saying.
That becomes central to the investigation.
4. The strange new phraseSeveral nights before Pepper disappears, he begins saying something nobody recognizes:
"Open the little door."
At first Mateo finds it funny.
He assumes Pepper heard it from:
Television
A neighbor
A video
A family member
Mateo himself without Mateo remembering
Pepper keeps repeating it.
But he only says it after dark.
More specifically, he says it when facing the window on the side of the house pointing toward the old mansion.
Pepper sometimes pauses afterward as though listening.
Then he says:
"Okay."
Nobody taught Pepper that sequence.
5. Pepper's disappearanceThe evening Pepper disappears, Mateo hears him calling from his room.
Pepper: "Open the little door."
Mateo calls back:
Mateo: "What little door?"
Pepper answers:
Pepper: "Okay."
When Mateo enters the room later, Pepper is gone.
The main enclosure door is still closed.
One small feeding door on the side is open.
A few feathers lie near the window.
The window itself is slightly open.
Nothing indicates violence.
Outside, Mateo eventually finds one small green feather farther toward the mansion.
That becomes his first physical clue.
6. What actually happenedThe game should maintain a fixed internal explanation.
Pepper had been hearing the House for several nights.
Animals are more sensitive than humans to some of the mansion's supernatural communication.
The House detected Pepper repeatedly calling when members of the family were in other rooms.
It incorrectly interpreted ordinary parrot social calling as distress.
To the House, Pepper appeared to be:
Confined
Calling for company
Frequently alone
The House concluded that Pepper needed sanctuary.
Rather than physically entering Mateo's house, it began communicating with Pepper through a supernatural voice.
The voice encouraged him:
"Open the little door."
This referred to the small feeding door on his enclosure.
Once Pepper learned to open it, the House began producing familiar sounds outside.
A door chime.
Mateo's voice.
A whistle.
Other bird calls.
Pepper followed them.
An impossible route eventually led him onto the mansion property.
The House then accepted him as a voluntary arrival.
This logic is deeply flawed.
The House created the circumstances under which Pepper "chose" to come.
7. Why Pepper was particularly interesting to the HousePepper did something unusual once inside.
He repeated the House's own supernatural voice back to it.
The House had rarely encountered an animal capable of doing this so precisely.
Pepper began mimicking:
Ghost Writing spoken aloud
Bells
House announcements
Old voices
Door calls
Names
Instructions
The mansion interpreted Pepper as unusually receptive to it.
Instead of simply placing him in an animal ward, it began encouraging this ability.
This accelerates his transformation.
8. Pepper's transformation conceptPepper's emerging supernatural identity centers on:
Echoes, voices, communication, memory, and supernatural mimicry.
The internal working concept is:
Echo Bird
This is not an additional playable Companion.
Pepper is rescued before the transformation becomes permanent.
9. Pepper transformation stagesStage One: Normal PepperPepper behaves normally.
He can mimic sounds he has actually heard.
Stage Two: Impossible MimicryPepper begins repeating sounds that should be impossible for him to have heard.
Examples include:
Conversations occurring several rooms away
Voices from empty rooms
Bells before they ring
Ghost Writing as spoken sentences
Animal names Pepper has never been taught
His feathers remain physically normal.
## Stage Three: Echo Adaptation
Pepper's mimicry becomes supernatural.
He can reproduce not simply the sound of a voice, but traces of what accompanied it.
Possible manifestations include:
Repeating a voice with exactly the same supernatural resonance
Causing nearby Ghost Writing to briefly appear while speaking
Repeating a bell and making a distant door react
Echoing the last few words spoken in a room long before he entered it
Producing two overlapping voices at once
Feathers faintly displaying moving line patterns similar to written words
Hearing House messages that humans cannot perceive
Pepper remains himself.
He still recognizes Mateo.
He still uses his normal favorite phrases.
Stage Four: Projected Menagerie formThe Transformation Gallery can show what Pepper might eventually become.
His plumage would develop faint luminous script like patterns.
His voice could contain whole choruses.
He might reproduce the remembered voices of a room.
A call could awaken sleeping writing or make old doors answer.
The form should be beautiful, strange, and slightly uncanny.
It is not treated as corruption.
The ethical issue remains the same:
Pepper never chose this future.
10. Transformation progression rulePepper's transformation advances with his investigation story.
It never advances because:
The player spent too many expeditions
Mateo lost an expedition
The player explored optional content
Haunt Level increased
The player chose another Kid for several runs
Pepper can always be rescued through normal campaign completion.
11. Mateo investigation phasesMateo's investigation has five phases.
## Phase One
Pepper Heard Something
Mateo proves Pepper's strange phrase originated inside the mansion.
## Phase Two
Something Answered
Mateo discovers that the mansion deliberately communicated with Pepper before his disappearance.
## Phase Three
Pepper Is Echoing the House
Mateo discovers Pepper's supernatural mimicry.
## Phase Four
Follow the Voice
Pepper begins deliberately leaving messages that Mateo can follow toward the Heart.
## Phase Five
Were You Listening?
Mateo finds Pepper and confronts the House over the difference between hearing someone and actually understanding them.
