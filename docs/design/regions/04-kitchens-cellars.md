# The Kitchens and Cellars
Companion: Taffy Boss: The Confectioner Region identity: Splitting, recombining, consuming, transforming, copying, and enemies changing physical composition during battle
The Kitchens and Cellars should feel chaotic compared with the Sleeping Quarters.
This region is full of things that refuse to stay in one shape.
Dough rises.
Jam leaks out of jars and crawls across countertops.
Candy creatures fuse together.
Utensils become limbs.
Ovens transform harmless ingredients into dangerous enemies.
The central combat lesson is:
The enemy you are fighting now may not be the enemy you are fighting next turn.
Taffy naturally understands this because Taffy’s own combat identity revolves around splitting, recombining, stretching, absorbing, copying, and transformation.

# 2. Dough Blob
Role: Splitting enemy Courage: 34
A mound of supernatural dough with a face pressed into its surface.
It stretches, bubbles, and keeps trying to become more dough.
### Core mechanic: Divide
When Dough Blob falls to 16 Courage or less for the first time, it uses:
Divide
The original Dough Blob disappears.
It becomes two Doughlings.
Each Doughling has 11 Courage.
This does not consume the Dough Blob's normal turn.
The split occurs immediately after the damage resolves.
### Doughling moves
Sticky Slap
Deal 5 damage.
Squish Together
If two Doughlings are alive, both gain 4 Guard.
### Preventing the split
If Dough Blob is reduced from above 16 Courage directly to 0 in one hit or one resolving effect, it does not Divide.
This gives burst damage a distinct advantage.
### Dough Blob moves
Knead
Gain 8 Guard.
Dough Slam
Deal 8 damage.
Rise
Recover 4 Courage.
Gain 3 Guard.
Rise cannot raise it above its original 34 maximum Courage.
### Pattern
Knead.
Dough Slam.
Rise.
Dough Slam.
Repeat.
### Combat lesson
Damage thresholds can create new enemies.
Sometimes killing something cleanly is better than simply damaging it efficiently.
# 3. Jam Jar
Role: Deck interference and support Courage: 25
A glass jar with sticky purple jam sloshing around inside.
The jar walks on two teaspoons.
### Core mechanic: Sticky
Jam Jar can add Sticky to the player's discard pile.
Sticky:
Cost 1 Nerve.
Draw 1 Trick.
Remove this Trick from combat after playing it.
Sticky is not a dead Trick.
It slows the player down by charging Nerve for card replacement.
### Moves
Spill
Add 1 Sticky to the player's discard pile.
Jam Splash
Deal 5 damage.
The next Trick the player draws through an effect this turn costs 1 additional Nerve.
This applies only once.
Seal the Lid
Gain 11 Guard.
### Pattern
Spill.
Jam Splash.
Seal the Lid.
Repeat.
### Design purpose
Jam Jar introduces a different form of deck interference from Lost Luggage and Pillow Puff.
The status is still usable, but it interferes with tempo rather than hand size.
# 4. Kitchen Imp
Role: Fast ordinary attacker Courage: 29
A tiny creature assembled from utensils.
Fork hands.
Whisk tail.
Measuring cup helmet.
### Core mechanic: Grab a Tool
Every few turns, Kitchen Imp grabs one random utensil.
The utensil modifies its next attack.
Possible utensils:
### Fork
Next attack deals 3 additional damage.
### Whisk
Next attack hits one additional time at half damage.
### Ladle
After its next attack, Kitchen Imp gains 6 Guard.
### Moves
Grab a Tool
Gain one utensil.
Utensil Jab
Deal 7 damage.
Apply the held utensil effect.
Then discard the utensil.
Countertop Scamper
Deal 4 damage twice.
### Behavior
Grab a Tool.
Utensil Jab.
Countertop Scamper.
Repeat.
### Combat lesson
Enemy states can temporarily modify familiar attacks.
The player can plan around the visible utensil.
# 5. Candy Clump
Role: Absorption enemy Courage: 38
Several pieces of hard candy have melted together into a brightly colored lumpy creature.
Bits of wrapper remain stuck to it.
### Core mechanic: Absorb
Whenever another enemy is defeated, Candy Clump absorbs part of it.
It gains a different bonus depending on what kind of enemy died.
### If a Dough enemy dies
Gain 6 maximum Courage.
Recover 6 Courage.
### If Jam Jar dies
Its next attack applies Sticky.
### If Kitchen Imp dies
Gain the Imp's current utensil effect for its next attack.
### If another Candy Clump dies
Gain 2 permanent attack damage.
### If Pantry Mimic dies
Gain 10 Guard.
### If Rising Batter dies
Gain 1 Sweetness.
Each Sweetness increases attack damage by 2.
Maximum 3.
### Moves
Candy Bash
Deal 9 damage.
Harden
Gain 10 Guard.
Sugar Rush
Deal 5 damage twice.
If it has at least 1 Sweetness, deal one additional 5 damage hit.
### Behavior
Candy Bash.
Harden.
Sugar Rush.
Repeat.
### Strategic question
Do you kill Candy Clump first?
Or accept that it will absorb other enemies while you remove more urgent threats?
# 6. Pantry Mimic
Role: Transformation and deception enemy Courage: 35
It looks like a sack of flour.
Or a crate.
Or an ingredient cupboard.
Then teeth appear.
### Core mechanic: Disguise
Pantry Mimic begins combat Disguised.
While Disguised:
Its exact Courage is hidden.
The player sees only:
Low
Medium
or
High
Courage.
The Mimic remains targetable.
The first time it takes damage:
It Reveals itself.
Its exact Courage becomes visible.
It immediately gains 5 Guard.
### Moves
Stay Still
Gain 6 Guard.
Remain Disguised if possible.
Pantry Bite
Deal 10 damage.
Swallow Something
Remove the top Trick of the player's draw pile from combat temporarily.
The Trick is shown beside Pantry Mimic.

### Pattern

Pantry Bite.
Swallow Something.

Repeat.
### Design purpose
This enemy attacks information and temporary deck access without permanently damaging the player's deck.
# 7. Rising Batter
Role: Escalation enemy Courage: 40
A mixing bowl overflowing with batter that has grown legs.
The batter keeps rising over the rim.
### Core mechanic: Rise
Rising Batter begins with 0 Rise.
Maximum 4.
At the end of every player turn in which Rising Batter took no damage:
Gain 1 Rise.
Each Rise gives:
2 additional attack damage.
At 4 Rise, it also gains 5 Guard at the start of its turn.
### Moves
Batter Flop
Deal 7 damage plus 2 per Rise.
Overflow
Gain 7 Guard.
If at 2 or more Rise, add 1 Sticky to the player's discard pile.
Collapse
Deal 10 damage.
Remove 2 Rise.
Collapse is used only at 3 or 4 Rise.
### Behavior
At 0 to 2 Rise:
Batter Flop.
Overflow.
Repeat.
At 3 or 4 Rise:
Collapse.
Then return to normal pattern.
### Combat lesson
Like Dust Bunny, ignoring it is dangerous.
Unlike Dust Bunny, its escalation changes its whole behavior rather than only one attack.

# 11. Encounter generation rules
Candy Clump cannot appear before the player has encountered at least one other enemy type that it can Absorb.
Pantry Mimic cannot appear in the first Scuffle.
Rising Batter cannot appear alongside Candy Clump until the advanced pool.
Two Candy Clumps cannot appear together at baseline difficulty.
Two Rising Batters cannot appear together.
Dough Blob should first appear alone before appearing with Candy Clump.
Scuffle 14 belongs only to the advanced pool.
# 12. Big Scare: The Oven Maw
Courage: 118
Role: Transformation engine
A huge cast iron oven has grown legs and teeth.
Its door opens like a mouth.
Heat glows behind hundreds of tiny eyes.
### Core mechanic: Bake
The Oven Maw can place another enemy inside itself.
That enemy becomes Baking.
While Baking:
It is removed from the battlefield.
It does not act.
It cannot be targeted.
After two enemy turns, it emerges in a transformed form.
### Possible baked transformations
### Dough Blob becomes Crust Beast
Courage: 42
Attacks deal more damage.
Cannot Divide.
### Jam Jar becomes Caramel Creeper
Courage: 31
Sticky generated by it costs 2 Nerve instead of 1.
The Sticky still draws 1 Trick.
### Kitchen Imp becomes Cutlery Devil
Courage: 36
Begins with two utensil effects.
### Candy Clump becomes Brittle Candy Beast
Courage: 44
Deals more damage but takes 25 percent additional Attack damage.
### Rising Batter becomes Cake Thing
Courage: 46
No longer gains Rise.
Instead gains 4 Guard every turn and attacks consistently.
### The Oven Maw can create enemies
If no valid enemy exists to Bake:
It uses Mix Ingredients.
Summon one Dough Blob at 50 percent Courage.
### Moves
Bake
Place one eligible enemy inside.
Oven Door Slam
Deal 14 damage.
Heat Wave
Deal 6 damage.
Add 1 Sticky to the player's discard pile.
Preheat
Gain 14 Guard.
The next baked enemy emerges one turn earlier.
### Behavior
Mix Ingredients if alone.
Bake.
Oven Door Slam.
Heat Wave.
Preheat.
Repeat.
### Strategic question
Do you kill potential ingredients before they transform?
Damage the Oven Maw directly?
Or let it temporarily remove an annoying enemy and deal with the transformed version later?
# 13. Big Scare: The Sugar Golem
Courage: 134
Role: Multi stage disassembly encounter
A massive sparkling creature made of crystallized sugar, candy glass, caramel, and brittle sweets.
### Core mechanic: Layers
The Sugar Golem has three physical layers.
### Outer Layer: Candy Shell
From 134 to 91 Courage.
Gain 6 Guard at the start of every turn.
Attacks deal normal damage.
### Middle Layer: Caramel Core
From 90 to 46 Courage.
No automatic Guard.
Whenever the player plays a third Attack Trick in one turn, add 1 Sticky to their discard pile.
This triggers once per turn.
### Inner Layer: Sugar Crystal
45 Courage or less.
Takes 25 percent additional damage.
Attacks deal 4 additional damage.
### Moves
Sugar Fist
Deal 12 damage.
Candy Shards
Deal 4 damage three times.
Harden
Gain 14 Guard.
Crack Apart
Used whenever entering a new Layer.
Deal 5 damage.
Gain 8 Guard.
Then immediately adopt the new Layer.
### Final death effect
When defeated:
Sugar Collapse
The player gains 6 Guard.
Why?
Because the entire thing collapses into an enormous pile of soft powdered sugar.
It is a small comic reward for surviving the encounter.
# 14. Big Scare: The Pantry Poltergeist
Courage: 108
Role: Constant transformation and battlefield composition
The Pantry Poltergeist is mostly invisible.
You know where it is because food, pans, jars, utensils, and sacks keep flying around the room.
### Core mechanic: Possess
The Pantry Poltergeist begins with three Pantry Objects floating beside it.
They are not separate enemies.
Each object changes one of its moves.
The three starting objects are randomly chosen from:
### Frying Pan
Its basic attack deals 4 additional damage.
### Flour Sack
Every third turn adds 1 Sticky.
### Cleaver
Its multi hit attack gains one additional hit.
### Mixing Bowl
Whenever it gains Guard, gain 3 additional Guard.
### Spice Tin
Its next damaging attack applies Frightened.
### Rolling Pin
After attacking, gain 5 Guard.
### Move: Throw Something
Use one currently possessed object's effect.
Then discard that object.
### Move: Possess Another
Gain one random new Pantry Object not currently held.
Maximum 3.
### Other moves
Kitchen Storm
Deal 3 damage three times.
Each current object may modify this attack.
Cupboard Slam
Deal 11 damage.
Ransack
Gain 10 Guard.
Possess Another.
### Behavior
Throw Something.
Kitchen Storm.
Possess Another.
Cupboard Slam.
Ransack.
Repeat.
### Strategic identity
The player sees the objects clearly.
The fight is variable but not opaque.
The challenge is reading what combination of objects currently matters.
# 15. The Confectioner
Boss Courage: 305
The Confectioner is a tall, elegant figure in a flour covered apron.
Its face resembles a smiling porcelain chef mask.
Its arms are long enough to reach every countertop in the room.
Its fingers end in piping bags, knives, spoons, whisks, and sugar pulling hooks.
It does not merely cook food.
It believes everything can be made better by combining it with something else.
Its philosophy is:
Nothing is finished until it becomes something new.
That makes it a natural thematic opposite for Taffy.
Taffy changes because Taffy wants to.
The Confectioner changes things because it assumes transformation is improvement.
# 16. The Confectioner's core mechanic: Recipe
The Confectioner has a visible Recipe Board.
A Recipe consists of three Ingredient slots.
Possible Ingredients:
Dough
Jam
Sugar
Spice
Cream
Each Ingredient modifies the next completed Recipe.
The Confectioner adds Ingredients through its moves.
When all three slots are filled:
It immediately creates a Dish.
Then the Recipe clears.
The player always sees exactly which Ingredients are currently present.
# 17. Ingredient effects
## Dough
The resulting Dish gains additional Courage.
## Jam
The Dish adds Sticky through one of its moves.
## Sugar
The Dish deals additional attack damage.
## Spice
The Dish applies Frightened on its first attack.
## Cream
The Dish gains Guard every turn.
Duplicates are allowed.
Three Sugar Ingredients therefore create a very aggressive Dish.
Three Cream Ingredients create a defensive one.
# 18. Dishes
The Dish is a secondary enemy summoned by The Confectioner.
Only one Dish can exist at a time.
If a new Recipe completes while another Dish exists:
The existing Dish is Replated.
It receives the new Recipe's effects instead of creating a second enemy.
Baseline Dish:
Courage: 38
Nibble
Deal 7 damage.
Set
Gain 7 Guard.
It alternates between these two moves.
The Recipe modifies it.
### Dough
Plus 8 maximum Courage per Dough.
Recover the same amount when created.
### Jam
Every Nibble adds 1 Sticky.
Multiple Jam Ingredients do not add multiple Sticky.
Instead, with 2 Jam, Sticky goes to the draw pile rather than discard.
With 3 Jam, the Sticky also costs 2 Nerve.
### Sugar
Nibble deals 3 additional damage per Sugar.
### Spice
First Nibble applies Frightened.
With 2 Spice, every second Nibble applies Frightened.
With 3 Spice, every Nibble applies Frightened.
### Cream
Gain 4 Guard at the start of each turn per Cream.
# 19. Destroying the Dish
When a Dish is defeated:
The Confectioner becomes Offended.
Offended lasts until the end of its next turn.
While Offended:
The Confectioner deals 3 additional attack damage.
However:
The Confectioner cannot add Ingredients while Offended.
This means destroying the Dish creates both a danger and a breathing window.
# 20. Confectioner phase one
From 305 through 171 Courage.
The fight begins with:
1 random Ingredient already on the Recipe Board.
### Move: Add Ingredient
Add one random Ingredient that is not currently the most common Ingredient on the board.
This prevents the Recipe from becoming triple copies too frequently at baseline difficulty.
Gain 6 Guard.
### Move: Sugar Hook
Deal 11 damage.
### Move: Taste Test
Deal 5 damage twice.
If a Dish exists, the Dish recovers 5 Courage.
### Move: Stir Everything
Add one random Ingredient.
If a Dish exists:
Give it 7 Guard.
### Opening pattern
Add Ingredient.
Sugar Hook.
Stir Everything.
Taste Test.
Sugar Hook.
Repeat.
Recipe completion can interrupt the rhythm by producing a Dish.
# 21. Player interaction with the Recipe Board
The player should have a limited way to influence Recipes.
Whenever The Confectioner loses at least 24 Courage during one player turn:
The most recently added Ingredient becomes Spilled.
Remove it from the Recipe.
This can happen once per player turn.
### Why this matters
The player can inspect the Recipe and decide:
That third Sugar is unacceptable.
That Cream is fine.
I actually want the Dish to appear now because I can exploit it.
Again, the mechanic creates agency rather than random punishment.
# 22. Phase transition
At 170 Courage:
The Confectioner uses:
Enough Measuring
Clear the current Recipe.
Destroy the current Dish if one exists.
No Offended effect is triggered.
The Confectioner throws away measuring cups, recipes, and utensils.
A giant vat of supernatural candy begins bubbling behind it.
Phase two begins.
# 23. Phase two core mechanic: Improvisation
The Recipe Board now has only two Ingredient slots.
Recipes therefore complete faster.
In addition:
The Confectioner now personally absorbs one Ingredient effect from each completed Recipe.
The Dish receives the other.
### Example
Recipe:
Sugar plus Cream.
The Dish may receive Sugar.
The Confectioner receives Cream.
The assignment is shown before the player acts.
The effects alternate in priority so the system remains predictable.
# 24. Confectioner personal Ingredient effects
### Dough
Gain 12 Guard immediately when acquired.
### Jam
Its next attack adds 1 Sticky.
### Sugar
Its next attack deals 5 additional damage.
### Spice
Its next attack applies Frightened.
### Cream
Recover 7 Courage.
Each personal Ingredient effect is consumed after triggering.
# 25. Phase two moves
### Candy Cleaver
Deal 15 damage.
### Whisking Frenzy
Deal 4 damage four times.
### Toss It In
Add one Ingredient.
Gain 5 Guard.
### No Recipe Needed
Add one Ingredient.
If this completes the Recipe:
The resulting Dish immediately acts once after The Confectioner finishes its turn.
### Lick the Spoon
Recover 8 Courage.

### Pattern
Toss It In.
Candy Cleaver.
No Recipe Needed.

# 26. Phase two Recipe disruption
The player can still Spill the latest Ingredient.
Threshold:
20 Courage lost during one player turn.
Because Recipes use only two Ingredients now, disruption becomes more important.
# 27. Final escalation
At 70 Courage or less:
The Confectioner gains:
Sugar Rush
Its damaging moves deal 2 additional damage.
The Recipe Board remains unchanged.
It does not gain another full phase.
This prevents the fight from becoming overcomplicated.
The final stretch is simply faster and more dangerous.
# 28. Why Taffy interacts well with The Confectioner
Taffy should feel unusually expressive here without receiving numerical bonuses.
### Splitting
Taffy may be particularly good at handling Dough Blob, Sugar Golem stages, and summoned Dishes through distributed damage.
### Recombining
The Confectioner's Recipes mirror Taffy's own identity in a distorted way.
Taffy chooses how to combine pieces.
The Confectioner imposes combinations.
### Copying
A Taffy build that copies Tricks may save strong copies for Recipe disruption thresholds.
### Absorbing
Candy Clump and The Confectioner establish thematic parallels to Taffy's absorption mechanics.
### Transformation
Taffy is comfortable with changing Trick forms, meaning a player familiar with Taffy already thinks in terms of temporary states and altered properties.
The region should feel intuitive to Taffy players without being trivial.
# 29. Boss narrative outcome
### Taffy not yet rescued
The Confectioner has been repeatedly melting Taffy down, adding ingredients, reshaping Taffy, and trying to discover the "perfect recipe."
Taffy keeps returning to a preferred form.
The Confectioner interprets that as stubbornness rather than identity.
Defeating The Confectioner lets Taffy decide what shape Taffy wants to be.
### Taffy already rescued and currently active
This becomes Taffy's Legacy homecoming.
The narrative emphasis is:
Transformation is not the problem.
Who chooses the transformation is the problem.
### Another Companion active
Normal regional boss reward.
No Taffy Legacy advancement.
# 30. Multiplayer scaling
Use the established baseline.
2 players:
160 percent Courage.
3 players:
210 percent.
4 players:
255 percent.
Mechanics should create more interaction rather than merely increasing enemy health.
# 31. Dough Blob multiplayer
Divide threshold remains at approximately 47 percent maximum Courage.
When it Divides:
Number of Doughlings remains 2.
Doughling Courage scales normally.
This prevents enemy count from exploding in four player combat.
# 32. Jam Jar multiplayer
Spill targets one player.
Prefer the player with the fewest Tricks in their discard pile.
This increases the likelihood that Sticky cycles soon.
Jam Splash targets one player.
# 33. Kitchen Imp multiplayer
The held utensil remains global.
Utensil Jab targets one player.
Whisk's extra hit may target a different player.
# 34. Candy Clump multiplayer
Absorb remains unchanged.
Sugar Rush attacks one target per hit.
If it has Sweetness, the extra hit targets the player with the lowest Guard.
# 35. Pantry Mimic multiplayer
Swallow Something targets one player's draw pile.
The stolen Trick remains visible to the entire team.
That player gets the Trick back when the Mimic is defeated.
This can create a strong cooperative reason to focus the Mimic.
# 36. Rising Batter multiplayer
Rise checks whether any player damaged it during the team round.
If at least one player did, it gains no passive Rise.
This encourages assigning one player to keep it controlled.
# 37. Oven Maw multiplayer
Maximum active summoned or transformed enemies:
2 with one or two players.
3 with three or four players.
Baking remains shared.
Players can coordinate around which enemy gets allowed into the Oven.
# 38. Sugar Golem multiplayer
Layer thresholds remain proportional.
Its multi hit attacks distribute hits among players.
Candy Shards must target at least two different players when possible.
# 39. Pantry Poltergeist multiplayer
Pantry Objects remain global.
The player team sees all current objects.
Spice based Frightened targets the player struck by the relevant attack.
# 40. The Confectioner multiplayer
The Recipe Board is shared.
All players can contribute damage toward spilling Ingredients.
### Spill threshold
2 players:
32 Courage during the team round.
3 players:
42 Courage.
4 players:
51 Courage.
A Recipe can be disrupted only once per team round.
### Dish targeting
The Dish chooses targets independently from The Confectioner.
This creates genuine two threat encounters.
### Offended
When players defeat a Dish:
The Confectioner's 3 bonus damage applies regardless of player count.
It does not stack from multiple Dish defeats.
# 41. Multiplayer cooperative opportunities
One player can focus on maintaining pressure against Rising Batter.
Another can remove Jam Jar.
A player with strong burst can save it to Spill a dangerous Recipe Ingredient.
A player with wide damage can clear Doughlings.
A defensive player can prepare for an aggressive Sugar Dish while teammates focus The Confectioner.
A player whose important Trick was swallowed by Pantry Mimic can ask teammates to prioritize it.
This region should feel particularly dynamic in cooperative play.
# 42. Haunt Level progression
### Haunt 1
Ordinary enemies gain approximately 8 percent Courage.
Big Scares and The Confectioner gain approximately 6 percent.
### Haunt 2
Advanced formations appear earlier.
### Haunt 3
Dough Blob Divides at 18 Courage rather than 16.
The split becomes harder to skip accidentally.
### Haunt 4
Kitchen Imp begins advanced Scuffles already holding one random utensil.
### Haunt 5
Jam Jar's first Spill adds Sticky directly to the draw pile.
Later uses go to discard.
### Haunt 6
Candy Clump begins advanced encounters with 1 Sweetness.
### Haunt 7
Rising Batter gains 1 Rise if it takes fewer than 5 total damage during a player turn rather than requiring zero damage.
### Haunt 8
Pantry Mimic begins with its first swallowed Trick already taken from the bottom three Tricks of the player's draw pile.
The player is shown which Trick was taken immediately.
### Haunt 9
Big Scares upgrade.
Oven Maw's first baked enemy emerges one turn sooner.
Sugar Golem gains 8 Guard on each Layer transition.
Pantry Poltergeist begins with 4 Pantry Objects instead of 3.
### Haunt 10
The Confectioner begins with two Ingredients on the phase one Recipe Board.
The first Dish can therefore arrive quickly.
# 43. Higher Haunt behavior upgrades
Potential later behavioral changes include:
Doughlings may recombine into a Dough Blob if both survive long enough.
Jam Jar may stick two Tricks together temporarily, causing them to be drawn together.
Kitchen Imp may swap utensils based on the player's current Guard.
Candy Clump may choose not to Absorb an enemy if a different future absorption would be stronger.
Pantry Mimic may swallow a Trick from the player's hand instead of the draw pile.
Oven Maw may bake itself temporarily to gain a new form.
The Confectioner may deliberately Spill one of its own Ingredients and replace it with a more dangerous choice.
These should be reserved for high mastery difficulty because they significantly increase complexity.

# 47. What the Kitchens and Cellars teach
By the time the player reaches The Confectioner, the region has taught:
Enemies can split into new targets.
Some transformations can be prevented through burst damage.
Defeating one enemy can strengthen another.
Enemies can temporarily remove Tricks from circulation.
Enemy states may represent tools or ingredients rather than simple buffs.
Some enemies become completely different forms during battle.
Enemy composition may change repeatedly within one encounter.
The player can sometimes manipulate or delay enemy transformations.
Transformation itself is neither positive nor negative.
Control over transformation is what matters.
That last idea makes the region particularly important for Taffy's story.
