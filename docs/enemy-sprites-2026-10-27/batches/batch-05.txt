create a composite image of sprites of the following enemies in a turn based ccg. make sure the final image has a truly transparent background. Make sure the images are good quality and will 2D animate well (nothing in mid-action pose, extra floating parts around it, etc.). Make sure the enemies match the visual style of the included menu screen with companion characters from another part of the game:

1. Hanging Pots (Level/Region 4)
Cluster of copper pots and pans swinging down from a ceiling rack under invisible control.

2. Boiling Pot (Level/Region 4)
Huge stockpot sliding across the stove or floor while steam forms temporary faces and hands above it.

3. The Hungry Dumbwaiter (Level/Region 4)
A large antique service lift that seems to connect to impossible locations.
Each time its doors open, something different appears inside:
A completed dinner.
A pile of dishes.
A spectral waiter.
A dark empty shaft.
An entirely different kitchen.
A moonlit hallway.
The threat comes from what arrives rather than from giving the dumbwaiter monster anatomy.

4. The Cellar Steward (Level/Region 4)
A tall translucent household servant in rolled sleeves, waistcoat, and apron carrying a lantern and inventory ledger.
Wine bottles and barrels move around them without being touched.
They should appear meticulous rather than threatening.

5. The Confectioner (Level/Region 4)
Keep the existing role, but redesign the character away from a conventional evil chef.
The Confectioner is a spectral pastry chef whose body is partly formed from flour, steam, sugar dust, and translucent kitchen light.
Possible visual traits:
Tall old fashioned chef's coat.
Large toque fading into steam at the top.
Hands sometimes disappear entirely while utensils continue moving.
Pastries and ingredients orbit nearby preparation tables.
Sugar sculptures animate briefly when commanded.
Flour outlines portions of the body that otherwise disappear.
Reflections in polished cookware show a different expression than the actual ghost.
The Confectioner should conduct the kitchen like an orchestra.
This is the strongest choice if you want to preserve the current boss identity.

6. Walking Planter (Level/Region 5)
Large ceramic planter gliding across the floor while its roots drag behind it and rearrange nearby plants.
