create a composite image of sprites of the following enemies in a turn based ccg. make sure the final image has a truly transparent background. Make sure the images are good quality and will 2D animate well (nothing in mid-action pose, extra floating parts around it, etc.). Make sure the enemies match the visual style of the included menu screen with companion characters from another part of the game:

1. The Eternal Dance (Level/Region 10)
A pair of spectral ballroom dancers locked in a perfect waltz.
They should function as one unit and always be posed elegantly in side view.
One can lead and one can follow, with flowing coats and skirts making the animation readable.
This is one of the strongest elite concepts for the region.

2. The Velvet Host (Level/Region 10)
A tall, faceless figure in a rich velvet tailcoat carrying a serving tray or goblet.
The body can be mostly smoke and cloth with very clean silhouette language.
This reads well as a side view mini boss and feels strongly ballroom themed.

3. The Ballroom Chandelier (Level/Region 10)
A lowered chandelier suspended on chains, drifting and rotating while crystal drops and candles swing.
This one is slightly harder than humanoid elites, but still very usable in side view because its silhouette is iconic.

4. The Master of Revels (Level/Region 10)
This is still the strongest character boss if you want to preserve canon.
A tall supernatural host in ornate crimson or burgundy formalwear with a conductor’s baton, cane, or goblet.
For side view animation, the key is to make the silhouette very readable:
high collar
long coat tails
one arm often gesturing outward
masked or porcelain like face in profile
ribbons or sash trailing behind
He should feel like the spirit of a party host who cannot allow the evening to end.
This is the easiest of the four boss options to turn into a great side view battle sprite.
