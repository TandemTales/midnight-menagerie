1. The Moon Lens (Level/Region 8)
A gigantic suspended lens hanging in a brass frame.
It concentrates moonlight into intense beams.
Reflections move independently inside the glass.

2. The Celestial Clock (Level/Region 8)
A huge astronomical clock mounted into the wall.
Instead of ordinary numbers, it shows:
Moon phases.
Star positions.
Planetary symbols.
Its hands move backward, skip ahead, and stop at moments that visibly change the room around it.

3. The Watcher in the Rafters (Level/Region 8)
The Watcher should be an enormous shadowy spider-like presence distributed across the attic structure.
Possible visual traits:
A pair of large eyes briefly visible between rafters.
Long shadow limbs crossing ceiling beams.
Several silhouettes appearing at different distances.
Telescopes turning whenever the Watcher shifts.
Moonlight outlining pieces of a body that never fully resolves.
The shadow stretching from ceiling to wall to floor without obeying perspective.
At times it may resemble a very tall person crouched across several beams.
At others, it is nothing more than a collection of watching shadows.
This is the strongest option if preserving the established boss name.

4. Wall Sconce (Level/Region 9)
Antique wall lamp that floats free from the wall while its flame bends toward targets.

5. Gaslight Ghost (Level/Region 9)
Tall translucent figure visible only inside the glow of old gas lamps, fading whenever the light dims.

6. Lantern Bearer (Level/Region 9)
Empty old coat and gloved hands carrying a floating lantern, with no visible person inside the clothing.
