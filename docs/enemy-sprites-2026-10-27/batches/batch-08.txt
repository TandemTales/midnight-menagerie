create a composite image of sprites of the following enemies in a turn based ccg. make sure the final image has a truly transparent background. Make sure the images are good quality and will 2D animate well (nothing in mid-action pose, extra floating parts around it, etc.). Make sure the enemies match the visual style of the included menu screen with companion characters from another part of the game:

1. The Mourning Angel (Level/Region 6)
A large weathered cemetery angel statue.
It should not become a gargoyle monster.
Instead:
Its head changes direction.
Hands shift position.
Stone wings slowly open.
Its expression changes subtly.
It may appear in a different place after the screen darkens or another animation plays.
The power comes from the statue becoming just slightly too alive.

2. The Mausoleum (Level/Region 6)
A small ornate mausoleum whose interior is impossibly deep.
Rather than giving it a mouth, the open doorway becomes a shifting void.
Inside, the player might see:
Candles.
A staircase descending forever.
A spectral procession.
Rows of empty coffins.
An entirely different cemetery.
The structure itself barely needs to move.

3. The Cemetery Gate (Level/Region 6)
The main wrought iron graveyard gate becomes possessed.
Chains rise.
Locks move.
Gate sections bend inward.
Names appear across the ironwork.
Ghostly hands occasionally grip the bars from the wrong side.
This is essentially a piece of graveyard architecture acting as the enemy.

4. The Widow of the Grounds (Level/Region 6)
A tall spectral mourner in old black formal dress and a long veil.
She carries no weapon.
Wherever she walks:
Candles light.
Flowers bend.
Statues turn.
Rain falls in small localized patches.
Ghosts gather silently behind her.
Her veil occasionally stretches through the entire cemetery like moving darkness.
She could embody grief becoming so possessive that it refuses to let anything pass on or leave.

5. Rolling Ladder (Level/Region 7)
Large wooden library ladder racing along brass rails under invisible control, stopping and changing direction abruptly.

6. Loose Pages (Level/Region 7)
Dozens of old pages swirling around an unseen center, repeatedly arranging themselves into temporary words and silhouettes.
