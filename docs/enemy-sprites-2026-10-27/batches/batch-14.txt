1. The Main Switchboard (Level/Region 9)
A large room sized control panel covering an entire wall.
Brass switches.
Knife switches.
Needles.
Meters.
Labels.
Indicator bulbs.
Everything operates itself.
When the Switchboard acts, entire sections of the room move from bright light to darkness.
This would be one of the best elites for emphasizing the Lampworks as infrastructure.

2. The Gas Manifold (Level/Region 9)
A huge network of old brass pipes, pressure gauges, valves, and glass tubes.
Valves turn on their own.
Blue flames ignite along open jets.
Pressure gauges spin wildly.
Ghostly silhouettes appear briefly inside escaping vapor.
The pipe network itself is the enemy.

3. The Glassworks Rack (Level/Region 9)
A tall storage frame packed with lamp glass, lenses, bulbs, reflectors, and chimneys.
All the glass lifts into the air and rotates around an invisible central presence.
Light refracts through everything at once.
This can produce dramatic visual effects without becoming a literal creature.

4. The Lamplighter (Level/Region 9)
The Lamplighter is a tall supernatural maintenance worker in a long black coat.
Possible visual traits:
Face obscured by darkness beneath a cap or hood.
Long lighting pole used as a tool, not a spear.
Coat tails fading into smoke or blue flame.
Small lamps ignite as he passes.
Wall sconces turn toward him.
Gas valves open automatically.
Electrical lights flicker despite him belonging to an older era.
His silhouette occasionally remains behind after his body moves.
He should feel like the personification of the mansion’s need to keep every light on.
This remains the strongest character boss option.

5. Masquerade Mask (Level/Region 10)
Floating ornate ballroom mask with trailing ribbons and a crisp side profile.

6. Waltzing Shoes (Level/Region 10)
A pair of elegant formal shoes dancing by themselves in synchronized side stepping motions.
