create a composite image of sprites of the following enemies in a turn based ccg. make sure the final image has a truly transparent background. Make sure the images are good quality and will 2D animate well (nothing in mid-action pose, extra floating parts around it, etc.). Make sure the enemies match the visual style of the included menu screen with companion characters from another part of the game:

1. The Living Index (Level/Region 7)
A huge wall of catalogue cabinets operating as one possessed system.
Drawers open in waves.
Cards fly between them.
Labels rewrite themselves.
Entire sections slide sideways.
There is no face or body.
The cataloguing system itself is the enemy.

2. The Restricted Cabinet (Level/Region 7)
A tall locked bookcase with iron mesh doors.
Something luminous moves behind the books.
Chains loosen.
Locks turn themselves.
Individual books float out briefly before being violently pulled back inside.
The cabinet never needs monster anatomy.

3. The Atlas Room (Level/Region 7)
A huge standing atlas opens into impossible geography.
Pages become windows onto forests, cities, oceans, deserts, and mansion rooms.
Objects or shadows briefly cross from those spaces into the library before being pulled back into the page.
This works well as a highly visual elite.

4. The Librarian’s Desk (Level/Region 7)
A large formal desk surrounded by a powerful invisible presence.
Pens write.
Stamps fall.
Cards sort themselves.
Books slam shut.
A chair rolls backward as though someone just stood up.
You never need to show the librarian.

5. The Black Ink Manuscript (Level/Region 7)
A huge open handwritten book sits on a lectern.
Ink continuously writes new lines by itself.
Words spill off the page as liquid black shapes.
Whole paragraphs occasionally rise as translucent calligraphy in the air.
The book remains stationary while the writing becomes the supernatural threat.

6. The Head Librarian (Level/Region 7)
A quieter and more traditional spectral librarian.
They carry:
A ledger.
Reading glasses.
A ring of archive keys.
A small lamp.
Their most distinctive visual feature is that the books obey them.
Shelves slide.
Ladders move.
Reading lamps turn.
Books return themselves to shelves.
The Head Librarian barely needs to move.
A raised finger is enough to make the entire library react.
This version makes the boss feel authoritative without becoming visually busy.
