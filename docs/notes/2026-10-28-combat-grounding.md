# Combat floor placement — 2026-10-28

Foreground furniture bypassed the room's stage clearance when it was placed by hand, and clearance ran before authored width/height overrides. This could put a companion through a chair or rocking horse. The clearance pass now runs on final furniture dimensions, uses the actual pitched/shifted camera projection, and reserves floor across the whole width so resizing does not reveal a new obstruction. Furniture is moved before instance sorting and shadow generation, keeping each object's shadows attached. Architectural pieces and pieces intentionally hidden beneath the hand retain their placement.

Camera framing also raycasts the outer standing positions against the side walls, reserving space for a full party in narrow or angled rooms. Pool and pond views now frame the near walkway from a lower eye height. Fighters stand on dry flooring instead of being projected over water; relocated furniture also stays outside the basin. The kid and companion's floor shadows now originate at the sole line, correcting the contact ellipse's five-unit vertical offset. The companion uses its fixed paw-shadow anchor rather than sprite bounds, which can include clipped atlas frames.

A separate resize bug was found with the Archivist: measuring transient card bounds could leave the boss raised by 140px even after the hand settled. The scene now fits after both its own and the hand's resize observers have measured the new viewport. Combat plate fitting also uses the hand's stable resting geometry, including the pointer-catcher clearance. Boss lift is capped at the visible standing band, and resizing schedules settled remeasurements.

Validation: `python tests/grounding/run.py` exercises actual Chrome/WebGL rooms and checks world-space floor bounds, water exclusion, projected furniture overlap and hero shadow alignment. Cases include Pool Gallery, Reflecting Pool, the Archivist boss and a four-enemy corridor, each at 1280×800, 1920×1080 and 1024×768. Screenshots and results are in `tests/grounding/`. Eight additional room backgrounds were visually compared during development. Syntax and whitespace checks cover all four changed modules.
