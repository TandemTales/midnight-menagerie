## Hard problems in the supplied architecture

### 1. High — the ordinary front exit competes with the finale's necessary exit

**Locations:** Episode 1 Marmalade crossing; ledger episodes 2–5 repeated returns; A108-05 through A108-10.

The children repeatedly return through ordinary circulation to the Foyer. Marmalade already walks outside there. After all eight pets are physically reunited in episode 8, the architecture requires repairing and holding a separate departure corridor. It does not make visible why the children cannot take their pets back the way they entered. Calling one system a human exit and another an animal departure system is not enough, particularly when the ghost cat has demonstrated that an animal can use the front door. The reunion gate remains open and the group can see its pets, which is emotionally right, but makes the missing alternative especially noticeable.

A child does not need every passage explained. They do need to understand why the difficult action is necessary. Otherwise the Keeper conflict feels prolonged by the writers.

**Repair direction:** At the first wide geography shot, establish that the previously used approach no longer connects to the Foyer while the Keeper holds the sanctuary closed, and show that the visible departure corridor is the available connection to outside. A failed look back or recognizable shut junction can do this without another adventure. Prefer reusing the existing architecture and Keeper controls over inventing a rule that mysteriously singles out pets. Preserve the safe, connected Garden and do not turn the solution into carrying pets through danger. Show the exit stays usable after everyone lets go.

### 2. Low — the pilot reads the register before being permitted to inspect it

**Location:** Episode 1, Register Alcove followed by Receiving Chamber.

Marmalade leads the group to the register, where they learn all eight arrivals and the blank departure column. The next paragraph says the Butler requires a room choice before allowing them to inspect that register. The withheld thing has already been obtained. This is a small but actual ordering contradiction, not a performance risk.

**Repair direction:** Move the Butler's condition before the inspection, or change what he withholds to the next route. Preserve his expectation that the Governess will settle them and the kids' knowing acceptance of limited access. No new scene is needed.
