## Remaining required repairs

### R2-C01 — Major source-package inconsistency: Mopsy's origin conflict is claimed to be recorded but is absent

**Locator:** `04-character-and-comedy-bible.md`, Mopsy row (“origin conflict retained”); `01-source-ledger.md`, established Companion statement and conflicts.

**Issue:** The bible correctly avoids claiming Mopsy was a living rabbit, but the cited source ledger does not record this conflict. `docs/design/companions/10-mopsy.md:6` explicitly calls Mopsy a well-loved stuffed rabbit brought to life; the core overview broadly calls Companions transformed animals. Future script/cutscene work could import the broad origin claim and contradict Mopsy's detailed chapter.

**Repair:** Add the exact conflict and working choice to the source ledger: the sixteen are a separate supernatural resident group; not every member's pre-House origin is settled by the general premise. For Mopsy preserve the specific stuffed-rabbit design and avoid a definitive prior living-pet biography. This does not change the eight missing-pet distinction.

### R2-C02 — Moderate access ambiguity: Pixel's E3 preference could be mistaken for a reachable pet

**Locator:** E3 “Priya discovers Pixel has rejected …” and “leaves the desired stone accessible”; continuity ledger says his current location is the Heart by E6 and stone later accompanies him.

**Issue:** Unlike the now carefully separated Mooncake sighting, this language does not specify whether Priya watches Pixel nearby, infers an old choice from marks, or sees a feed window. If Pixel is reachable here, the earlier Mooncake problem recurs.

**Repair:** State the evidence form in the actual E3 prose. For example, the children inspect an empty former care enclosure with a dated tray sketch and displaced platform; a care cart carries the chosen stone through a closed animal-service channel. No current Pixel is accessible. Keep the stone's movement visible or explicitly recorded so the ward appearance is a payoff, not teleportation.

### R2-C03 — Moderate prop continuity: brass fitting is retrieved twice

**Locator:** E6 Curator loans handle in Crypt, then “They retrieve the nonliving brass fitting” from the rotten gate, then carry it toward the Heart spindle.

**Issue:** There is no beat placing the already-loaned handle into the rotted gate. The same fitting seems to be discovered twice. It also only fits the inner spindle, so installing it at the outer gate would contradict the new limit unless merely placed there for safekeeping.

**Repair:** Name one object and its holder throughout. Keep the handle on the Curator's supervised tray while the children demonstrate the old gate is unserviceable. After the gate is composted, the Curator hands the already-recovered handle to Eli for the intact inner release. Do not imply the outer gate contains a second required fitting unless deliberately adding and tracking one.

### R2-C04 — Moderate pacing risk: substantive boss coverage is still unproven

**Locator:** E6/E7 budgets and E8 first three minutes.

**Issue:** E6 and E7 still contain three principal bosses, three Companions, three elites, ordinary actors, geography, comedy and emotional turns in twenty minutes. E8 initially includes a Housekeeper, Memory Animals, Hall of Names elite, Door That Says Stay and Perfect Sanctuary in three minutes before five minutes of reunions. Allocate time to a choice, failed attempt, changed action and consequence for each principal boss. If actual beats exceed the envelope, increase episode duration or count openly using the brief's permitted structure adjustment; do not certify timing on paper by making action descriptions shorter. Keep this category below target until scene breaks demonstrate the solution.
