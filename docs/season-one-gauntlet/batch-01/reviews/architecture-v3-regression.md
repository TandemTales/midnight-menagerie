**Location:** continuity ledger 05, Ordinary brass handle row, “rotted outer gate.”

### V3-G02 — Moderate missing transition: New Arrival Ward to false-sanctuary latch

**Location:** E7 ending/A107-12 versus E8 opening/A108-01.

**Ambiguity:** E7 reaches the New Arrival Ward and sees the pets through glass. E8 starts along Hall of Names into Inner Sanctuary, then opens the approach latch into the Pet Holding Ward. Those can coexist, but the current package omits why/how the group leaves the visible ward window for the latch access. Without a clear link, the emotional urgency could look like another detour away from reachable pets.

**Required correction:** Add one visible marked side approach from the New Arrival Ward's outer vestibule, through Hall of Names, to the Inner Sanctuary service side of that same ward latch. Establish that the viewing pane is not itself an opening. Keep the contact through the latch as the next action, with no unrelated quest.

### V3-G03 — Moderate map ambiguity: Kennels gate in the finale axis

**Location:** continuity ledger 05, Outside door versus ward doors row, versus timing test 07 finale geography.

**Ambiguity:** Ledger 05 says the Kennels outer gate, inner release and porch are different parts of one corridor. Timing 07 specifies Ward → Garden → Departure Corridor → porch, with Heart Chamber beside it, but does not identify the earlier Kennels gate on that axis. E7 itself uses the gate to gain courtyard access and then follows the Moon Dial into the Heart. This is not necessarily impossible in a supernatural House, but the two maps currently invite different staging.

**Required correction:** Choose and record one rule. Either the Kennels gate is only an approach gate and the finale Departure Corridor is a distinct established House route, or explicitly show its manifested alignment as a segment of the final corridor. Do not imply a new ordinary adjacency without a transition. The separate approach/departure gate choice is the simpler local edit, provided earlier ledger phrasing is updated.
