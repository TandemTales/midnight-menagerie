## One concrete cleanup before scene breaks

**Medium clarity issue — the new destination picture points at the wrong kind of door.** Episode 6's added paragraph and A106-01 describe a single picture of the **ward door beside the missing-handle silhouette**. Episode 8 distinctly says the ordinary ward approach latch opens contact, while the brass handle fits the departure-release spindle in the Heart Chamber. Repeating a ward-door/handle picture can teach a young viewer that the handle opens the reunion barrier, then seemingly change the rule in the finale.

**Repair:** Make that repeated picture show the ward as the destination landmark, followed by the separate exit corridor and its missing-handle control. Put the handle silhouette specifically beside the exit control, not beside the ward door. Keep the picture simple and use the same graphic in episode 8. This is a small consistency edit, not a new subplot, decoding scene, or reason to remove the useful destination picture.
