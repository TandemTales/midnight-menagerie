## Remaining revision directions

### R2-C1 — Medium: Character relationships remain more complementary than dramatic

The bible says how each child challenges another, but the architecture mostly has one make a mistake and the other provide the right observation. Episode 4 is the best opportunity for an earned relationship turn: Priya's posted plan exposes them to the Watcher, while Mateo's earlier theories obstructed the Library. Direction: give them a specific disagreement about whose information can be trusted, let each have one defensible reason, and make the revised unobserved route require Priya accepting an uncertain live clue and Mateo respecting a constrained test. Include a small repair afterward. This can replace explanation already allocated rather than add runtime. A lasting friendship under strain will distinguish the episode from two consecutive puzzles.

### R2-J1 — Medium: Episode 7 towel mistake lacks character logic

Wanting the warmest towel does not naturally cause Amina to place it where a route marker belongs. It reads as an inserted flaw to prevent her being perfect. Direction: make her immediate want cause a comprehensible inconvenience, such as keeping a self-warming towel on her shoulders while its embroidered return-to-laundry instruction repeatedly redirects the trolley. She can choose to surrender its comfort for the actual route, with no sermon. Alternatively omit the mistake and give her playfulness in a quieter prior episode. She need not fail merely to satisfy a balance requirement.

### R2-V1 — Medium: Episode 7 pumpkin and gate

If the gate opening is physically smaller than the pumpkin, swinging the gate outward does not enlarge the masonry opening. Direction: establish that inward-opening leaves or a ceremonial measuring frame narrow an otherwise adequate arch. Show Pipkin accidentally proving the full opening's width, then let the kids reposition the leaves/frame. This preserves a short visual payoff and avoids an impossible geometry fix disguised as common sense.

### R2-C2 — Medium: Episode 6 brass fitting continuity

The Curator lends a brass handle, then the Maze paragraph says the children retrieve a brass fitting from the old gate. The end says the handle fits only the inner release. It is unclear whether they now possess two fittings or have discovered the same one twice. Direction: state that they carry the already loaned handle through the Maze and recover only an inscription, mounting diagram, or other clearly separate information from the gate. If two pieces are necessary, name and track each before the finale. One ordinary control is simpler and more memorable.

### R2-P1 — Medium: Episode 8 reunion allocation

Five minutes of ward contact plus four minutes of home 'reunions' risks repeating eight recognition beats. The ward contact is the emotional culmination and should stay. Direction: call the home sequence returns, not reunions, and give it a changed everyday activity that shows each pet's restored autonomy: a chosen resting place, a familiar household sound, a small changed habit accommodated. Use a coherent short sequence rather than repeating approach/contact for every pair. A brief collective home aftermath may leave more air for the Keeper's difficult decision.

### R2-A1 — Medium: Finale vocabulary and geography

The explanatory ledger is clear to an adult reader, but the episode currently moves among approach latches, ward barrier, release spindle, four named Sanctuary Locks, Return Lock, test route, exterior porch, and Garden. Direction: the scene break must use an establishing image with only the immediate obstacle visually dominant. A kid can say 'That opens this door' rather than recite the system. Mark which parts are already open and where the pets are. This is a break-stage requirement, not a request for new lore.

### R2-J2 — Low: Reuse of paper labels

The elaborate nest joke makes the ending's sandwich-label sneeze less useful; it is also not clearly established in version two. Direction: use one of the MATEO nest labels as the payoff or drop the sneeze and end on the map discovery. A callback should recall an actual earlier event, not a prior draft.

## Adversarial and co-viewing verdict

This version has a recognizable show identity: intrusive hospitality, actual animal preferences, and kids who become better at sharing an investigation. The main remaining danger is an impressive itinerary whose relationships never become emotionally inconvenient. Add a consequential disagreement and repair inside an existing episode, not another conceptual speech.

The final voluntary-return action is stronger than a purely verbal answer. It demonstrates that an open door need not erase a relationship; it does not prove the outside is safe, and the script should not imply that it does. The House's incomplete progress at the end is credible.
