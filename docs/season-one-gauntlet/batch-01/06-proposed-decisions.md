# Midnight Menagerie proposed adaptation decisions

These choices allow writing to proceed without treating the show as new immutable game canon. None requires pausing the current writing work. The source ledger retains alternatives where the evidence conflicts.

| Decision | Working choice | Reason and alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Season length | Eight approximately 22-minute episodes | Honors the brief. Episodes 6 and 7 carry one objective across three regions each; timed scene breaks must test whether the stories fit. If they do not, recommend a clearly identified extension rather than deliver abbreviated scripts. |
| Mansion traversal | First substantial regional visits follow the 17-region authored order, Foyer through Heart | Satisfies the user's requested progression while acknowledging that game expeditions branch. Returning through earlier corridors is allowed and must be legible. |
| Coverage standard | All 17 principal bosses act consequentially; all 17 regions receive scenes; selected ordinary enemies and elites recur naturally | All 52 elites and all 340 rooms are not promised. A boss parts/summons list is not a list of additional major villains. |
| Season ending | Reunite all eight pets before the Keeper confrontation; open departure choice afterward; begin sanctuary practice | Delivers a complete emotional season. The brief permits fewer rescues, but this route reaches the Heart and should fulfill the central promise. The developed sanctuary concept is sourced; season placement and exact action are proposals. |
| Companion gathering | Introduce all sixteen in their regional stories; ask each to share knowledge or help, without sixteen full origin arcs | Supports the complete route and ensemble. Full rescue milestones in the game remain separate from television meeting/assistance scenes. |
| Companion homes | Mossbit at Graveyard, Pudding at Kennels, following authored narrative chapters | Current schema assigns the reverse. This is a recorded adaptation choice, not a claim that the user has resolved the discrepancy or that the game was corrected. |
| Mopsy origin | Show present rag-doll identity and preferences; do not explain her original life | Her chapter describes an animated stuffed rabbit, conflicting with a universal “formerly ordinary pets” reading. An origin episode could later address the exception without inventing a biography now. |
| Child ages | Use detailed-profile ages for internal writing; avoid unnecessary spoken age exposition | UI conflicts on five ages; no later explicit age decision found. Pronouns independently follow the designer-authoritative schema. |
| Mochi and Pixel pronouns | Long-profile he/him as provisional choice; use names when convenient | UI uses she/her; child-pronoun schema does not settle pet pronouns. Do not silently call this conflict resolved. |
| Speech convention | Companions can use brief speech; pets remain behavior-led, with Pepper's sourced mimicry | Conserves active speakers and protects animal identity. No humanlike pet speeches to explain consent. |
| Comic relationships | Pair complementary wants rather than fixed leader/sidekick ranks | Maya/Mateo, Priya/Mateo, Lena/Eli, Amina/Jordan and Samir/Lena distribute turns. These pairings are inventions grounded in traits, not historical relationships newly claimed as canon. |
| Accessibility | Broad service circulation and specific lifts/platforms at necessary transitions | These exact routes are proposed staging. Maya participates in the expedition with her forearm crutches; no cure magic or obligatory carrying. |
| Door mechanism | A copied architectural plan, a preserved ordinary handle, a specific inner release, four sourced Keeper locks, and a physically supported exit | Concrete investigation payoff. The brass fitting and repair plot are new; they must never function as a universal magical key. |
| Keeper change | A freely returning Companion becomes evidence during an opposed physical attempt to hold the exit open | A practical test begins change; no speech instantly cures the House or promises perfect outside safety. |
| Recurring props | Route labels, excessive hospitality equipment, Samir's accepted ceremonial jobs | Each recurrence changes function. Cut gratuitous recognition gags that crowd emotional beats. |
| New backstory | No definitive founder, dead family, tragedy, or curse origin | Sources leave history open. Present-day motives supply enough dramatic conflict for this season. |
| Adaptation files | New local production package, versioned reviews, later complete scripts | No publishing, source overwrites, game changes, or third-party contact. |

## Decisions that remain testable during writing

Do the triple-region episodes still feel like complete comic stories when broken into timed scenes? Can a viewer follow the final exit mechanism without memorizing four technical terms? Does each child have a changing relationship rather than only one thematic demonstration? Do quiet reunion beats survive the 22-minute allocation? These are script gates, not questions the user needs to answer before work continues.

The next batch should preserve the approved architecture revision as its starting point, then produce actual scene breaks and full screenplay text. No outline or proposed joke sample may be counted as a drafted episode.
