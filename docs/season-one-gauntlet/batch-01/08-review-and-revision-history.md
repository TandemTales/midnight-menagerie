# Midnight Menagerie architecture review and revision history

This history records actual independent agent reviews and changes to the supplied architecture. Scores assess architecture evidence only. No score certifies full screenplay dialogue, 22-minute runtime, audience response, or superiority to another show. Fresh child and family perspectives are adult editorial simulations, not real audience tests.

## Roles and independence

| Responsibility | Agent or execution |
|---|---|
| Showrunner and architecture writer | Parent agent; integrates source evidence and revises actual text |
| Head writer | Parent agent for architecture samples; complete screenplay work not yet begun |
| Comedy room, character/relationship editor, adversarial critic | Creative audit agent, independent of architecture drafting |
| Children's television, canon and continuity | Canon audit agent; separate source audit and two critiques |
| Mystery, animation staging and visual comedy | Mansion audit agent; second independent continuity/tone check |
| Fresh child-oriented final perspective | Fresh child reviewer with no prior drafts, scores or defenses |
| Fresh family co-viewing final perspective | Fresh family reviewer with no prior drafts, scores or defenses |

The first three reviewers received the same draft and wrote their own critiques without reading one another's reports. Roles are combined because there were four simultaneous agent slots, not because ten independent human specialists were involved. Two newly spawned agents receive only the current work for the fresh pass. Findings remain attributable; scores are not averaged into a passing score.

| Reviewer | Score | Principal findings |

| Canon and children's tone | 76/100 | Unsupported elite names; reachable Mooncake left behind; no party/pet timeline; home-reaching welcome mat undermined safe ending; unclear exit handle; inaccessible routes not staged |

Actual version 2 changes: replaced invented enemies with registered encounters; placed fixed glass before Mooncake's sighting and gave the children an attempted release; kept the welcome mat at the estate; removed reversal magic; showed Jordan safely handing off the current animal before declining redundant work; made the handle ordinary hardware for a specific release; changed several guardians to remain opposed or pursue independent goals; made Harvest about transport rather than an endless party; made Marmalade's voluntary exit/return an early setup and finale observation; added practical accessible circulation, character profiles and detailed continuity tracking.

An independent source check also caught the mansion auditor's stale four-starter claim. The actual current starter definition is Marmalade only. The matrix records that correction; neither the initial audit nor an implementation comment was treated as infallible.

## Disagreements and limits retained

The canon reviewer rated round-two pacing 11/15, below the requested 80% floor; the other reviewers rated it 12/15. The lower concern is not averaged away. The timing pressure test adds scene evidence, but full scripts and staged action must still prove the crowded episodes can breathe.

The creative reviewer rated round-two source fidelity 5/5 while explicitly declining to perform a second exhaustive roster audit. The other reviewers rated it 4/5 and identified missing conflicts. Those source-package corrections were made even though one score was already high.

Reviews support distinct visual comedy more strongly than finished dialogue because only samples exist. Full scripts must undergo their own two substantive critique/revision rounds and fresh pass. Architecture scores will not be reused as episode scores.

The reference-series study uses official descriptions and one creator interview to extract general craft principles. It is not a complete close reading of their seasons, real child research, or evidence that this show exceeds them.

## Fresh final perspective round on version 3

| Reviewer | Score | Findings retained |
|---|---:|---|
| Fresh child-oriented simulation | 83/100 | The finale did not exclude simply returning via the previously usable Foyer; pilot register was read before permission to inspect it; three-region pacing and ordinary friendship remained weak. |
| Fresh family co-viewing simulation | 85/100 | Same register issue; route mechanics needed one visual destination; pumpkin sequence felt like an errand after Scout's signal; Eli's permission turn, Samir's fear of replacement and family context needed actual events. |

These fresh scores are lower than some returning reviewers' scores. They remain in the record and are not replaced by the most favorable number. The fresh reviewers found useful gaps after the original reviewers had verified substantial improvements.

Version 4 repairs: the Keeper visibly folds away the incoming Foyer route for everyone, while the safe Garden remains connected; the Butler withholds the next route rather than an already-read register; the episode 6 model carries one destination picture, and rubbing/handoff happen within the compost action; the pumpkin-blocked marker is visible before the Kennels gate struggle; Hush declines a private pocket and Eli accepts a shared route that later matters; Samir reacts imperfectly to evidence of Bean's new friendship and later makes room for it; a short family meal handoff grounds Maya's search; Amina/Eli have an actual reciprocal disagreement over a borrowed tray wheel; Maya makes a consequential midpoint choice to trust the revised route; Library and Ballroom comic structures diverge from simple multiplication.

The final regression auditor separately verified all 17 planned bosses, authoritative child pronouns and the 22-minute arithmetic, and caught three small geographic inconsistencies. Version 4 also aligns the internal rotten gate, New Arrival-to-Inner-Sanctuary approach and Kennels gate's separate function across the ledgers.

One preliminary fresh-review pronoun concern was withdrawn after checking antecedents: the nearby masculine pronouns referred to pets or other characters, not Mateo. It did not become a finding or trigger an incorrect text replacement. Criticism is checked against evidence rather than accepted automatically.

## Post-repair round 4 and regression

The child-oriented reviewer scored version 4 **87/100**; the family reviewer scored it **89/100**. Both met all category floors and explicitly recommended moving into scene development rather than adding speculative outline explanations. The 90 target remains missed. No score was rounded upward or substituted for another reviewer's result.

One further child-review clarity correction moved the repeated handle silhouette from the ward-door graphic to the separate departure control; the ward contact latch explicitly has no matching silhouette. The family review's picture-prop note is repaired by making Bean and an identifiable blue-button Button Baby visible in the drawing; chew marks only corroborate contact with the paper, not happiness. The pilot's mat gag now explicitly precedes its final bowl/home image. These are narrow clarity corrections, not a fifth quality-scoring round.

Final canon regression found the prior internal-gate, side-approach and Kennels-gate inconsistencies repaired, with no new contradiction in that narrow scope. It did not certify performance pacing or supply another score. The late episode tables were independently checked as 12 rows and 22 minutes each.

Remaining concerns are carried into the next phase: Priya/Jordan's reciprocal relationship in episode 3, episode 6 visual-object load, episode 7 urgency through the pumpkin obstacle, episode 8's opening minute, sustained dialogue distinction, and staged rather than arithmetic timing. No architecture report is counted toward an episode's required complete-script reviews. The guide's integration paragraphs describe actions inside existing scenes; the next chronological breaks must not stage them twice.
