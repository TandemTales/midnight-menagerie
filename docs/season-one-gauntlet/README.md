## Candid quality result

The pilot's first complete-script reviewers scored 87 and 86. Its second-round reviewers both scored 90. Fresh readers then scored the actual third draft **82.75 and 85**, catching an apparently usable exit the children ignored, an unshown move across an obstruction and excessive trolley procedure. Those findings changed the scenes, not just a response memo.

Post-repair scores were 86.95 and 91. Final draft-5 rereads scored **88.05 from the child-oriented perspective and 92 from the family perspective**. Both meet every 80-percent category floor and identify no remaining major blocker. The overall 90 target is still unmet for one evaluator; scores are not averaged into a pass. The strongest version is delivered at the requested five-pass cap. Remaining concerns are comic consistency, supporting-ensemble depth and ending rhythm. These reviews are editorial simulations, not real child or family testing.

Final reports: [child-oriented](batch-02/reviews/MM101-script-r5-child.md) and [family co-viewing](batch-02/reviews/MM101-script-r5-family.md). All earlier drafts and lower scores remain in the [history](batch-02/01-review-and-revision-history.md).

Approximately 22 minutes remains a target, not a measured runtime. No timed read, laugh test, boards or animatic was performed. The breaks' minute allocations are planning estimates. In particular, the pilot's final clue/decision sequence expanded beyond its original one-minute break allocation; the script needs a real timing pass rather than a claim that the old arithmetic proves its length.
