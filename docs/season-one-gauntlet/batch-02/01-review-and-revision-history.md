## Fresh pass and further actual revisions

Two new agents read the complete v3 without earlier drafts, scores or the writer's defense. The child-oriented perspective scored **82.75/100**, with comedy and pacing below their category floors. The family co-viewing perspective scored **85/100**. These lower scores are retained. Both found an apparently usable cloth exit that the children ignored and an unshown move across the trolley obstruction. The child-oriented reviewer also found the decision to leave after hearing Orbit-like scratches insufficiently motivated. Both wanted less trolley procedure; the family reviewer wanted a brief personal acknowledgment between Maya and Mateo.

Version 4 repairs the actual story:

- S09 shows only a palm-sized unstitched view; the rest is sewn shut. Mopsy can open it but is still following the closing order. Maya explicitly chooses to ask the Governess for the key.
- S10 places all eight on the window side of Bedtime Hall before the trolley blocks two neighboring doorways. Mopsy stays inside the closet; Governess stays beyond the Story Nook arch. No side-switch is assumed.
- S11 concentrates the escape into three changes: divert the Giant, lose the intended parking bay to its beloved tiny bed, make one alternate turn guided by Mateo. Amina's useful attention earns the tiny bed a privacy card, which then makes the Giant even less willing to move it. Support and access stay visible without repeated equipment tests.
- Maya owns cutting Mateo off. After the scratches, they stop playback and listen to the actual House. They cannot place the sound and choose Mopsy's offered breakfast service route next time, instead of retrying the locked Nursery tonight.
- The Butler's delivery explanation gets a visible receipt; Eli's repair reflex responds to the recorded hinge squeak.

The same fresh reviewers independently reread the full v4: **86.95 child-oriented; 91 family**. All category floors now met, all major comprehension blockers closed. They still disagreed about comedy consistency and overall strength. The child-oriented reviewer flagged the privacy card's reach; the family reviewer flagged a stale Governess movement phrase and the opportunity to plant Orbit's habit earlier.

Version 5 fixes those final details in the full script. Amina's card is beside her shoe on the children's side. Governess steps from the cleared arch to the neighboring closet, without circling a trolley now out of the way. Maya demonstrates Orbit's three-scratch/pause habit in S01. Marmalade actively clears the receipt and pen from her box while the Butler catches his own unnecessary paperwork.
