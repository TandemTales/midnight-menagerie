## Reusable encounter cores

Grand Coatcheck's attention, Butler's hospitality bargain, Button Baby's escalating offers, Patchwork Giant's favorite bed and Governess's blanket decision can support local dialogue. Exact combinations and TV mechanisms are new staging proposals, not claims that those mechanics exist in the game.

Register entries, a recently missing child's recognition, Mopsy's first move to the gallery and a first-time ghost discovery need separate variants for already-known evidence or a previously rescued Companion. Do not replay the children's surprise or reset a resident's agency in a later procedural visit. The small cloth return is a human-sized proposed connection; it is not Marmalade's pass-through-cloth power.
