# MM103 — A Very Small Parade

Scene break v1. All plot actions, dialogue fragments, connections and timing are television proposals. This is a complete chronological scene break, not a screenplay or measured runtime. Governing sources: batch-01 architecture v4; source ledger 01; bible 04; continuity ledger 05; mansion structure sections 5–6; verified roster below.

**Estimated duration: 22:00.** Third evening. First substantial traversal: Impossible Greenhouse, then Mansion Graveyard. Priya and Jordan drive the story. All eight children travel together; other children stay visible and perform practical work without becoming a reaction chorus. Brambleboo and Mossbit use authored home regions, preserving the explicit Mossbit/Pudding code conflict. No current pet is reachable in these regions.

**Episode goal:** follow Pixel's care route, find evidence that animals once left the House, and secure the record that identifies the next route. **Reciprocal relationship:** Jordan helps Priya relinquish an attractive but unhelpful object without taking control of her decision; Priya later helps him finish a modest useful task without turning it into an achievement display. Their repair language is shared work, not an argument or lecture. His improvisation and her plan are both needed.

### MM103-S01 — Follow the warm route

**Purpose/action:** A brief opening movement shows the eight passing the Foyer's marked service entrance with the copied kitchen map, then the same vegetable-cart symbol at the Orangery doorway. **Comic escalation:** Priya's map is long; Jordan unrolls it to help and discovers he is holding it upside down while the greenhouse's glass reflection looks correct. They quietly rotate the physical map together rather than argue with the reflection.

**Emotion:** Partnership begins in easy cooperation. **Clue before → after:** a stone-like object on a Greenhouse-bound cart → matching service symbols confirm they are following the mapped care route, not locating Pixel yet. **Voices/party:** Priya and Jordan. **Comic escalation:** The Gardener trims a hedge corner; the Ivy straightens Priya's marker to match; Jordan straightens the marker back; the Gardener trims the opposite corner. **Emotion:** Jordan chooses to trust Priya's preparation; she enjoys having a clear answer. **Clue:** two possible routes → the clean route is a guardian's preference, not evidence of safety. **Voices/party:** Priya, Jordan, Head Gardener.
