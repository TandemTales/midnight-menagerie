# Episode 101 Your Room Is Ready scene break

Version 1. Animation, approximately 22 minutes; estimates, not measured performance. All scene actions and new connections are adaptation proposals. Canon comes from batch 01 ledgers and primary game/design sources. Eight kids enter together; two or three dominate each conversation. Maya uses her forearm crutches. No pet is reunited here. Focus: Maya and Mateo learning to investigate together rather than treating uncertainty as a contest.

Action/purpose: eight pet notices fight for space. Jordan's loose Scout photo folds over Priya's Pixel picture; she straightens both; Lena pins the corner with a yellow button she immediately takes back and replaces with a tack. Maya has laid out a route to search. Mateo brings a recording made before Pepper disappeared: Pepper says “Open the little door,” with Mateo audible in the background asking where he learned it. They choose a shared visit, carrying evidence and supplies rather than treating fear as a dare.

Comedy: different practical fixes compete on one board, then Jordan holds the whole board still instead of adding another fastener. The gecko picture gets the largest space only because it happens to brace the loose corner, frustrating Priya's elegant layout.

Emotion/clue: Maya has Orbit's empty blue collar, not an explanation. Quick photos/objects plant distinguishing pet traits without eight biographies. Amina taps Mochi's rhythm while checking the photo; Eli pockets a tooth-marked screw; Scout photo has Jordan's bark/huff notation; Bean and Mooncake identifiers appear visually. The old recording does NOT contain Orbit's scratches. A new recording made inside later will supply that clue, avoiding unexplained pre-disappearance mixed audio.

Party/cutscene state: eight present; no Companion yet; eight pets missing. Proposed neighborhood geography, not mansion room canon.
