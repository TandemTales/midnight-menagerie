## Episode contract

Fourth evening. All eight kids enter and leave together. Foreground: Mateo (they/them) and Priya (she/her); Maya makes a consequential trust choice. Objective: obtain the departure plan identified in the Graveyard, then test the plan's observation-route connection for a present pet lead. The first plan fails because the friends confuse useful categories with actual individuals; the second fails because they publish it to an observer. Their repaired method combines Mateo's bounded test, Priya's revised timing, and Maya's willingness to act on their work without independently proving every step.

No pet is physically accessible in this episode. The recording belongs to the kids and predates this visit. The speaking-tube answer is an interaction responsive to a new input; its source is likely Pepper but neither identity nor location is proven by mimicry alone. No omniscient cutaway confirms a hidden pet's exact room.

**Room/time allocation:** Library S01–S06 = 10:00; Attic/Observatory S07–S12 = 10:00; return/tag S13–S14 = 2:00. Working lifts, shutters, copied plans and exact joins are television staging proposals. Existing named rooms and creature identities remain sourced.
