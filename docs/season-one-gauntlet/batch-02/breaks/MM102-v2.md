# MM102 — Breakfast Is Following Us

Scene break v2. All plot actions, dialogue fragments, mechanisms, connections and timings are television proposals. This is a complete chronological episode break, not a screenplay or a claim of measured runtime. Sources: batch-01 architecture v4; source ledger 01; bible 04; continuity ledger 05; mansion structure sections 3–4; verified roster below. Revised after independent canon review B1-C03: the tray departure has its own scene; the ribbon request and demonstration occur on the return route. Four shorter repeated comic sequences fund the additional minute in S13–S14.

**Estimated duration: 22:00.** Second evening. First substantial traversal: Sleeping Quarters, then Kitchens and Cellars. Eli and Amina drive the story; Jordan supplies physical support. All eight children travel together. Only the listed active voices carry each scene; the other children perform visible supporting tasks. Pets are not physically found or rescued here.

**Episode goal:** follow the recognizable animal sounds into the service delivery system, obtain a route beyond the residential wing, and return with evidence. **Relationship:** Eli mistakes speed for universal help and borrows a critical part without asking. Amina's attachment to one promising tray becomes an unwillingness to test another route. They repair actual equipment and try a bounded experiment together.

## Chronological scenes

### MM102-S01 — A route worth trying

**Purpose/action:** Reestablish the second visit without replaying the pilot. In the Umbrella Gallery, all eight check their return mark and water. Amina demonstrates Mochi's two quick thumps and one slower thump against his photograph. Eli holds his previously chewed screw beside Sprocket's photo, then packs it into a divided tin. They take the already established Foyer service lift to the residential landing and enter the Guest Hall through the open Nursery-side corridor. One continuous doorway match makes the region change readable.

**Comic escalation:** Eli has labeled his little tin DO NOT LOSE. He opens three neatly labeled compartments before discovering the screw in the one marked OTHER. Amina quietly points to it; he closes the lid as if this was the intended demonstration.

**Emotion:** Familiar objects make the search personal; each respects the other's recognition skill. **Clue before → after:** checked-in names, no current locations → two distinctive behaviors reestablished for fair recognition. **Voices/party:** Eli and Amina; Maya chooses and operates the lift, Priya notes the route, Jordan holds its door, Mateo checks sound recorder, Lena photographs the landing, Samir carries the water. No encounter character or new power.

### MM102-S02 — The door gets away

**Purpose/action:** Eli starts undoing the Blue Bedroom latch in the Guest Hall. As he proudly lifts the latch, the whole door slides sideways to the next bedroom opening, leaving him holding perfectly functional hardware in front of plain paneling. He returns it before the door settles. Under a low cupboard, Boggle comments that the door does that. Amina hears the recognizable thumps beyond the wall. A Slipper Skitter pair scurries through the newly open route; an empty bed follows them on visible casters.

**Comic escalation:** Eli successfully opens the latch twice, always one doorway behind the actual door. Boggle gives increasingly useful directions from his hiding place, thereby revealing it. The slippers retreat whenever watched directly, preserving their sourced evasive behavior.

**Emotion:** Eli is fascinated rather than shamed; Amina prioritizes the sound over the mechanism. **Clue:** no bedroom lead → sound consistent with Mochi, location still uncertain; slippers visibly precede a moving bed. **Voices/party:** Eli, Amina, Boggle. All other kids remain in the hall in the wide shot; Lena tracks the real door, Maya keeps the clear floor route. **Transition:** open door leads through the named bedroom corridor into Canopy Bedroom, the slippers still visible ahead.

### MM102-S03 — Three pillows, three opinions

**Purpose/action:** The Blanket Hydra rises from the Canopy Bedroom's connected beds, blocking the far doorway. Its three recognizable heads want different pillow arrangements: Snoring settles, Biting chews an inert tassel, Crying fusses at the missing cushion. Jordan delivers the requested pillow, which one head immediately passes to another. Amina sees the heads pulling in different directions; she directs him to place three plain cushions on the three empty bed ends. The Hydra spreads itself around them and clears the doorway, temporarily.

**Comic escalation:** One pillow makes a single circuit faster than Jordan can follow; Amina then goes directly to three clearly placed cushions. There is no intermediate two-pillow attempt. The humor is incompatible comfort preferences, not mocking distress. No sentient Pillow Puff is bitten or used as bedding.

**Emotion:** Jordan's willing help needs Amina's observation. **Clue:** pet sound remains unresolved → no new pet proof, but the household directs bodies to assigned resting places. **Voices/party:** Amina, Jordan, Boggle. Maya and Priya hold the human-width route; Eli watches casters, Mateo and Samir move inert baggage, Lena records the doorway. **Transition:** the released far door opens directly onto Bedframe Court; all eight move through before the Hydra changes its mind.

### MM102-S04 — An unconvincing nap

**Purpose/action:** The Bedframe Beast reveals itself as the enormous whole bed, not a separate creature sitting on it. Boggle explains that his route lies beneath it, currently blocked by its trailing canopy. Eli proposes pretending to sleep so it will finish settling them and stop moving. The kids decline to climb onto the monster; they perform eight upright naps along the safe wall, against plain folded cushions. The Beast begins pulling the room's empty beds into itself, making the apparently simple plan progressively worse. The Hydra stretches through the doorway trying to keep up with the beds it was tucking in.

**Comic escalation:** Eli announces sleep too loudly, then tries one carefully timed snore. As the Beast starts gathering the beds, Amina shifts her uncomfortable cushion and abandons the nap. Boggle points out the moving canopy instead of coaching another snore. The failed test moves immediately into observation; no additional giant-cushion offer prolongs it. No pillow covers a child's face.

**Emotion:** Amina stops participating in a plan that ignores what is actually happening. Eli sees his test has failed without losing his curiosity. **Clue:** bed-following clue → repeated confirmation: the Slipper Skitter reaches a small parking mat and an empty bed rolls over that position. **Voices/party:** Eli, Amina, Boggle. All eight remain on the clear wall-side walkway; Maya sees the canopy's loose edge and an existing curtain hook within her reach. Boggle's local caster pawl is beneath the bed, a separate task from securing that edge.

### MM102-S08 — The Confectioner's bargain

**Purpose/action:** In his actual boss room, the Confectioner insists every prepared tray must leave with its delivery slip; his master slip has vanished. **Comic escalation:** The chef's six tasks require six precise gestures; every time Eli tries to point to a problem, an empty plate lands on the pointing finger. The last plate is miniature, because the chef has run out of space on him.

**Emotion:** A bargain has real limits; Taffy gains freedom of movement without becoming a prize. **Clue:** gate constraint and missing slip → specific task: stop the recirculation, search the lower route, return slip and trolley for a map. **Voices/party:** Eli, Amina, Confectioner; Taffy volunteers with a clear gesture. ### MM102-S09 — One borrowed wheel

**Purpose/action:** Establish the mechanics before the disagreement: serving trays are flat and wheel-less; Amina's tray rests on a small two-wheel carrier with a stable base. The Dish Room's existing return belt has an exposed hand-crank spindle but no crank wheel. The carrier tips onto its safe fixed base; Amina catches the covered tray before any food spills. He thinks faster circulation will help every pet. She refuses to lend the second wheel for his proposed guide roller, since she needs the carrier to roll. He dismisses waiting as another delay.

**Comic escalation:** Eli tests one empty plate successfully, then sends Jordan's tidy stack past the napkin station. In one visible circuit, the same plates return with fresh napkins and more servings. A Blob and its little partner salute the same identifiable saucer on opposite sides of that circuit. Cut directly to the blocking Sugar Golem; no second lap is needed. The conveyor circulates existing objects; the food service, not the belt, makes more food.

**Emotion:** A genuine breach of trust replaces abstract disagreement. Amina is hurt that he improved her equipment into uselessness. **Clue:** routing behavior not yet understood → trays return because the missing slip leaves the exit gate shut, not because every pet endlessly needs breakfast. **Voices/party:** Eli, Amina, Jordan. Other five keep the original animal tray and bags out of the moving belt and stand behind its marked safe edge.

### MM102-S10 — Stop improving it

**Purpose/action:** The Sugar Golem steps from Sugar Store into the Dish Room doorway, enforcing matching stacks on both sides. It keeps returning asymmetrical finished plates to the belt, worsening Eli's loop. Eli cannot simply yank out the borrowed crank wheel while it carries tension. Amina helps immediately: she closes the visible feed flap while he disengages the belt's catch; Jordan stabilizes the empty trolley. Lena silently places two equally tall stacks where the Golem wants them, so it finally holds still instead of redistributing plates. Eli removes the wheel only after motion stops and puts it back on Amina's carrier, checking that both wheels turn.

**Comic escalation:** The Golem moves one plate across, sees the new imbalance and moves that same plate back once. Lena's equal empty stacks end the loop immediately. The kids give its immense concentration an absurdly small target while Eli and Amina stop the belt; do not add further correction cycles. It is not smashed, melted, eaten, or morally converted.

**Emotion:** Amina does not make her help contingent on an apology. Eli repairs the specific damage and says plainly that he should have asked. She agrees to test a short manual route together, not surrender the tray to another grand machine. **Clue:** automatic recurrence → routing can be observed with one carrier at a time. **Voices/party:** Eli, Amina, Jordan. All eight contribute in frame or in the adjoining open kitchen; Taffy waits at the Cold Larder door and points to the useful route.

**Emotion:** Eli and Amina can disagree without undoing their partnership; Taffy offers one useful task and then pursues her own evening. **Clue:** map and loose screw retained → evidence filed as leads, plus a demonstrated finite kitchen-to-Orangery handoff. **Voices/party:** Eli, Amina, Taffy; Boggle chooses through gesture. ### MM102-S15 — Finally, breakfast

**Purpose/action:** The idle breakfast trolley has followed the eight back to Umbrella Gallery along that same service route. Jordan opens its huge lid: one tiny, exquisitely plated ordinary sandwich. He looks at the others, cuts it into shared bites with an ordinary utensil, then sits beside Eli and Amina for his own bite. Nobody eats a living kitchen creature. The meal can be small and still be wanted. All eight leave together through Front Vestibule, map and evidence packed; Boggle and Taffy choose the interior refuge.

**Comic escalation:** Grand trolley, immense lid, miniature sandwich; Jordan's long-awaited sitting-down is the payoff. No extra moral line. **Emotion:** An earned pause and mutual care. **Clue:** next goal is settled → Greenhouse route remains actionable for episode 3. **Voices/party:** Jordan, Eli, Amina at most; the others share the visual pause, then all eight cross the front threshold. **End image:** trolley presents a ludicrously large napkin to Jordan's now-empty place.
