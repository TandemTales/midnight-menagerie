## Episode contract

Fifth evening. All eight kids travel together; Samir and Lena carry the foreground, with one bounded technical assist from Eli. Objective: follow E4's lantern-marked service route to evidence about the pets, without surrendering the right to end a visit. Samir wants Bean back and wants the House's friendly residents to like him. Lena wants a useful clue and some quiet, but has to state those wants instead of quietly hoping someone notices. Samir's reflex to accept assignments first hides, then exposes, his fear that Bean may be happier with other friends.

Lamplighter's encounter is a concrete light-distribution problem with a binding constraint. Master of Revels' encounter is a social trap with an ordinary physical exit shown early. Neither boss becomes an ally or concedes the season's whole argument. The Red Page is a sourced narrative resident, not an invented combat boss. Bean's picture is evidence of a depicted relationship and handled paper, not proof of his present location or emotional state. The concluding wheek remains an audio lead.

**Timing:** Lampworks S01–S06 = 9:00; Ballroom S07–S12 = 11:00; refuge/exit S13–S14 = 2:00. Route fixtures, lamp-loan procedure, exact props, dialogue samples and service joins below are adaptation staging proposals. All eight pets remain beyond physical access.

**Fixed Lampworks layout:** the Service Catwalk overlooks one connected work court. Its left lane is Lantern Gallery, containing the walking Great Lantern; its right bay is Reflector Gallery, containing the small lamp's marked return shelf. A fixed masonry baffle separates these lanes. The Great Lantern is too broad to enter the narrow reflector bay, shown by its feet reaching the fixed lane curb and its shoulders meeting the baffle's width. The large **fixed** central enclosure holding Wisp occupies the Great Lantern Room's far wall; its side vent opens into the reflector bay and onward public Glow Chamber. The service sign is visible through the protected vent channel. These objects never migrate when a scene changes viewing room. Direct beams in the left lane are gathered by the elite; light routed behind the baffle is physically occluded from it. This is a staging proposal, not a new magical immunity rule.
