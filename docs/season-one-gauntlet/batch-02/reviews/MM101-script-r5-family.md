## Final changes checked in context

**S01 → S07 → S13: Orbit's clue is now fairly planted.** Mateo's question about whether Orbit would answer a call belongs naturally to a search discussion. Maya's three taps and waiting gesture demonstrate a personal habit before she explains it. The later three scratches are therefore something an attentive viewer can recognize. Their presence under a bell makes the children's failure to isolate the sound plausible. Playback then rewards both attention and Mateo's recording habit. Maya still does not claim to know Orbit's room or prove that he made the sound. This is a properly bounded clue, not a miraculous answer.

The S13 reminder repeats the habit established in S01, but that repetition serves the younger target audience across a substantial intervening adventure. It is not a new exposition problem. Keep its delivery personal and brief.

**S11: Amina's card stays on the correct side.** The card beside her shoe means she does not need to cross the trolley obstruction. Her help with the pincushion remains a small kindness; giving the Giant a DO NOT DISTURB card makes his refusal to move the bed a character joke with consequences. The action is comprehensible without imagining an unseen crossing.

**S11: the Governess's approach is repaired.** She now leaves the cleared Story Nook arch and approaches the neighboring closet. This agrees with the established room arrangement and the trolley's new position by the windows. The previous redundant circling route is gone. The children, Mopsy, and Governess remain on legible sides of the obstacles throughout the sequence.

**S14: the paperwork gag has a satisfying finish.** Marmalade clears her resting space; the Butler catches both displaced objects and pockets them. It is stronger than ending on a static wait for a signature. The joke comes from a cat's familiar priorities and his professional habits. Her ability to move an object does not newly contradict her ghostliness: the script already shows her batting the ledger corner in S05 while choosing to pass through other materials. The new business does not establish a ghost origin or turn her into Orbit.

## Full-script regression judgment

The opening still supplies a shared problem before introducing the mansion. Eight owner/pet pairs are a memory demand, but the episode does not require mastery of every pairing to follow Maya and Mateo. The board comedy gives the group an immediate social rhythm, and the collar, recording, and overlapping search maps provide converging reasons to investigate.

The middle retains the stronger version of the causal chain. The children follow their bags, learn a rule, recover them, find the register, seek staff, and enter the nursery in pursuit of answers. In S09 they can see the return landing but cannot pass through the sewn hanging. Mopsy's obedience makes Maya's attempt to obtain the key understandable. Asking what Mopsy wants changes that problem, rather than supplying an exit the children had inexplicably ignored.

Mopsy's decision remains earned. She helps before the children recruit her, values her work, and wants a specific modest experience. She decides to unpick the seam, decides to cross, and chooses the umbrellas before accepting an invitation outside. Her future availability remains conditional. The Governess's hesitation over the repaired blanket preserves a believable attachment without granting her an instant moral transformation.

Maya and Mateo's relationship also holds. Maya's dismissal visibly hurts; she names what she did and apologizes; she then trusts Mateo to guide the unseen corner. Mateo later stops speculating to let her listen. The shared question mark at the end is a payoff to those choices rather than a substitute for repair.

The departure is credible: Maya wants to pursue the sound, checks for a present signal, recognizes the limit of the nursery route, and accepts Mopsy's more useful breakfast route for tomorrow. The kitchen ending neither declares defeat nor supplies false reassurance. Eating, receiving ordinary care, sharing evidence, and packing Orbit's bowl are enough. The emotion is poignant rather than manipulative.

Pronouns are consistent throughout: Maya she, Mateo they, Amina she, Eli he, Priya she, Jordan he, Lena she, Samir he. Maya's forearm crutches remain ordinary support with practical staging. The missing animals are not replaced by the Companions. No automatic cure or definitive ghost origin appears.
