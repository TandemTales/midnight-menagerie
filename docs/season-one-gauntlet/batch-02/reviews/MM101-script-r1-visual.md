## Findings requiring revision

### SV1-01 — High, S07/S09/S12: establish which landing the Nursery occupies

Before the children enter the lift, the Nursery receiver and door are described as “across the landing,” and a cloth hand answers there. Only afterward does the lift rise one short level. S09 and S12 correctly put the return seam on the destination landing. Boarding cannot unambiguously preserve the single route if the Nursery appeared on the departure level.

**Fix:** Make the lower Bell Pull Gallery a clearly established atrium sightline to an upper Nursery landing, with the receiver visible above its rail, and show the lift rising to that rail; alternatively take the lift before the bell demonstration. Use one overview preserving the original Nursery entrance, Story Nook/Bedtime Hall arch, Mending Closet and adjacent return opening. Do not solve this with a newly magical lift.

### SV1-02 — High, S08/S10/S11: trolley load and brake states contradict its movement

The Giant returns with an “empty bed trolley piled high with folded frames and mattresses.” In S11 it takes one tiny bed from the top, but the children then move an “empty trolley.” The unremoved load changes force, clearances and the sightline through which Mateo addresses Mopsy. Separately, Maya tests a marked brake pedal, then “lowers the brake pedal” immediately before the trolley rolls, and later “sets its brake again.” Without another operating convention this depicts rolling with the brake engaged.

**Fix:** Choose an explicit load state. Show the Giant unloading the frames onto the prepared room positions before it parks the empty trolley across the arch/closet, retaining its tiny bed separately. Or keep a lightly loaded trolley and consistently call/show it loaded. Use explicit brake actions: press to test/stop, release to move, press to park. Keep casters and clear floor in the establishing insert; no child passes beneath a moving load.

### SV1-03 — Medium, S11: let the Giant's own small-bed choice complicate the escape once

The coatcheck sequence has a slipping coat and a second-sleeve adaptation. By comparison, the principal escape proceeds from one question through successful Giant diversion, successful trolley movement, successful unpicking and orderly crossing. The Giant's pincushion joke is charming but happens beside the solution rather than affecting it. The Governess's later choice matters to Mopsy, after all eight children have already crossed, so it does not provide the earlier physical adaptation.

**Fix:** Use the Giant's sincerely chosen tiny bed to occupy the wall bay the kids intended for the trolley. Show Maya/Jordan abandon that destination and choose the other already-established clear alcove, with Mateo's willing help keeping the Giant's bedding comfortable. One readable setback and altered path is enough; do not add a dangerous chase or another latch. The Giant should retain its caring intention and favorite bed.

### SV1-04 — Medium, S11/S12: show the bags leaving the bench before the children cross

Samir moves their bags onto the bench away from the wheels; Amina gathers a loose strap. The next scene begins Priya/Amina crossing with the bags, without retrieving or distributing them. Eight children, bags, crutches, a turning trolley, Giant, Governess and Mopsy are enough moving elements that this handoff should be visible rather than inferred. Maya's working crutch also rests beside the fixed bench and must remain reachable after the trolley shifts.

**Fix:** Once the trolley is braked in its bay, show a short bag chain from the bench to each owner. Priya/Amina can take the remaining loose items first; clarify which bags, rather than implying they carry all eight. Establish Maya's bench-side support and crutch remain on the open side of the parked trolley. This can fit into the existing exit wide shot rather than become new business.

### SV1-05 — Low, S04: Mateo's sixteen-arms question postpones their useful curiosity

The random historical-arm question reads as a digression, and Maya's correction feels justified; Mateo finds the actual solution through a later accidental cat observation. Their character is more specific when curiosity includes an imperfect practical hypothesis.

**Fix:** Replace or attach the question to a small test of what the Coatcheck considers a customer/coat. Let the test fail before Marmalade crosses the sleeve, then let Mateo revise it. Preserve Maya nearly missing the observation while pursuing numbered straps. Do not replace the strong empty-coat comedy with a verbal solution.

## Verified repairs and strengths to preserve

- **No original-exit bypass:** S08 visibly locks the solid Nursery door with the moon key; S10 repeats its locked state and key custody; S11 keeps it locked while the trolley moves. The return seam remains necessary. The outer front door's ordinary operation in S03/S14 is compatible with this local trap.
- **Blanket payoff is fair:** S09 shows Mopsy's bright crossed stitches on the Governess's long blanket, S10 carries it into action, and S12 threatens that exact repair. The Governess chooses to spare the work rather than being immobilized by thread. Mopsy chooses to cross. The separate small borrowed blanket is now explicitly offered; it is not the snagged long blanket appearing elsewhere.
- **Mopsy is located:** she enters the closet and takes the loose seam before the trolley blocks access. She exits through the real doorway, not Marmalade's ghost shortcut.
- **Recording provenance is clear:** old home video contains Pepper's cabinet mimic and little-door phrase; the newly started Bell Gallery recording captures the scratches. A recurring sound is treated as a clue, not proof of current location or magical identity.
- **Accessibility is embodied in action:** Maya chooses a broad route, checks level thresholds, gets seated rest, uses and regains her crutches, and makes observations/decisions as well as operating a brake. Preserve reachable support while repairing the blocking.
- **Fear and warmth fit the intended tone:** the locked door has understandable stakes without bodily threats. Needlework remains on objects. The repaired bag, chosen umbrella refuge, free cat return and final bowl provide care without dissolving the mystery. The final parent does not explain Maya's feelings for her.
- **Companion consent remains specific:** Marmalade is not commanded as a lure; Mopsy can refuse future help and chooses her refuge. The final unprompted return is observable, not proof that every animal should stay.
