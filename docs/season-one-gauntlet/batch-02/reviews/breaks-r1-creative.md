## MM101 — Your Room Is Ready

The pilot has a personal reason to enter, a visible investigation gain, a concrete resident freedom, and an ordinary home aftermath. Maya/Mateo friction produces a changed shared method instead of one child's competence defeating another's foolishness. The original Pepper recording and the new interior recording are now properly separated. Mopsy's permission to repair the bag contrasts well with the Governess's unwanted comfort.

### 101-C1 — Medium: S10–S11, Mopsy's position when the trolley blocks the closet

S10 calls her away to finish the seam; S11 blocks the Mending Closet doorway, then says she unpicks that seam. The intended geography can work, but the break should explicitly place Mopsy **inside the closet before** the trolley blocks it. Otherwise a script expansion might put her outside, require an unseen shortcut, or let her operate a seam she cannot reach. Add one staging clause; keep her agency and the later clear human lane.

### 101-J1 — Medium: S03–S11, too much escalation by size

Oversized coats, branching rack, giant bed stack, enormous buttons, tiny pocket blanket/bed in sock, and giant pillow all draw on related over-accommodation. Each individual gag is readable, but across roughly twelve minutes the audience may predict the next object will simply get bigger or fit the wrong thing. Direction: keep the memorable coat-through-cat, Button Baby's sideways solution, and Giant's tiny favorite bed; change S10's blanket fit sequence to **incompatible instructions** rather than more scale. For example, the Governess's attempt to keep a route quiet keeps putting a squeaky comfort object precisely where Maya needs to pass, and their helpful correction changes who can hear the answer they need. Use one specific local contradiction; avoid adding a new subplot.

### 101-C2 — Low: S15, coat button prop

The final enormous borrowed coat button has not been shown becoming Maya's possession. It is described as a **coat** button, so this is not necessarily the rejected Button Baby button and should not be mislabeled as such in revision history. Direction: seed it coming loose harmlessly during coat retrieval, or use an already established harmless loose object. This is prop continuity, not theft or an agency violation.

### 101-P1 — Low: S05 and S13, evidence caveats as possible repeated dialogue

The break correctly limits what the clues prove. Do not turn every accurate caveat into a full spoken disclaimer in the script. One concise distinction at the register and one hopeful question after the scratches will preserve mystery without making the children explain the writers' audit. Their uncertainty should be visible in what they decide to do next.

**Verdict:** No high-severity continuity, tone, or causal defect found in this pilot break. Apply the position and prop clauses, vary one comedy sequence, then proceed to actual screenplay work while retaining independent second-round/fresh break checks. The entire season must not be called complete because the pilot break is workable.

## MM104 — The Wrong Answer Club

The episode now has an earned reciprocal relationship arc. Mateo's questions create the first problem, Priya's public plan creates the second, and Maya makes an independent consequential trust choice. Three fixed nests competing for one assignment are a stronger escalation than an unlimited pile. The Watcher's observations and the side walkway are planted before use; the live response is evidence rather than omniscient proof. The handle drawing and Lampworks platform supply useful later continuity.

### 104-C1 — Medium: S04–S06, access to the original plan during copying

S04 returns the original correspondence/drawing to its Letter Archive drawer. S05 permits copying. S06 immediately has Priya copy the plan at the Map Room tracing surface, but no transfer or retrieval occurs. Direction: show the approved card releasing the numbered drawer's drawing to a supervised copying trolley, or have the group return to the drawer and carry the original with explicit permission. Then show its return. Do not quietly convert the child's earlier glimpse into a complete accurate memory of the key control drawing.

### 104-C2 — Medium: S06, S12 and S13, Crinkle/Wink future-help signal is not yet connected

Crinkle agrees to keep a copy; later he meets them at an 'agreed refuge bell.' That meeting and the specific bell path were not agreed in the shown scenes. Wink is asked to respond to a refuge signal but offers to watch the lantern plate; this still needs a defined incoming signal and report endpoint. Direction: use a specific paired local bell or physical card handoff, show each resident choose the duty, and name where the answer goes. MM105 already demonstrates a distinct Foyer–Musicians–Glow Chamber chain; coordinate rather than imply all bells reach everyone. Keeping a duplicate is valuable, but it is not by itself a communications mechanism.

### 104-J1 — Low: S03 and S08, repeat of the same blame

The second dispute is justified because the first ends only in a ceasefire. Nevertheless, avoid scripting the same 'your guesses / your rules' exchange twice. S03 can be an immediate practical snap over the jam. In S08 the new vulnerability is Priya discovering that her carefully publicized plan is exposing them. Let Mateo recognize that fact before explaining it, so the repair develops rather than replays.

### 104-P1 — Medium: S09–S11, visual information budget

The observation system includes Eye, posted route card, silver connection, model screen, full-size screen, speaking tube, unseen-side walkway, catch, crank and detent. All are plausible locally, but too many could be introduced as separate explanatory beats during six minutes of action. Direction: group the causal display into three readable images: Eye reads card; Orrery moves screen; side walkway has one latch with a holding notch. Plant the holding notch when the catch first appears, not while someone must cross. The break can retain technical details as production notes while the screenplay uses two or three visible verbs.

### 104-C3 — Low: S13, MATEO label pickup

S13 says the label was picked up during the Index escape, but S03 does not actually stage that pickup. Seed it sticking to Priya's sleeve during the nest shuffle, then leave it there until payoff. This avoids a callback to a nonexistent action.

**Verdict:** Strongest of these three breaks; numerical target reached for this reviewer at this stage. Copy custody and future-help handoff still need actual revisions. Sustained funny dialogue and timing remain to be demonstrated in full scenes.

## MM105 — Everybody Has a Job

Samir's social generosity has a cost that is specific to him. The Bean friendship picture adds emotional complication beyond generic boundary-setting, and Lena states her preference instead of silently resenting him. The public Ballroom exit is correctly established before the social escape. Wisp's local freedom and Crumbula's finite relay promise are distinct outcomes. Keeping the borrowed lamp separate from Wisp is essential and well intentioned, but the Lampworks staging is currently overcomplicated.

### 105-C1 — Medium: S03–S04, lamp returns before it is returned

S03 leaves Samir raising and carrying the borrowed lamp. S04's evidence paragraph says the lamp stays on its marked shelf, and its handoff then has Samir return it with his own hands. This is an actual prop-state contradiction. Direction: return the borrowed lamp at the start of S04, establish its fixed beam, then perform the moth-shadow test. The Lamplighter can inspect compliance in S05; there need not be a second return action.

### 105-V1 — Medium: S01–S05, three similar luminous objects and changing room positions

The small loaned lamp, Wisp's fixed enclosure, and walking Great Lantern are separate, but their geometric relationship moves across four named rooms. The cause of the alternate beam can become hard to draw: why does the light-gathering elite fail to turn toward the new beam when it followed every earlier angle? Direction: establish one wide spatial layout spanning the connected galleries, with a fixed baffle or shutter providing a **visible** blind angle the walking elite cannot occupy. Use the moth shadow to reveal that angle. Keep Wisp's enclosure and exit vent in the same place throughout; the boss encounter can be viewed across the Great Lantern Room threshold without relocating the apparatus. No new light-magic rule is needed.

### 105-P1 — Medium: S08 and S10 repeat the seating/aisle conflict

S08 has interrupting music, cleared aisle refilled by chairs, and a delayed pet clue. S10 repeats all three host constraints with the same chairs and masks. The current text labels it one escalating set, but the new behavior is mostly repetition. Direction: let S08 establish the chair conflict once and move quickly to Lena's wallpaper. After the Bean scene, S10 should turn Samir's **chosen pet announcement** into the new trap: he has secured an apparently real chance to ask a useful question, but only if he completes a farewell the Master will not end. The audience then understands why he tries once more without watching the same furniture business again.

### 105-C2 — Medium: S11's promised 'one ending' and three repeats

Lena agrees to watch one ending in S10. S11 contains three short versions before Samir leaves. This can be within one attempted cadence, but the phrase 'three versions of the same final bar' could be scripted as three separate endings and make him break the new boundary immediately. Direction: explicitly stage one continuous approach to the final bar, interrupted and held three times before it can resolve. Lena's agreement is honored; she does not have to endure three fresh attempts after promising one.

### 105-J1 — Low: S07/S11, Count Crumbula risks two versions of the same bow joke

The tiny food/toast is appealing. Keep the introductory ceremony short so the final modest bow remains the payoff. His choice to leave while enjoying the event is more valuable than several extra examples of his pretension. Do not make him recite the episode's lesson.

### 105-C3 — Low: S08, familiar tray identifier

Samir recognizes an identifying mark supposedly shown in his pet photo. The mark itself has not been specified in these materials. Direction: show a quick comparison with the carried photo at this moment and define one visible shape, or allow the Page to identify its past escort before the drawing corroborates it. Do not present a vague mark as an already-established fair clue without a visible reference. This can be planted in episode 1's photo if desired, but need not create a new required pilot scene.

### 105-A1 — Low: Companion pronouns as proposal

Wisp uses she/her here. The inspected companion chapter is primarily mechanical and does not settle a clear pronoun in the passages checked. This is not proof of an error. Add a working Companion-pronoun choice to the production ledger or use Wisp's name until an explicit source is found. Child pronouns are correctly handled; the schema authority for the kids should not silently be extended to every creature.

**Verdict:** Revise Lampworks spatial/prop logic and remove the duplicated Ballroom obstacle before advancing this break. The emotional ending works, and the paired-bell relay is specifically demonstrated. A score of 86 does not indicate failure of the premise; it identifies an episode that can become clearer and funnier by doing fewer repeated things.

## Cross-episode constraints for scripts

1. Treat the full group as working companions, not six motionless background bodies who never have a personal response. One specific silent task per supporting child across an episode is enough; do not add eight reactions to every clue.
2. Guard the actual quiet beats: Maya's bowl, Mateo listening to an answer, Samir turning Bean's picture face up. Put jokes before or after the decisions.
3. Do not turn the epistemic cautions into constant cautious dialogue. The characters can hope, make a bounded claim, and choose a next action without explaining every theoretical alternative.
4. Preserve local opponents' distinct ends: Coatcheck distracted, Governess spares a repair, Archivist refuses classification, Watcher bypassed, Lamplighter's real loan condition fulfilled, Master continues without the visitors.
5. These reviews have not certified all scripts because none of these reviewed artifacts is a full script. Re-review actual revised breaks and later perform the required independent screenplay passes.
