## Required repairs

### B1-C01 — Major prop/evidence discontinuity: unrecorded live answer is replayed

**MM104-v2 S10 and S14.** S10 deliberately shows the recorder stopped and pocketed before Mateo creates a fresh pattern and receives a reply. S14 then has Mateo replay that response on the porch. The later replay is impossible under the stated prop state. It also undermines the fair distinction between the older recording and new responsive evidence.

The recorder can remain visible with a record indicator; stop recording after the answer and retain that new clip separately. Alternatively remove the replay and let the porch beat concern the remembered response. Preserve the separate old home recording and MM101 inside-House clip. Do not resolve this with a narration claim that the pocketed device recorded secretly.
