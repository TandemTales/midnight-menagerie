### Minor continuity repair — establish that Amina can reach the privacy card

**S10–S11.** The Governess remains inside the Story Nook while laying out cards on intended bed positions. The trolley subsequently blocks the arch. Amina then takes a card from a floor mark before the trolley is moved.
