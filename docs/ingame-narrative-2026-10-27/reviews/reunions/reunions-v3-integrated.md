# Midnight Menagerie investigations and pet reunions

## Delivery contract

These are finished proposed scripts, not a claim that the pet campaign exists. They use the section 02 unit contract plus the rules here. Source: the eight detailed kid investigations and shared rescue rules in `docs/design/kids/08-kid-08.md`. Exact wording and compressed clue routes are new. The three evidence units for each pet replace a sprawling mandatory checklist with a complete short investigation; they do not pretend to reproduce every old proposed clue.

**E units:** optional examination, 15–30 seconds, in the stated preferred region. Eligible only while the relevant present kid's pet state is unknown and sanctuary is false. Live traces never spawn after that pet is found safely or home. After sanctuary, optional historical records use a journal heading “Before the gate opened”; they never imply a current frightened or trapped pet. Active matching kid or party member is required for spoken recognition; otherwise use the supplied narrator recap as anonymous evidence without naming the absent kid's pet or granting that kid knowledge. The object can be a caption on the existing Curiosity screen. Actions “Look closely” and “Continue searching” are new prose options using the existing choice format; no gear requirement, resource cost or combat effect. “Look closely” displays exact text below and sets proposed N.clues[unitID]; “Continue searching” returns to play and does not grant the clue. First full; repeat recap only. Skip suppresses optional text. All clues can be missed without blocking the Heart. Each unit's quoted recap is its speakerless fallback only when its named pet is eligible; otherwise suppress the personal unit rather than invent name substitutions.

**R units:** essential personal reunion in the Heart's Pet Holding Ward, after HEART-EVIDENCE and before Keeper encounter. The Ward must be guaranteed by engineering; currently it is a designed room, not a verified campaign trigger. Read active kid, N.pet[kid], relevant E units; unknown plays full, found plays F line, home suppresses R and uses H or the helping line. Finish or skip to the explicit recap commits found only after the recognition/approach is presented. Read-time 35–60 seconds; no forced timer. No choices change rescue eligibility. Existing-system alternative is a prose scene at a new guaranteed event; without that hook this is script-only and must not advertise persistent rescue.

**F units:** return after reunion but before home, 10 seconds. Their existence requires permanent found state. No repeat kidnapping, injury, escort or pet loss. The pet is safe in a story space outside combat; the child can try the route out again. Failure does not erase recognition or changes.

**H units:** home vignette after confirmed successful exit and committed home state, 15–25 seconds; in a proposed hub story panel. Repeat only on explicit reread; skip recap. No new pet deck, stat bonus or gear reward. A static pet image and caption carries every physical action. Home has no combat endpoint. Continue closes the scene. No automatic claim that a chosen animal must become a worker.

All adaptations below are mild source-grounded manifestations selected provisionally, never computed from real time, failures or Haunt level. The story may show a changed animal remaining themselves. This package does not need four visual transformation stages.

## Shared reassurance and handling

**PET-FOUND-RETRY.** Location: expedition result after a loss; trigger only if at least one active kid has committed found and not home. Narrator: “The expedition has ended. The reunion has not been undone. Your pet remembers you. The next search is for a way out.” Use individual F lines at the next Heart visit. State: no rollback. Read time 15 seconds. Skip: “Found pets stay found.” Current-build alternative before persistence exists: “The expedition has ended. You can try again.” Do not display the found promise without its implementation.

**PET-HOME-RETRY.** Same result, active kid home. Narrator: “Your pet is safe at home. This expedition has ended; another can begin when you're ready.” Skip same first sentence. No pet is reinserted into the Ward. Existing-system alternative suppresses pet-home language entirely when no verified home state exists.

**PET-PARTY-REUNIONS.** Trigger: several active kids unknown/found in Ward. Proposed scene queue presents each kid's R or F in seat order. Each affected seat acknowledges its own first reunion or chooses its own recap; another player cannot skip that seat's first scene. This acknowledgement is a reading control, not permission for the pet to be rescued. For pass-and-play, show the current child's name above the acknowledgement. After every delivered full scene/recap, commit only that profile's found fact. Disconnected or mismatched profiles receive neutral shared prose and no invented completion; on reconnect, resume their pending scene. Before combat, always show PET-WARD-REST, including after all recaps. Never announce all eight home from one selected kid's victory. Actual synchronization remains an engineering dependency; without it, enable only local-profile completion and do not advertise a shared pet campaign.

**PET-WARD-REST.** Only when at least one present pet has just been found or revisited in the Ward; immediately after the R/F queue and before combat. Suppress for an all-home party and sanctuary visits. Solo caption: “Beyond the inner gate is a quiet room with food, water and a comfortable resting place. Its inner door stays open.
