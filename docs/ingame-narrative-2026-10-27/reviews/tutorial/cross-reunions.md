# Independent investigations and reunions review

Reviewer: tutorial agent, fresh manuscript review of section 06, no writer defense or earlier score read. Roles: child comprehension, agency, absent-party/state handling and adversarial skipping. No human or live playtest claimed.

## Required revisions

1. **High — PET-LENA-E1 versus PET-LENA-R.** E1 presents a yellow button in the Museum drawer and explicitly leaves the collection in place. R then says Lena “brought” the familiar yellow button. With no collection action, this can be the same prop teleported into inventory. Change E1 to a visibly different treasure, or explicitly establish a separate carried button in R; do not silently give the player an item they left.
2. **High — PET-SAMIR-E2 recap.** “Bean accepted food and an escort” asserts assent to being carried. The scene establishes accepted lettuce followed by transport; it does not establish voluntary escort. This is exactly the House's mistake. Use “Bean accepted food. The Page carried him inside without offering a route back.” Preserve warmth toward the friend while separating that act from valid permission.
3. **High — HEART-PERSONAL-FALLBACK.** E2 recap alone sometimes gives a conclusion without observable evidence. For example Eli's “puzzle routes all return to confinement” and Jordan's “helpful behavior has become an endless assignment” are narrator conclusions, not the promised new printed card. If all optional clues were missed, actually display E2's object caption/inscription in a guaranteed short page, then the recap or R. A fallback must supply evidence, not tell the player that evidence exists offscreen.
4. **Medium — PET-ELI-R equipment branch.** The base scene puts Eli's multitool on the bench before the optional branch checks whether it exists. A no-multitool player still receives a tool in the base scene, and substituting a buckle into “rests his multitool” is awkward. Use his bag in the base scene, then choose tool or buckle only for the optional inspection, with both exact text variants printed.
5. **Medium — PET-PARTY-REUNIONS.** “Party skip” commits all delivered reunions, but who can skip other players' first scenes is unspecified. A remote player could erase a child's first reunion moment by pressing Skip. Require each affected local seat acknowledgement for first R, or preserve each scene for private replay and avoid shared skip until policy is implemented. This is a presentation permission issue, not a morality choice.
6. **Medium — R contract and PET-WARD-REST.** The R recaps generally say exit blocked, but the safe pet holding context comes after multiple queued reunions. On a party skip, guarantee the Ward safety recap too before combat. No combat UI should show the pet in a target slot; no “guard your pet” implication. Current contract is good but needs exact ordering after skipped R.
7. **Low — PET-PRIYA-H.** “A foot warmer and a desk lamp” calls the gecko a tool immediately after the story criticizes usefulness overriding preference. The following retirement line corrects it, but simpler warmth is available: notebook warmed by Pixel followed by “Even my plans get a warm corner.” This is optional disagreement; the original can also read as affectionate observation, not a job assignment.
