# Independent cross review of regions 09 through 17

Reviewer: tutorial agent, read all nine current packages without prior reviews or the writer's defense. Roles: child onboarding, agency, state continuity, accessibility, mystery and adversarial skipping. Source spot-check: core game tutorial/control audit; no combat simulation or player test.

## Required or substantive findings

1. **High — MM17 B0 essential recap.** “The House is the Keeper” collapses the careful established distinction that the Keeper is the House's protective instinct given form. The longer identity paragraph is correct, but a skip-only player receives the least precise version. Use “The Keeper is the House's protective instinct” in the exact recap; keep no separate villain claim.
2. **Medium — MM10 C1.** Samir says “I asked you to keep a coat,” but the event is examination of a ticket, not a coat-check action the player or character took. Change to a comment on the ticket's actual known service. This is a small but direct invented-action problem.
3. **Medium — MM10 P.** The guest takes a coat and is allowed to depart through “the door.” Unlike MM15, the scene never specifies this is leaving a room rather than the mansion. A player can infer an easy mansion exit long before the Keeper, undermining the blocked-departure mystery. Say Ballroom doorway and that the guest heads into another hall; keep the result a finished party visit.
4. **Medium — MM17 F/B0 plus root HEART-EVIDENCE.** Three mandatory summaries of care/refusal can stack before the Ward/encounter, followed by MM17 P and root END-01 both opening a door. Define one canonical dispatch order and remove duplicate compulsory recitation. The full version should have new present evidence; the short version should carry it once. This is an integration action for root as well as this chapter.
5. **Medium — MM17 B0 transformation assurance.** The recap states “Animals can change and still remain themselves,” but full B0 never shows or says this. Optional C3 is the evidence; root R often demonstrates it, but all-home and no-personal-support branches need a reliable carrier too. Either insert a brief paired-photo observation when unseen, or document the precise earlier essential scene that delivered it. Never mark C3 found merely because the general claim was shown.
6. **Medium — most P recaps.** The exact concrete actions are strong; adding a final general moral to every recap makes late regions feel like repeat lessons. Preserve one necessary inference where needed, but use current observable consequences for others. This is particularly noticeable across MM11/12/13/14, whose last sentences repeat “choice/not compulsory” after scenes already demonstrate it. The actual scenes need less change than their summaries.
7. **Low — MM09 C1.** “Rabbit prison” is darker and more abstract than the otherwise gentle evidence lesson. A curious Mateo can simply say “I was about to write ‘rabbit.’ New note: ‘check the flame.’” Not a cruelty violation, but the current line may plant an animal-suffering idea just to retract it.
