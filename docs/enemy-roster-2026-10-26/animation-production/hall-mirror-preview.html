Action design

Idle: soft breathing, a short blink and a traveling glass glint. Loops without moving the stand.

Attack: withdraws in anticipation, lifts its palm for a premature greeting, waves twice and recovers.

Hurt: recoils, shuts its eyes, tucks the hand inward and regains its resting pose.

Defeat: offers a last goodbye, bows and sinks out of the glass. The empty mirror remains, with a nonlooping final hold.

No separate cast is required in the approved Hall Mirror matrix: its distinctive reflection gesture is in attack. No gameplay intent mapping was changed.
