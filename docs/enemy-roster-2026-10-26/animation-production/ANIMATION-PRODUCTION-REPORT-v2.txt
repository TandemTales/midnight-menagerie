METHOD AND LIMIT
This is a character-specific 2D source-art rig, not fully redrawn frames or AI video. The approved full-resolution Hall Mirror painting supplies the authentic stationary gold frame, face, hand and palette. A clean glass plate samples the source's genuinely empty upper glass. Signed reflection layers are articulated separately for head, body and palm; closed eyelids and local glass ripples/glints are composited. The geometry and alpha of the mirror stand stay fixed. It does not substitute whole-image wobble or duplicate static frames.
The initial silhouette-shaped removal mask left a faint outline in defeat. Revision 3 replaces the full affected glass strip, so the final pose is clean empty glass. Recovered imagegen originals, the previous diagnostic, and the revision-2 rig are retained.
This method is appropriate for a stationary mirror with an animated reflection. It does not establish that every other enemy can reuse this rig, and no low-quality generic batch was produced.

ACTION DESIGN
Idle: breathing reflection, brief blink, moving glass glint; clean periodic boundary.
Attack: withdraws in anticipation, lifts palm, waves twice, returns to source pose.
Hurt: distinct recoil with eye closure and an inward hand tuck, then recovery.
Defeat: last goodbye, bowed head, sinking and disappearing reflection; the stand remains as an empty mirror and never gets back up.
