Midnight Menagerie Enemy Roster
Complete level roster and 2D animation direction
A complete design roster for all 17 mansion regions: 119 regular enemies, 51 elites and 17 bosses, totaling 187 distinct named slots. Each level has seven regulars, three elites and one boss. The 29 enemies with existing animations are retained and marked in the roster.
Levels 1–10 preserve the finished list where compatible with required counts and existing art. Levels 11–17 develop the remaining regions. Familiar families deliberately retained from the source are differentiated by silhouette, haunting routine and motion—not simply recolored. Keep the tone cute and spooky, never predatory.
For existing animations, reuse the supplied clips unchanged. New animation directions are production proposals. Small casts, bounded props and readable tells keep the designs practical for 2D.
Enemy behaviors describe proposed design intent. Balance values, card rules and animation production remain implementation work.

Regular enemies

A narrow freestanding antique mirror with one small reflection offset inside the glass. Its reflection waves before the visitor does, then awkwardly tries to catch up.
