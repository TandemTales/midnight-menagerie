### PATTERN
Only one thing differs between the versions: where help/discoveries live. One build uses round purple enamel medallions on the left frame rail at mid-height, the other uses labelled brass "Discoveries / How to play" plaques that move around. The medallions are closer to the samples' round gold-rimmed buttons and stay in the same place on every screen, but they have no label. Worse, their keyboard/controller focus state throws away the medallion and draws a flat web box with a yellow outline. That box straddles the frame and covers content: the map's "THE WAY IN" lettering and the combat frame's rule. The plaques read better and keep their brass under focus, but on every screen they need a new place to fit. They cover the right-hand candle on the map and atlas, sit on top of the Plan button in the clubhouse, and peek out from under the settings and draw-pile dialogs. The better system is the medallion position with a focus state that stays a medallion (a lit gold rim, not a box). Even then, neither version gives the samples' engraved cartouche label; the medallions need a small engraved tag or tooltip plate to match `selectKid.png`'s round buttons.
