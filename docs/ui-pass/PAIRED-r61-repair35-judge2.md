### PATTERN
The addition wins when it is a solid, self-contained mass with a base and a shadow on open floor. It loses when it is a see-through lattice laid over the room's focal point. Needed next: (1) middle-floor objects with painted volume: side planes, a lit edge and varied detail in place of a flat silhouette with repeated stamps; (2) darks that hold colour in the side aisles and upper corners; (3) crisper mid-distance drawing.
