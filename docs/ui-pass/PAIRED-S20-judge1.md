### PATTERN
The two builds are the same everywhere except two rooms' near floor: every screen differs only by animation frame, flicker or a random painting, and 13 of the 17 rooms are byte-identical. Where they differ, the better version is the one that keeps the near floor lit without softening the objects on it. The attic's lifted corners hold the boards together, but the kennels' new bale silhouettes trade a crisp drawn block for a puffy, felt-like smear. The three things the rooms most need next to reach the samples: (1) the near-floor corners still fall toward black in most wings, and they should carry modelled, candle-lit boards or stone right to the frame; (2) near-floor objects (bales, trunks, rugs, urns) need crisp, inked, textured surfaces like the samples' painted props, not soft or lumpy outlines; (3) the screens' painted-board language (gold filigree, candles, cobwebs) is consistent, but the map's blueprint still cuts its right-hand room labels at the frame edge, and that should be fixed.
