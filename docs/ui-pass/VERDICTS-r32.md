# Round 32 (THE FIGHTS: THE BOSS ON THE FLOOR, ONE STAGE, THE CROWDED HAND) -- judges' verdicts, for the graft

Winner: PHTHALO (2 of 3 judges), 6.89 vs GINGHAM 5.06 (+1.83). RAWUMBER 6.78, CERULEAN 6.44.
Screens: PHTHALO combat-boss (2/3), combat (3/3); combat-crowd no majority (a three-way tie on Borda, PHTHALO on the tiebreak).

### verdict
The four builds share the same chrome. What separates them is staging. PHTHALO best answers this round's questions: the Butler stands in the room in front of a see-through gilt frame, his feet on the boards with a shadow, and in the ordinary fight the enemies are lowered onto the Kid's floor line with clean spacing at both sizes. CERULEAN is a close second and wins the crowded hand at 1280 because every card name fits on one line, though the long title gets very small. RAWUMBER's spotlight cone looks like an effect laid over the room, its boss drops below the Kid's floor line at 1280, and its Kid crowds the Door Greeter. GINGHAM repeats the defect the rubric names: the boss is boxed on a private dark backdrop, the Kid is pinned to the left edge, and four card titles wrap at 1280. No build fits between the samples yet. The party is still undersized next to the boss, and the 1280 chips and card titles are small.

### winner_fixes
- combat-boss, Butler's feet: add a darker, tighter contact shadow ellipse under his shoes, plus a lit floor pool like RAWUMBER's cold cone, so he is planted on the boards and lit by the room.
- combat-boss, top of frame: drop the gilt frame and its shield/12 chip about 20px so the medallion is not jammed against the top rail.
- combat (both sizes), enemy row: keep the lowered floor line but pull the nameplates and HP bars up tighter under each enemy, so a hovered card cannot cover the HP numbers.

- combat-crowd-1280, status chips: enlarge the Kid's and the Butler's chip coins and number badges about 20% to CERULEAN's size. That keeps the 2/2/3 and 3/2/4 counts legible on the Deck.
- combat-boss and combat, Kid: scale the Kid and Bones up about 15-20% on the same feet line, with a visible contact shadow, so the party reads as an equal on the stage.
- combat-crowd-1280, enemy chip row: tuck the chip cartouche closer to the HP bar so it does not drift right toward the column edge.
### grafts
- from RAWUMBER: The cold moonlight cone falling from the frame onto the boss, ending in a lit floor pool with a crisp contact shadow, gives warm candle against cold moonlight and grounds him.
- from CERULEAN: Larger status chip coins with gold number badges under both the Kid and the enemies are the most legible at 1280.
- from CERULEAN: The Butler stepping forward out of the open frame's mouth, overlapping its lower edge, sells the idea that he came out of the painting into the room.
### worst problems
- combat-boss / RAWUMBER (7): On combat-boss (both sizes) the Kid and Bones are small beside a Butler nearly twice their height, so the party reads as a minor figure. Scale the Kid up about 20% at the same feet line.
- combat-boss / GINGHAM (5): On combat-boss the Butler is boxed inside the gilt frame on its own dark blue backdrop, a portrait pasted over the room. Meanwhile the Kid stands at the far left edge, her feet about 50px lower than his, so there is no shared floor.
- combat-boss / CERULEAN (7): On combat-boss the Butler's shoes sit right on the gilt frame's bottom scroll and the crescent medallion, so he looks perched on the frame sill instead of on the floorboards. At 1280 the Kid is also pushed to the left wall.
- combat-boss / PHTHALO (7): On combat-boss the frame's shield medallion and its 12 chip are jammed against the top rail at y~60 with no breathing room, and the Butler's contact shadow is faint compared with RAWUMBER's lit pool.
- combat-crowd / RAWUMBER (7): On combat-crowd-1280, 'Put Yourself Back Together' wraps to two lines, and the three Bites are cropped to narrow slivers with only about 70px of face each. Widen the fan overlap on the Sit Pretty cards instead.
- combat-crowd / GINGHAM (5): On combat-crowd-1280 both left 'Sit Pretty' cards, the seventh 'Sit Pretty' and 'Shake, Boy!' all break their names onto two lines. The Kid's status chips are also the smallest of the four builds, and their numbers half-clip below the coins.
- combat-crowd / CERULEAN (6): On combat-crowd-1280 'Put Yourself Back Together' is forced onto one line by shrinking it to tiny type, and the Bites' ATTACK plates shrink to unreadable slivers. Fitting by shrinking the type defeats the brief, even though its status chips are the largest and clearest.
- combat-crowd / PHTHALO (7): On combat-crowd-1280 the 'Go Get It!' rules text wraps to three lines, and 'Put Yourself Back Together' takes two. The enemy chip row also pushes right to x~1010, close to the column edge.
- combat / RAWUMBER (7): On combat the Door Greeter, Dust Bunny and Calling Bell hang at mid-room (bases ~y430) with nameplates floating on bare floor below them. They read as hovering rather than standing on the boards in front of the Kid.
- combat / GINGHAM (5): On combat the Kid and Bones are jammed against the left wall, with their feet ~80px lower than the enemies' bases. This gives two separate stages, not one floor.
- combat / CERULEAN (6): On combat-1280 the Kid and Bones are tucked to the far left at x~150-290 with a wide empty gap before the Door Greeter. The enemies float at mid-height with no visible contact shadow.
- combat / PHTHALO (7): On combat the enemies are lowered onto the Kid's floor line, but their HP bars now sit only ~35px above the hand's top edge. A raised card will cover the Dust Bunny's 20/20.
### verdict
The FIGHT track splits on staging, not chrome; all four share the same gilt rail, hand and room. PHTHALO wins every screen. Its boss stands larger in front of an open frame, on the same floor line as the Kid. On the ordinary fight it lowers the enemies onto that floor, and at 1280 the crowded hand keeps every name and cost readable. RAWUMBER is a close second, with the best boss lighting but floating ordinary enemies. CERULEAN fits the crowded hand only by shrinking type. GINGHAM keeps the defect the brief names: the boss boxed on a private dark backdrop, the Kid off on a lower floor at the left edge, and card names wrapping at 1280. No candidate reaches sample level; the room and the small Kid hold them all near 7.
