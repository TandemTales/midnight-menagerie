### PATTERN
The two versions are pixel-identical apart from the one focused control on the left frame rail, so these votes are only about how keyboard or pad focus is drawn. The better version keeps the round purple enamel medallion with its gold rim, the samples' button language, and shows focus by adding an engraved cartouche label and a warm halo. The worse version swaps that object for a flat rectangle with a 1px yellow outline: a default web focus ring that also loses the "?" icon, so the control stops matching its neighbour (the book medallion on the map) and the rail is no longer consistent. Even in the better version the label plaque is drawn on top of the play area, over the map's "THE WAY IN" lettering and route line at 1280x800 and over the room's floor in combat, rather than sitting inside the gold frame. Its plaque is also plainer and smaller in ornament than the samples' ribbon and cartouche nameplates.
