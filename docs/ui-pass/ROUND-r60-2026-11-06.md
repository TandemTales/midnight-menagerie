# Round 60: the boards fitted to the larger type

**Winner: SCAPOLITE, 2 of 2 blind judges.** It scored 6.73 against DIOPSIDE 6.55, CHRYSOPRASE 5.95 and the baseline GALENA 4.20, a gain of +2.52. No screen is reference-fit. Merged as `fcacec99`.

## Why the round existed

Survey 11 lost its paired test, and the larger reading type was one of the causes. The type is the owner's and does not shrink, but nobody had fitted the boards around it. The shop showed 3 of its 5 Tricks behind a "More wares below" rail. Gameover's stats and the event's third choice were below the fold on the Deck, the settings frame ran off the screen, and the atlas's name plaques covered its plans.

## The winning build

SCAPOLITE imposed one layout system on every board:

- a title band;
- a decision area;
- an action bar;
- cards sized to whatever space is left.

Shop, reward, gameover, event and rest became one family that shows the whole decision at both sizes.

## Per screen

- **DIOPSIDE took four:** atlas, atlas-house, settings and clubhouse. Its atlas plans are inked strongly and its settings frame fits the screen.
- **SCAPOLITE took the other seven.**

Every screen was decided by 2 of 2 judges.

## The graft (`a992df2e`)

All of it is in SCAPOLITE's system:

- **Atlas:** DIOPSIDE's inked plate, with the plans strong, captions under each plan, and the key and compass in the north-east bay. The Discoveries and How to play plaques now sit inside their brass frames.
- **Settings:** DIOPSIDE's layout, with the frame and its four corners inside the screen at 1600 and 1280. DIOPSIDE's tighter line-height on the notes was not taken.
- **Clubhouse:** Discoveries and How to play hang side by side above Plan the Expedition, whole at both sizes. HAUNT LEVEL stays on one line under its crest at 1280.
- **Gameover:** the cartouche is clear of the top at 1600. At 1280 every Final Trick and all eight stats are in view.
- **Event:** each stake has its own line, so the three choices are one height.
- **Reward:** the location is one caption inside the plaque.
- **Rest:** REST is weighted with a gilt fillet, candle glow and a larger gold name. The plates stack in the corner, clear of the painting.
- **Map and run strip:** the pencil tray sits lower at 1280, so the bottom row of notes shows.
- **Shop:** each ware stands on a shelf board, its text on a notched parchment ticket set from the left.

Skipped:

- The 17 wing plans as distinct footprints. Every plan comes out of the generator as the same strip, about 2.7:1, so this needs plan generation, not the atlas.
- The cut room labels at the map's right edge. They come from the floor plan's own pan.

## The paired check

Two new blind judges, in opposite orders, compared the graft against plain SCAPOLITE on all 11 screens at both sizes: **graft 20, SCAPOLITE 1, same 1.**

- Reward is the only split: one vote the same, and one for SCAPOLITE's place names flanking the ribbon.
- Every other screen went to the graft, 2 of 2.
