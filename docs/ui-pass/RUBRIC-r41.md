# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 41: THE FOUR LOWEST ROOMS — INKED OBJECTS, THEIR OWN FLOORS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; and **a room that is
SOFT, hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step** — a
line should be crisp AND smooth, like ink.

**Five screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms as a player meets
them -- `sheet-hedge` (the maze, the fountain, the yew walk), `sheet-kennels`
(the kennels, the wash house), `sheet-sleeping` (the dormitory, the bedroom),
`sheet-passages` (the passage, the library passage, the closet) -- and one
fight, `fight-hedge`, also at 1280x800 (the Steam Deck). Everything was drawn
at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **Objects drawn, or rendered primitives?** Does each object that matters
   have an inked, broken silhouette, a material (leaf mass, weathered boards,
   grain, weave) and a contact shadow -- or is it a soft-edged, flat-shaded
   low-poly shape standing in an evenly lit render? This is the round's first
   defect.
2. **Each wing's own:**
   - the Hedge: clipped yew walls with a hard top line, an inked silhouette
     and a dark root line; a dark path -- not lumpy smeared blobs over a milky
     grey slab;
   - the Kennels: weathered, muted, inked doghouses; straw in clumps and
     drifts -- not primary-colour toy boxes over yellow confetti;
   - the Sleeping Quarters: its own beds, trunk and furniture, nothing cropped
     at both edges -- not the nursery's rocking horse and toy chest; no black
     right third;
   - the Passages: varied, inked panelling with doors and sconces -- not one
     stretched wood grain repeated down both walls.
3. **Its own floor:** does each room have a floor covering of its own, or the
   same red medallion runner you may have seen in other rooms of this house?
4. **The near floor** lit and dressed, nothing falling to black or empty
   mottle; ink, not grain; dark, not milky.
5. **Each room of a wing is a room that wing really has**, not the same room
   re-lit.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. Judge the near floor's dressing and light, not
  that the capture shows it.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-kennels' wash house the straw is yellow sticks scattered evenly over
the whole floor" is useful; "the materials are weak" tells a builder nothing.
