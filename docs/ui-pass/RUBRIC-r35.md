# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 35: THE NEXT FOUR ROOMS AND THEIR NEAR FLOOR

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; and **a room that is
SOFT, hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step** — a
line should be crisp AND smooth, like ink.

**Five screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms as a player meets
them -- `sheet-attic` (the attic, the attic library), `sheet-sleeping` (the
dormitory, the bedroom), `sheet-graveyard` (the yard, the plots, the gate),
`sheet-crypt` (the catacomb, the family vault, the ossuary) -- and one fight,
`fight-graveyard`, also at 1280x800 (the Steam Deck). Everything was drawn at
the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The near floor.** Is the floor nearest the camera dressed and lit (a
   rug, flags, turf with edges, a light pool, a prop or two standing in it),
   or half the frame fading to black with everything against the back wall?
   This is the round's first defect.
2. **Each wing's own:**
   - the Attic: no plain cubes -- strapped trunks, slatted crates, dust
     sheets with folds, a dress form; the left half lit, not black void; no
     orange or purple haze lying on the floor;
   - the Sleeping Quarters: the iron beds DRAWN -- inked rails and finials,
     patterned quilts with folds, pillows; the walls a wainscot with grain;
   - the Graveyard: dark grass with path edges, never a milky grey murk; the
     chapel a building -- buttresses, a slate roof with ridge and eaves, a lit
     porch -- not a box with a brick texture;
   - the Crypt: the plaques and sarcophagus carved, dark, grimed stone (not
     milky boxes); vault ribs lit overhead, not a black ceiling.
3. **Ink, not grain; dark, not milky.** Edges that matter crisp; soft or hazy
   is worse than stepped; a dark washed pale is a defect.
4. **Each room of a wing is a room that wing really has**, not the same room
   re-lit.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. Judge the near floor's dressing and light, not
  that the capture shows it.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-crypt's ossuary the sarcophagus at right is a pale flat box with no
carving" is useful; "the lighting is weak" tells a builder nothing.
