### PATTERN
Every pair differs by a few small objects and every margin is slight. The better versions give an object a lit edge and a shaded side (the cooler stone pedestal and bench, which also stop matching the floor's brown), draw stacked crates as full-size boxes sitting on the box below instead of shrunken ones, and put a contact shadow under each chair foot. Costs on the winning side: the full-size crates crowd the vase/bookshelf behind the side desks, one right-wall library crate looks sheared, and the chair-foot pools are small and hard. What the better versions most need next: (1) the heart stone given a real side plane and painted surface detail (veining, chips, a moulded panel) rather than a colour shift plus a one-pixel edge line, and the urn itself modelled to match; (2) crate stacks squared up and resting plumb, kept clear of the desk vases, with planks, nails and a top face painted and some size variety at floor level rather than uniform boxes; (3) contact shadows that are soft-edged and joined to the chair's main cast shadow, applied to the other standing furniture too (desk, globes, benches).
