# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 44: THE GREENHOUSE, THE GRAVEYARD, THE PUMPKIN GROUNDS, THE NURSERY

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; **a room that is SOFT,
hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step**; and **a
drawn object beats an empty, dimmed, muted or smoothed surface** — a room is
not better for having less in it, or for being tidier.

**Five screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms as a player meets
them -- `sheet-pumpkin` (the courtyard, the patch, the pond),
`sheet-greenhouse` (the conservatory, the palm house, the overgrown house),
`sheet-graveyard` (the yard, the plots, the gate), `sheet-nursery` (the
nursery, the wardrobe room) -- and one fight, `fight-pumpkin`, also at
1280x800 (the Steam Deck). Everything was drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **A void band?** In the Pumpkin Grounds and the Greenhouse, is the ground
   continuous and lit from the back wall to the front -- or does a black band
   cross the floor where the fighters stand, with objects floating above it?
2. **Each wing's subject, drawn:**
   - the Pumpkin Grounds: pumpkins on the ground with shadows, the mansion
     behind the wall a crisp inked silhouette -- not a soft pasted backdrop;
   - the Greenhouse: verdigris iron columns with rivets and rust -- not flat
     saturated green tubes;
   - the Graveyard: a lawn broken by a path, roots, leaves and long grass,
     clustered stones with shadows, an inked chapel -- not a flat green-brown
     plane with a few stones on it;
   - the Nursery: a painted rocking horse with dapples and a mane, lettered
     blocks sitting on the rug -- not a pale cutout and floating cubes.
3. **Its own floor:** does the Nursery have a rug of its own, or the red
   diamond rug you may have seen in other rooms?
4. **Ink, not grain; dark, not milky.** Each room of a wing a room that wing
   really has, not the same room re-lit.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. Judge the near floor's dressing and light, not
  that the capture shows it.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-pumpkin's patch a black band crosses the floor between the wall and
the flags, and the pumpkins float in it" is useful; "the lighting is weak"
tells a builder nothing.
