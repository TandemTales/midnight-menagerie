# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 20: THE RAIL AND THE HAND

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Two standing rulings hold: a
screen's COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter — and the darks are where this house
keeps its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect.

**Six screens**, each also at 1280x800: `hud-late` (the map with a run's
strip FULL — ten Keepsakes and eight Gear), `map`, `shop`, `combat`,
`combat-boss` and `combat-crowd` (nine cards in hand). **1280x800 is the Steam
Deck**, the platform this game ships on first: a layout that breaks at 1280 is
a defect of the screen, scored in `composition` and `readability`, not only a
readability note.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The run strip across the top.** Does it hold ONE row at 1280 even on
   `hud-late`, legible, with nothing under it crowded? Does it read as ONE
   carved object — a rail with ends, a surface and a shadow, the readouts set
   into it — or as a toolbar of separate pills? Does every folded group say
   what it holds? A bare number that does not say what it counts is a defect.
2. **The hand.** On `combat-crowd`, can you read every card's NAME? Is the
   rules text readable at 1280? Does the fan use the space it has?
3. **The boss under the rail** on `combat-boss`: crown and shield clear.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The room behind the fight (the WebGL room), the creatures, the Kid and the
  Companion, and which way the creatures face — identical in every candidate.
- Card ART and painted assets.
- A "PREVIEW" plate on a board: a harness screen opened outside a run.
- Arrangement dealt by seed.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this screen was painted by the same hand as the
samples? Say which object costs a screen its TRUE. For `worst_problem`, name
the screen, the object and where it is. "At 1280 on hud-late the seed, Haunt
and Tricks drop to a second row under the Keepsakes" is useful; "the HUD is
busy" tells a builder nothing.
