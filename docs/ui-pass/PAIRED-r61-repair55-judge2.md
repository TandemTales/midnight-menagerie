### PATTERN
The better versions replace a web-style text button with a drawn object (chalk stick, rubber, near-black enamel plate) and keep a round control's rim concentric and clear of its neighbours. The worse versions either leave a tool as one more cartouche or enlarge a rim until it collides: the double cog's slipped crescent at 1280, the zoom knobs kissing each other and the ruler and rod, the medallion sitting on the lower rule. No version has everything right; the ideal takes A's Chalk, Eraser and Undo/Clear from the map screens together with B's smaller knob and medallion sizes from the same screens. What the better versions most need next:
1. Shrink the two zoom knobs and the map-bar medallion so their richer rims keep 3-4 px of ground from each other, the ruler, the rod and the rules, especially at 1280.
2. Give the cog a seat that is concentric at both sizes: the double ring's 1600 look without the 1280 slip, and without the single cog's dim ghost ring to its right.
3. Carry the drawn-object treatment to the remaining cartouche buttons in the tray (Hand, X room/Unmark, You are here/Overview), and give the Hand its icon at 1280 as it has at 1600.
