SURVEY-room-greenhouse-conservatory: A by 1 -- the near-right planter trough, its ferns and the corner urn below the rail carry warm candlelight in A and read as lit, drawn objects, where B leaves them a dimmer, flatter olive; the fountain and lamp stay the clear centre in both, and both are equally crisp.

### PATTERN
The two builds match everywhere except the near ground, mostly the right third; A lights that foreground to the frame's foot and is a little more saturated, without going milky or pulling the eye off the fountain.
