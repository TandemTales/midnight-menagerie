### PATTERN
The better version of every pair is the same family of controls: round enamel buttons with a readable antique-gold rim (left-frame book and help, the back button with leaf finials, the cog in a complete riveted ring), which is what the samples' round purple buttons have; the worse version's rims fall to dark brown hairlines or a lopsided socket, so the enamel reads as rimless. The differences are small at actual pixels and every win is slight, with nothing clipped, covered or collided in either version beyond the eyelet under the label plate. Next the better versions need: (1) rim weight and modelling closer to the samples' arrow and check buttons, since the rims are still thin flat bands with no bevel highlight or mottled bronze; (2) the cog ring fills the strip from rail to rail and crowds the "16"/"0" beside it, so it wants a little air or a proper mount; (3) the left medallions at 1280 still hug the frame edge and the back button's finials are tiny, so both want slightly more room and bolder ornament to read on the Deck.
