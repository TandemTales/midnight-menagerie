## THE LOOP'S LESSON

A round's paired test compares it with the round before it, so a change outside a round's screens
is not seen, and a merge of many rounds has no judge at all. **A survey paired against the last
survey is the only thing that sees the whole house. Run one after any merge.** Rounds 48-58 ran
for 13 days without one.
