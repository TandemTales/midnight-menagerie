# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 40: THE BOARDS — TYPE AT 1280, THE HAND, THE PAINTINGS, THE MAP

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; the
darks are where this house keeps its colour; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck.

**Six screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `shop` is Mr.
Moth's market; `combat-crowd` is the boss fight with nine cards in hand;
`gameover` is the end-of-run board; `hud-late` is the map late in a run with
ten Keepsakes and eight Gear in the run strip; `map` is a wing's blueprint;
`reward` is the board after a fight, with two of the house's paintings hung.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **At 1280, can you read every line meant to be read?** Cards' rules text
   in the hand, the shop and gameover; list lines; plaque italics; the run
   strip's figures; the map's room pills. Anything about 9-11 px is the
   defect.
2. **The hand at nine cards:** narrow and low, below the fighters, every
   title readable, duplicates stacked -- or a wide fan climbing over the floor
   in front of the fighters? The boss's effect medallions by him, or bolted
   onto his bar?
3. **The paintings:** each nameplate on its own plate BELOW the frame -- or
   lettered over the canvas? Does each painting have its own wall, or is it
   squeezed into a gutter against the cards (reward at 1280)? On gameover,
   do the pictures hang on cords with their plates under them?
4. **The map's regions:** drawn shapes that follow the rooms they mark -- or
   rectangles with a dashed line and corner marks, like an editor's selection
   box?
5. **The run strip and the shop:** can you tell the ten Keepsakes and eight
   Gear apart at 1280? Are the shop's Keepsakes and Snacks objects on a shelf,
   or gold bars in a list?

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The fight's room and fighters; the map's room layout and routes; the cards'
  face design (art, frame) -- their text SIZE is this round's.
- What the paintings depict (the owner's artwork): judge how they hang.
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" and "Standalone preview": the harness.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe these boards were made by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on reward-1280 The Conservatory's three-line plate covers the bottom
quarter of its canvas" is useful; "the text is small" tells a builder nothing.
