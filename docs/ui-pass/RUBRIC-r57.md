native-size PNGs for each neutral candidate in the exact supplied order. Images
only: no source, reports, tests, identities, prior scores or another review.

Judge the room around the objects as well as the objects, at both sizes: gameover's
rows and badges against the central card, shop cost disks against their worn rims,
event's moonlight landing on wainscot and floor rather than ending as a cone, the

removing what was on it earns nothing unless what replaced it is richer.

scrolling. Do not prescribe smaller fonts or removed information. Below-viewport
content alone is not an access failure. Make no interaction claims from stills.

or altered lifetimes from one frozen frame. Ignore preview banners and unchanged
combat room/actor/hand art.

1280x800. A 9 means the same painted hand as the reference paintings, not the best
