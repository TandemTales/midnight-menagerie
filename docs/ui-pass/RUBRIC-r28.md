# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 28: THE PROPS AND THE DARKS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; and **a room that is
SOFT, hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step** — a
line should be crisp AND smooth, like ink.

**Six screens.** Five contact sheets of empty fight rooms — `sheet-passages`
(the passage, the library passage, the closet), `sheet-pumpkin` (the
courtyard, the patch, the pond), `sheet-graveyard` (the yard, the plots, the
gate), `sheet-kitchens` (the kitchen, the scullery), `sheet-kennels` (the
kennels, the wash) — and one fight, `fight-passages`, also at 1280x800 (the
Steam Deck). Everything was drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **Does the Secret Passage have a far end?** A wall or doorway at the end
   of the corridor, a ceiling line, sconces, side walls closing in — or a lit
   slab of floor floating in black? A starfield indoors is a defect.
2. **The mansion behind the Pumpkin Grounds and the Graveyard.** Is it a house
   at `UI/mainMenu.png`'s level — slate roofs, turrets, dormers, finials, lit
   arched windows, inked crisp against the sky — or a box with stamped window
   rectangles? Stepped glyphs and a soft blur both count against it.
3. **Is every hero prop a drawn object with a CONTACT SHADOW?** The range,
   pans and prep tables; the doghouses and pet beds; the headstones; the
   crates and lamp posts; the pumpkins. A flat-shaded primitive, a line-drawn
   wireframe or a prop floating with no shadow is the defect this round exists
   to remove.
4. **Are the darks dark?** A milky grey ceiling band, a lifted grey-beige
   floor, a grey-green lawn, a single red wash with no second light: each
   costs `background` and `palette`.
5. **Each room of a wing is a room that wing really has**, with the wing's
   palette and arch, and no two rooms of a wing the same room re-lit.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. A large bare floor on a sheet is the capture.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-kennels' wash room the pet beds at bottom left are flat grey tins
with no shadow" is useful; "the props are weak" tells a builder nothing.
