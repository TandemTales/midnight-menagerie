# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 2

Read `RUBRIC.md` (the eight dimensions, the 0–10 scale, the blindness rules — 9 is
"a viewer could not tell this screen was not painted by the same hand as the
samples") and then `RUBRIC-r1.md`. Round 2 uses round 1's two decision rules:

- **POLISH and COMBAT follow the REFINE section.** One `winner` whose work replaces
  the current screens; `screen_winners` for the record.
- **EXPAND-2 follows the EXPAND section.** `screen_winners` decide what merges, one
  screen at a time; `winner` is for the record.

On the COMBAT track the two screenshots are one screen in two fights — an ordinary
Scuffle and a boss — so judge them as one system. The creatures, the Kid and the
room are the same in every candidate; you are judging everything the player
touches and reads around them, and how it sits in that painted world.
