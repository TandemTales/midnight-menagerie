### PATTERN
Most pairs are the same picture: 10 of 17 rooms and several boards differ only by flicker, twinkle or noise. Where there is a real difference, the better version is the one that DRAWS the object -- grass, inscriptions and edge moss on the graveyard stones, a dresser with a base and an enamelled jug in the kitchen, a table with a drawer in the passages, a complete rim round the cog and zoom knobs, a plate that does not run into the title scroll -- and the worse version is the one that softens or veils: the pale, smoothed statues in the Heart, the flattened armchair quilting and plain drawer bars in the study, and above all the grey haze laid over the foyer room behind both fights and the opening. Note the title-side counter plate is fixed on the atlas in one build but collides on atlas-house and clubhouse in the same build's counterpart, so neither side has it right everywhere at 1280. What the better versions most need next: (1) carry the graveyard/kitchen level of painted detail to the remaining near-floor props that are still dark silhouettes or flat shapes (heart urn plinth, kitchen crates and baskets, foyer chairs) while keeping the hard modelling of the study chairs and heart statues; (2) keep the fight room crisp with darks that hold colour -- no haze layer -- and let it show floor under the furniture without cutting the column; (3) make every top-rail and header control sit with clear air at 1280: one consistent full-ring cog on all screens and a counter plate that never touches the title cartouche.
