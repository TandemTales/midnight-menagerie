# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 47: THE FOUR BOTTOM ROOMS — THE KENNELS, THE HEDGE, THE ATTIC, THE BATHHOUSE

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; **a room that is SOFT,
hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step**; and **a
drawn object beats an empty, dimmed, muted, smoothed or tidied surface** — a
room is not better for having less in it, or for being neater.

**Five screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms as a player meets
them -- `sheet-kennels` (the kennels, the wash room), `sheet-hedge` (the
maze, the fountain court, the yew walk), `sheet-attic` (the attic, its
library), `sheet-bathhouse` (the bathhouse, the steam room, the pool) -- and
one fight, `fight-kennels`, also at 1280x800 (the Steam Deck). Everything was
drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The hero props: inked objects, or primitives?**
   - the Kennels: doghouses of weathered painted boards with shingle roofs
     and name boards -- or flat primary-colour toy boxes with soft edges?
   - the Hedge: clumped leaf masses with gaps, withered patches and an inked
     shadow line -- or a tiled noise texture wrapped on flat planes?
   - the Attic: trunks with bevelled lids, straps and brass corners -- or
     low-res plaid boxes with stair-stepped edges?
   - the Bathhouse: enamel or copper tubs with a rim highlight and water that
     reflects -- or flat-coloured low-poly bowls?
2. **The near floor:** dressed with objects standing in a light pool -- or
   empty straw, empty lawn, black void?
3. **Milk on the darks?** The Attic's moonlight one hard shaft on dark boards
   -- or a lilac wash? The Bathhouse's tiles dark and inked -- or milky
   patches?
4. **Does anything float?** Every object standing on the floor has a contact
   shadow sized to its foot.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. Judge the near floor's dressing and light, not
  that the capture shows it.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-kennels' wash room the left doghouse is a flat green box with soft
edges" is useful; "the props are weak" tells a builder nothing.
