### PATTERN
Thirteen of the 33 pairs are identical or differ only by animation phase, flicker or camera drift. In the rest, the better version is always the one where a single object or control is more fully drawn: modelled furniture in the study and heart, lit and grounded props in the graveyard, kitchens, foyer and ballroom, a complete gold seat round the cog and map knobs, and a status plate moved clear of the title flourish. The worse versions are flat shapes, darks that fall to black, or a control that collides with its neighbour. None of the screen differences is more than slight; eight of the sixteen screens turn on the cog seat alone, which shows only at 1600. The three things the better versions most need next are:
1. Model the remaining near-floor props. The passages crates and tables, the kitchen crates and baskets, and the heart bench should get the study's treatment (tufting, panels, handles, turned legs, contact shadows); they are still flat-shaded boxes.
2. Make the darks hold colour. The kitchens' left wall and floor edge, the passages' shadow pools and ceiling, and the study's and heart's upper corners still drop to pure black.
3. Give the map tool strip and small HUD controls the samples' enamel and cartouche weight. The pencil, chalk and eraser chips and the Undo/Clear plates are still small and web-like beside the samples' buttons, and the -/+ knobs are cramped against the ruler at 1280.
