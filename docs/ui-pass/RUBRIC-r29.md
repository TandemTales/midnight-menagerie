# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 29: THE NEXT FOUR ROOMS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; and **a room that is
SOFT, hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step** — a
line should be crisp AND smooth, like ink.

**Five screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms — `sheet-hedge`
(the maze, the fountain court, the walk), `sheet-sleeping` (the dormitory, the
bedroom), `sheet-heart` (the Heart), `sheet-greenhouse` (the conservatory, the
palm house, the vinery) — and one fight, `fight-sleeping`, also at 1280x800
(the Steam Deck). Everything was drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **Is each wing its name?** A maze of withered, dark, clipped hedges with
   crisp tops and gaps -- or a stamped repeat of bright-green lumps? A
   dormitory with rows of iron beds down both walls -- or an empty dark box?
   Carved statues and legible portraits in the Heart -- or grey blobs under
   haze? A glasshouse of iron ribs and arched bays -- or a graph-paper grid?
2. **Is every hero prop a drawn object with a CONTACT SHADOW?** Beds,
   washstands, statues, planters, the fountain. A flat-shaded primitive or a
   prop floating with no shadow is the defect.
3. **Are the darks dark?** A grey lawn, a milky far wall, hazy light shafts
   over the portraits, a teal haze over the glass, a milky blue-grey floor:
   each costs `background` and `palette`.
4. **Each room of a wing is a room that wing really has**, with the wing's
   palette and arch, and no two rooms of a wing the same room re-lit.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. A large bare floor on a sheet is the capture.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-sleeping's dormitory the beds along the left wall are flat grey
slabs with no shadow" is useful; "the props are weak" tells a builder nothing.
