### PATTERN
The two builds are the same everywhere except three rooms. In those rooms the better version takes a near-floor object out of silhouette and models it: the passages' lit book stacks, the heart's pale stone urn and the hedge's leafy spiral topiary. Every boarded screen and both fights differ only by animation and light-shaft frames, so they are effectively ties. To get closer to the samples, the better versions most need three things next. First, near-floor objects need painted surface detail (grain, tooling, wear), not flat-shaded blocks; the book stacks and the urn's plinth are still untextured slabs. Second, the rooms' deep greys and blacks need warm candle bounce, so the dark masses (the hedge, the heart's far chairs, the kennels' near floor) hold colour instead of going to black. Third, the hand-built 3D props (the heart's shrub crown, the attic trunks, the kennels' hay bales) need the samples' ink line and painterly finish, so the rooms sit closer to `mainMenu.png`'s painted level.
