### PATTERN
The better version is always the one whose nearest flagstones stay lit enough to show their joints and grain instead of sinking to black in the bottom-left corner; the lift keeps its warm colour and does not go milky. The difference is small (about 5-10 levels), so each win is slight. Needed next: (1) near-floor objects painted with real surface detail and crisp contour ink rather than soft, slightly hazy 3D shading (pumpkins, crates, headstones); (2) side walls and deep left/right edges holding colour and form (pumpkin courtyard has no side enclosure, lampworks' benches are cut by both frame edges); (3) a stronger candle-versus-moonlight split with gold and lavender accents.
