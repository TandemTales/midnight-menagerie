# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 27: THE CARD

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's COLOUR
is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn, well-detailed
objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps its colour; a
soft or smeared surface is not a fix for a flat one.

**Five screens**, each also at 1280x800 (the Steam Deck): `combat-boss` (a
hand of five), `combat-crowd` (nine cards), `reward` (three cards offered),
`shop` (six cards for sale), `piles` (the draw pile viewer).

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The lower half of every card** — the rules panel under the art. Is it a
   PAINTED surface (paper, vellum or enamel with a bevel, a vignette, a faint
   pattern, gilt corners, as on the samples' framed panels) or a flat purple
   fill? A flat fill is the defect this round exists to remove. Does the
   card's TYPE (Attack, Skill, Power) still read at a glance?
2. **The rules text at 1280.** Is every line readable — about 11-12 px or more
   — on every screen? Do the names in a row of cards keep one size?
3. **The nine-card hand** at 1280: does every name stay inside its ribbon,
   every cost coin show, every rule read as a block rather than a one-word
   column? A name spilling over the art is a defect.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The card ART (the illustration in each card's window).
- The rooms behind the fights and the boards, the creatures, the Kid and the
  Companion, the run rail (except the "NO KEEPSAKES" plate).
- A "PREVIEW" plate: a harness label.
