# Round59 blind rubric amendment

Read RUBRIC.md. Inspect all four references first, then every individual native-size PNG for each
neutral candidate in the exact supplied order. Images only: no source, reports, tests, identities,
prior scores or another review.

Ten of these screens are empty fight rooms at 1280x800 (`room-*`). Four are the same rooms with a
fight in them (`fight-*`), which is what a player sees, at 1600x900 and 1280x800. Judge:

- **room-*:** do objects stand on the near and middle floor, with feet and shadows, or is half the
  frame bare floor and carpet? Are the room's ceiling, far wall and side walls in frame? Does the
  floor hold together, or does it fall to black between the back wall and the near floor? A row of
  small objects along the back wall is a bare room.
- **fight-*:** does any fighter stand THROUGH a piece of furniture, or on one? That is the worst
  defect on these screens and outweighs any amount of dressing: mark it down hard, and name the
  piece and the fighter. Does each fighter stand on lit floor, with the furnished room around and
  behind them? Can you still read every fighter at a glance? Does a prop cross a nameplate, a
  sign or a Courage bar?
- **fight-party:** can you recognise all four Kids and all their Companions, standing on floor?

A drawn object beats an empty, dimmed or muted surface. An empty floor that avoids a collision
earns nothing. A pale, milky or grey dark is a defect. A soft, hazy or smeared room is worse than one
whose lines stair-step. A translucent smear no one can name is a defect.

Do not prescribe moving fighters, smaller fonts or removed information. The `PREVIEW` tag and
`STANDALONE PREVIEW` banner are the capture's, not the game's: ignore them. The boards around the
fight (hand, plates, run strip) are unchanged: ignore them. A room's name along the foot of an
empty capture is the capture's too.

Score each screen conservatively. A 9 means the same painted hand as the reference paintings, not
the best incremental candidate. Reference-fit requires coherence>=9 and no default-web-UI appearance.
Select one coherent winner, rank every screen and give specific regional fixes and only worthwhile
grafts.
