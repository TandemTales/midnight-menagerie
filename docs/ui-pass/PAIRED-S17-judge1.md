### PATTERN
This pair differs almost only in how far warm light reaches the near floor of five outdoor or long rooms. Every screen and twelve of the seventeen rooms are identical apart from animation frames and camera sway. Where the better version wins, its near flagstones, planks or beds stay lit and legible down to the frame's foot. But warmth alone does not win: in the hedge maze, the darker near third reads as murk rather than mood. What the better versions most need next to reach the samples:
1. Real objects standing on the near floor, with feet and shadows. The hedge path, the passages' runner and the courtyard foreground are still wide empty planes.
2. A painted finish on the rooms' surfaces. The hedges, flagstones and wall panels still read as tiled 3D texture, not brushwork like `mainMenu.png`.
3. Crisper near-floor detail without a grey veil, so lit foregrounds keep the samples' deep aubergine-black darks instead of lifting toward flat brown.
