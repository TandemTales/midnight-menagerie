# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 45: THE PROPS — THE LAMPWORKS, THE KITCHENS, THE BATHHOUSE, THE HEDGE

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; **a room that is SOFT,
hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step**; and **a
drawn object beats an empty, dimmed, muted, smoothed or tidied surface** — a
room is not better for having less in it, or for being neater.

**Five screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms as a player meets
them -- `sheet-lampworks` (the lampworks, the wax room, the boiler house),
`sheet-kitchens` (the kitchen, the scullery), `sheet-bathhouse` (the
bathhouse, the steam room, the pool), `sheet-hedge` (the maze, the fountain
court, the yew walk) -- and one fight, `fight-lampworks`, also at 1280x800
(the Steam Deck). Everything was drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The props: drawn objects, or pale placeholders?**
   - the Lampworks: LAMPS crates with slats, nails, worn stencils and straw,
     a lantern with a lit glass and flame -- or pale plywood boxes with
     stepped edges and a blown-out lozenge?
   - the Kitchens: sacks in burlap with ties and flour, glazed crocks, a
     scarred butcher's block -- or grey blobs and a flat box?
   - the Bathhouse: enamelled tubs with rims, claw feet and towels -- or
     toy capsules with flat fills?
   - the Hedge: leaf walls that hold their texture on the side faces, torn
     withered crowns -- or vertical stretched streaks on the oblique walls?
2. **Does anything float?** Every object standing on the floor has a contact
   shadow sized to its foot.
3. **Milk on the darks?** The floors dark and coloured -- or grey, lavender
   or milky patches and haze?
4. **Ink, not grain.** Each room of a wing a room that wing really has.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. Judge the near floor's dressing and light, not
  that the capture shows it.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-lampworks' wax room the right LAMPS crate is a pale flat box with
stepped edges" is useful; "the props are weak" tells a builder nothing.
