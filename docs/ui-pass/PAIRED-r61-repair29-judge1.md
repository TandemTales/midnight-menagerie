### PATTERN
All three pairs are the same room; the only difference is how much light reaches the near floor in the bottom third, so every margin is slight. The better version is the one whose foot-of-frame flagstones stay lit and saturated rather than falling to black. None buys this with a pale or milky dark. Needed next: (1) near props (crates, hay bales, grave slabs, pumpkins) painted with modelled detail and firm contact shadows rather than soft dark smears, most visibly the pumpkins' black blob shadows; (2) a crisper overall image; (3) the remaining dark floor corners (lampworks bottom-left, pumpkin bottom-left and bottom-right), and edge-cut fragments such as the pumpkin at far left of the courtyard and the candle stand at the crypt's right edge brought fully in or removed.
