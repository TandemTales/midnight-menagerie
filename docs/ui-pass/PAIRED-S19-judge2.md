### PATTERN
This pair is almost entirely static: 30 of 33 pairs are pixel-identical or differ only by animation phase, swaying ornaments or random paintings. The three real differences are all near- and mid-floor objects in rooms, and the better version wins each one the same way. It keeps the object lit, coloured and modelled (the passage's book stacks, the hedge's spiral topiary, the heart's urn) rather than letting it sink to a dark silhouette or swapping in a drab boxy prop. To get closer to the samples, the better versions most need: (1) near-floor props that are modelled and lit by the candlelight and carry painted detail, as in the sample boards' still-life dressing, instead of flat-shaded low-poly blocks (the hedge topiary and the kennels' hay bales are still crumpled or boxy); (2) room surfaces with painted texture and ornament rather than smooth gradients, especially floors that fade to bare brown or black at the foot of the frame; (3) a richer gold-filigree treatment of the screens' many thin rectangular panels (the gameover lists, the settings boxes, the shop's keepsake and snack trays), so that they read as cartouches rather than bordered web panels.
