### PATTERN
The better versions draw the top of the room: a ceiling with beams, a panelled or furnished upper wall, a cornice line, rail posts, so the room is closed overhead instead of fading to black. Where the only change is a small stone pedestal, the better version is the one with straight, hard, lit edges; the worse one bows or slants. The winners still need: (1) upper-wall and ceiling additions kept clear of what already hangs there (chandeliers now sit on top of the cornice, a print's frame and the bookcase-top objects in the study); (2) side-wall versions of the new detail drawn as crisply as the back-wall versions (the library's side posts and the heart's side-wall panels/cornice stair-step or smear with colour fringing); (3) the new ceiling beams given weight and wood colour, since they read as thin pale grey rails that stop short of the walls rather than timber.
