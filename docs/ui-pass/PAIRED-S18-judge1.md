### PATTERN
This survey's two builds are nearly the same build. Every screen, and thirteen of the seventeen rooms, is pixel-identical or differs only by an animation frame. The four rooms that differ all differ the same way: the better version has drawn, shadowed objects on the near or middle floor instead of bare boards or lawn. Heart and hedge favour A for that; passages and pumpkin favour B. To reach the samples, the better versions most need, first, near-floor objects that are lit and modelled rather than near-silhouettes (the passage's book stacks and the hedge's faceted topiary both read low-poly). Second, they need painted, textured surfaces in place of the CG sheen on the rooms' furniture, so the rooms sit closer to mainMenu.png's painting. Third, the blueprint map needs to hold its decision inside the frame, because its right-hand rooms are still cut by the edge at both sizes.
