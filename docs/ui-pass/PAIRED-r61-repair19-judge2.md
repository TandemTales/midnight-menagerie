### PATTERN
Every pair differs only in near/middle-floor dressing; backgrounds, haze and darks are identical, so the decision is always whether the foreground is furnished. The furnished version wins each time, by most where the added objects are well lit and clearly modelled (bench, armchair, hay bale, jack-o'-lanterns) and by least where they are dark or low-poly (the passage's unlit book stacks, the hedge's faceted topiary).
