### PATTERN
In every pair the geometry, objects, crispness and framing are identical; the only difference is in the darkest tenth of the picture, where the better version lets shadow keep a hue (wood-red, oxblood, indigo, violet) and the worse one desaturates it toward grey or dead black. The coloured darks are not paler or milkier (mean luminance moves by under 1 level out of 255), so they read as painted shadow rather than a lifted haze, and they give the warm-candle-against-cold-shadow contrast the samples have. The differences are slight at native size and only clear when zoomed, hence 1 throughout. Next the better versions most need: (1) the far floor band just under the far wall still collapses to a featureless black strip in all four rooms, losing the feet of far objects such as the heart's statues and the ballroom's column bases; (2) near and middle objects such as the crypt's headstones, the study's book piles and the ballroom's chairs need more painted surface detail and firmer contact shadows to match the samples' modelling; (3) the side walls at the frame edges (the crypt's outermost niches, the heart's portrait walls, the study's left bookcases) are still too dark to read as drawn surfaces and need local candle light on them.
