### PATTERN
The better version in every pair is the same build: its small round controls each get their own concentric, studded or leafed gold rim (back button, settings cog), its side medallions carry a brighter inner ring and sit a few pixels in off the frame edge, and the help label plate is joined to its medallion by a drawn collar and leaf bracket rather than a stud. The worse version leaves the cog's ring off-centre inside the strip's end cap, so two arcs collide, and its back button rim is too dark to read as gold. The differences are small at actual pixels (never more than "slightly"), nothing is cut or covered in either version, and the 1280 frames show the same result as the 1600 ones.
