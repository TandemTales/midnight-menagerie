## WHAT BOTH JUDGES SHARE

1. **The shop's Coatcheck Ticket is cut off with an ellipsis at 1280** --
   both, as worst_problem ("the one function the shop exists for ... fails on
   the Deck"); and the goods under the domes are 20-30 px icons, too small for
   their domes.
2. **The crowded hand at 1280** -- both: the ×3 stack medallions overlap the
   next card's cost and title; Put Yourself Back Together's three-line title
   eats its art; the Butler's intent coins crowd his nameplate's top rule
   (J1: "lift the status coins off THE BUTLER plate"; J2: "a small dark plate
   of their own"). With the paired verdict: back to survey 6's bracket.
3. **The Hedge** (both 4, top headroom): "lumpy smeared blobs with no clipped
   top line, and the path is a milky grey slab" (J1); "clipped leaf masses
   with an inked silhouette and a dark root line" (J2).
4. **The Kennels** (both 4): "flat primary-colour toy boxes" and "straw is
   yellow line-segment confetti over the whole floor" (both, nearly word for
   word).
5. **Un-inked CG props** (both SYSTEM): chairs, tubs, doghouses, the rocking
   horse, sacks, crates, the trunk read as "low-poly, flat-shaded objects ...
   in a well-lit render" (J1) / "soft edges and no line ... a render, not the
   samples' inked painting -- the material defect behind most rooms' 4-5"
   (J2). The paired judges say the same of round 39's new props.
6. **One reused red runner** (both): the Foyer's medallion runner in the
   Foyer, Heart, Passages and Sleeping Quarters, the same pattern as the rug
   in the Study, Attic, Nursery and Kitchens; and the Nursery's rocking horse
   and toy chest reused in the Sleeping Quarters.
7. **The front floor falls to black or empty mottle** (both): J1 sleeping,
   greenhouse, attic, crypt, pumpkin, lampworks; J2 heart, pumpkin, kennels,
   bathhouse, passages, hedge, crypt, ballroom, kitchens, attic, graveyard.
8. **The last web controls** (both): the piles' search input, the settings
   seed fields, the event's arrow circles beside the numeral medallions (J1:
   "each choice drawn twice").
9. **The pale parchment family** (J1 SYSTEM; J2 on atlas): the map, hud-late,
   atlas, atlas-house, event and clubhouse cork are where the house loses its
   darks.
