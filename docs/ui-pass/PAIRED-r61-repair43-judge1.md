### PATTERN
The better version in every pair is the one whose small round controls are finished objects: the glyph sits centred on the purple enamel, the rim is lit antique gold rather than dark bronze, and the medallion mounts have lit rivet beads, which is how the samples' round buttons are drawn. The worse version's strip glyphs slide off the left of their buttons, which reads as a layout bug more than a style choice, so the gap is clear on every screen that shows the strip icons and only slight on atlas and clubhouse, where the rim on one button is all that changes. The cost of the better version is small: rims about 3 px wider leave the 1280 strip tight around the numbers, and the gear loses its outer ring and leaves a faint ghost arc.
