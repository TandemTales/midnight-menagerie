### PATTERN
The two builds are near-identical: 25 of the 33 pairs differ only by animation frames, camera sway or not at all, and the real changes are in the near-floor light of five rooms and one newspaper prop. Where they do differ, the better version lights the bottom of the frame warmly enough for the near floor to read as a lit surface, without washing it pale. The hedge maze shows the risk in the other direction, where the brighter version turns milky. The better clubhouse clipping wins because its prop is complete (it has a headline), not because it is busier. What these rooms most need next to reach the samples is: (1) real objects with feet and shadows on the bare near floor of the pumpkin courtyard, passages, hedge and heart, which so far have gained light and nothing else; (2) the painted grain and candle-and-moon lighting of mainMenu.png in the far walls, which still look like clean renders next to the samples; (3) on the boards, fewer flat dark panel fills behind the text, using the gold-rimmed cartouche and damask treatment of selectKid.png instead.
