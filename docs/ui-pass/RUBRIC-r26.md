# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 26: THE BOARDS' WALLS AND THE DIALOGS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; and a soft or smeared
picture is not a fix for a missing one.

**Six screens**, each also at 1280x800 (the Steam Deck): `reward`, `event`,
`rest`, `gameover` (four boards that share one painted room behind them),
`settings` and `piles` (two dialogs).

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The pictures on the boards' walls.** Behind the reward's ribbon, above
   the event's parchment, behind the rest's cards and the gameover's plinth:
   is every framed picture a DRAWN picture — a subject you can name, painted
   in the samples' hand — or grey smeared noise? A frame showing a smear is a
   defect of `background` and `material`; a frame taken away is better than a
   smear, and a drawn picture is better than either.
2. **What the dialogs stand on.** A dialog over a flat black void scores low on
   `background`. A dimmed room behind it is the answer.
3. **Every control.** Does any still read as a web form element in gilt — a
   segmented OFF/ON switch, a select box with a chevron, a rectangular outline
   button? That is a `coherence` and `material` defect.
4. **Small type at 1280.** Any caption under ~11 px at 1280x800 — the italic
   sub-captions in action plaques, the gameover's lists — is a `readability`
   defect. Game Over must not run its lists to the panel's foot.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The run rail across the top, the hand of cards, the creatures and Kids'
  painted art, the WebGL room behind the pile viewer's fight.
- Card ART. A "PREVIEW" plate is a harness label.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

Name the screen, the object and where: "on event the middle frame above the
parchment is a grey smear with no subject" is useful; "the wall is weak"
tells a builder nothing.
