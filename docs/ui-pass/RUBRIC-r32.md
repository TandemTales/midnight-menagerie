# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 32: THE FIGHTS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; the
darks are where this house keeps its colour; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck.

**Three screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `combat-boss`
is the Foyer's boss fight with a five-card hand; `combat-crowd` is the same
fight with nine cards in hand and effects on the board; `combat` is an
ordinary Foyer fight. Everything was drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **Does the boss stand IN the room?** His feet on the room's floor with a
   contact shadow, the room's light on him -- or boxed in a frame on its own
   private dark backdrop, a portrait pasted over the room? A gilt frame
   behind him as ornament is fine; a separate painting is the defect.
2. **One stage.** Do the Kid and the Companion stand on the same floor line
   as the enemies, at a size that reads, with shadows -- or small at the far
   left edge, higher up the floor?
3. **combat-crowd at 1280: can you read every card's name on one line and see
   every cost?** Overlap should eat the art, not the title band; a hovered or
   focused card lifts clear.
4. **The status chips** under the enemies and the Kid: big enough to read the
   number at 1280?

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The room behind the fight (its walls, stair, columns): no builder changed it
  this round. Score the FIGHTERS' relation to its floor, not its drawing.
- The card faces' own art and text; the run strip; the enemies' painted art.
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this fight was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on combat-crowd-1280 the third Bite's name is hidden under the fourth
card" is useful; "the hand is crowded" tells a builder nothing.
