# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 6

Read `RUBRIC.md` first: the eight dimensions, the 0–10 scale and the blindness
rules. 9 is "a viewer could not tell this screen was not painted by the same hand
as the samples". Then read `RUBRIC-r1.md`.

- **POLISH and COMBAT both follow its REFINE section.** `winner` is the one
  candidate whose work replaces the current screens; `screen_winners` are
  recorded, but do not decide the merge.

Only these two tracks run this round. There is no DIALOGS or KIDS' PLACES
judging; those screens keep round 5's winners.

On the COMBAT track the three screenshots show one screen in three fights: a
Scuffle, a boss, and the same boss with nine Tricks in hand and conditions stacked
on both sides. Judge them as one system, and judge the crowded board as hard as
the other two. You are judging everything the player touches and reads around the
creatures.

**The creatures are animated, and every candidate caught them on a different
frame.** Since round 5 the enemies play real animation, so the Door Greeter or
The Butler will be in a different pose in each candidate's capture of the same
board, and in the baseline's. That is not a difference any builder made and it is
not evidence about any candidate. Do not score a creature's pose, and do not let
one candidate's luckier frame flatter it: score the board, the furniture, the
cards, the readouts and the frame around the creature.

**The background dimension.** The room behind every board is still a render, not
one of Josh's paintings, and it is the same render in every candidate on a given
screen. Score it honestly — it is the dimension that holds this pass at 7 — but
do not let it swing the candidates apart, because no candidate can fix it this
round. Where a candidate has genuinely changed what sits on or in front of that
ground (a lit window, a painted wall panel, a dressed alcove), that IS its work
and counts.

**The top HUD strip belongs to POLISH this round**, on every screen including the
three fights. If it has improved, credit POLISH for it; if it has not, say so in
POLISH's `winner_fixes`, not COMBAT's. Score what you see on the COMBAT screens
either way — a fight that still wears a web toolbar is still a fight that does
not fit between the samples.
