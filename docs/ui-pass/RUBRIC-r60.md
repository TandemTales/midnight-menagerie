# Round60 blind rubric amendment

Read RUBRIC.md. Inspect all four references first, then every individual native-size PNG for each
neutral candidate in the exact supplied order. Images only: no source, reports, tests, identities,
prior scores or another review.

These are the boards, at 1600x900 and 1280x800. The text on them is meant to be large; that is
the owner's decision. Judge whether the board was FITTED around it:

- **Every board:** can a player see the whole decision without scrolling? Count what is cut off.
  A "More below" or "More wares below" rail that hides a card, a choice, a stat or a Trick is a
  defect; one at 1280 that still shows every choice above it is a lesser one. Text cut by an
  edge, a plaque or a bar, or one panel running past another, is a defect. Smaller words are
  NOT a fix: if reading text looks shrunk compared with the other boards, mark it down.
- **atlas, atlas-house:** is the house's drawn plan what you see, each wing a recognisable
  footprint on a real plate, or do labels and plaques cover the plans? Does the legend or a
  title cover a wing? Is any count or label clipped?
- **shop:** are all five Tricks and every shelf visible, each line of every Trick readable?
- **gameover, reward, event, rest:** are the featured cards, every Final Trick and stat, both
  rarity plaques, at least two whole event choices, and all four rest choices in view?
- **settings:** is every section reachable and obvious, and does every plate and figure stay
  on its page?
- **hud-late:** can you read the wing's name in the run strip, or is it cut to a letter?
- **map:** does the sheet hold its darks like a saturated cyanotype, or read milky? Is every
  map control still there?
- **clubhouse, atlas:** do `Discoveries` and `How to play` belong with the painted controls, or
  read as plain web boxes?

A drawn object beats an empty, dimmed or muted surface. A board made to fit by removing what
was on it earns nothing.

Make no interaction claims from stills. The `PREVIEW` tag and `STANDALONE PREVIEW` banner are the
capture's, not the game's: ignore them. Names in a plate are fixture names. Hung paintings change
at random between captures: do not judge which painting hangs.

Score each screen conservatively, with explicit notes for 1600x900 and 1280x800. A 9 means the
same painted hand as the reference paintings, not the best incremental candidate. Reference-fit
requires coherence>=9 and no default-web-UI appearance. Select one coherent winner, rank every
screen and give specific regional fixes and only worthwhile grafts.
