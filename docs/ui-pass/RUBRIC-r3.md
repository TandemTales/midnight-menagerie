# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 3

Read `RUBRIC.md` first: the eight dimensions, the 0–10 scale and the blindness
rules. 9 is "a viewer could not tell this screen was not painted by the same hand
as the samples". Then read `RUBRIC-r1.md`. All three tracks this round follow its
**REFINE** section:
- `winner` is the one candidate whose work replaces the current screens;
- `screen_winners` are recorded, but do not decide the merge.

On the COMBAT track the three screenshots show one screen in three fights: a
Scuffle, a boss, and the same boss with nine Tricks in hand and conditions stacked
on both sides. Judge them as one system, and judge the crowded board as hard as
the other two, because fights are played with a full hand. The creatures, the Kid
and the room are the same in every candidate. You are judging everything the
player touches and reads around them, and how it sits in that painted world.

On the DIALOGS track, two screenshots are dialogs opened over a screen: Settings
over the map, and a pile of cards over a fight. The third is the opening's story,
told over the lit room. Judge the dialog and how it sits over what it covers. The
screen underneath is the same in every candidate, though each candidate chose how
far to dim it. A dialog either belongs to the painted world or is a web page laid
on top of one.
