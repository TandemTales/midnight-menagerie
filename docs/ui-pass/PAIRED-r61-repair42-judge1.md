### PATTERN
In both rooms the better version is the one whose repeated objects are modelled as solids with light falling across them (round skulls with a lit side, pumpkins with lobed ribs) rather than flat faces or seam lines printed on a silhouette; nothing else in either frame moves, so floor, walls, framing and crispness are a draw. The better versions are still soft and noisy beside the samples' ink-crisp drawing. They most need next: (1) crisp drawn edges and ink lines on the near props, since the skulls' eye sockets, the pumpkins' stems and carved faces and the hay bales are all blurry or stair-stepped at Deck size; (2) painted surface detail and variety on the objects, since every skull and every jack-o'-lantern face is the same stamp, the hay bales are a camouflage blotch rather than straw, and the vine leaves are flat green smears; (3) darks that hold colour and firmer contact shadows, since the crypt's side aisles and the near foot of the courtyard fall to near-black and several pumpkins sit on vague pools rather than grounded shadows.
