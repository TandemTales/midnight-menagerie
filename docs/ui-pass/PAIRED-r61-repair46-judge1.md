### PATTERN
The better versions are the ones where each small gold object is whole and separate: header plates stand clear of the title sign's scrollwork instead of tucking a finial under it, the status rail's numbers have air between them and their round icons, the purse plate's bracket end no longer bites "+1"/"+3" at 1280, and the settings cog has one clean rim rather than a second, off-centre arc that reads as a rendering slip. On the map the better version also gives the zoom knobs and the home medallion the samples' double gold rim with studs, so they read as the same family as the rail medallions rather than flat dull discs; that is the clearest gain and why the map screens score 2 while rail-only and plate-only screens score 1.
