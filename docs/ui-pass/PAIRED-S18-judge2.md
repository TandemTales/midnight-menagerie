### PATTERN
Only six pairs really differ, and five of those are rooms. In every one, the winner is the version with drawn objects standing on the near and middle floor, casting shadows: the pumpkin-and-bale group, the topiary and urn, the bench and armchair, and the book stacks. The losers leave that ground as an empty lit plane. The one screen that differs is the clubhouse, where the clipping with a printed headline beats the bare one. Every other screen differs only by an animation phase. To get closer to the samples, the better versions need three things:
1. Near-floor props with the samples' detail and candle-lit modelling. The passage book stacks read as black blocks, and the hedge topiary is faceted.
2. Rooms that read as paintings rather than 3D renders with smooth, untextured floor planes. The heart's armchair and the study's floor are the plainest.
3. A sharp painted backdrop behind the opening's story panel in place of the drifting blurred room.
