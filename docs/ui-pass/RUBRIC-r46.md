# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 46: THE BOARDS — THE RUN STRIP, THE LAST WEB CONTROLS, THE ATLAS, REST, EVENT, GAMEOVER

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; the
darks are where this house keeps its colour; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck; and
**a drawn object beats an empty, dimmed or shrunken one** — a board is not
better for showing less.

**Seven screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `hud-late` is
the map late in a run with ten Keepsakes and eight Gear in the run strip;
`settings` is the settings dialog over the map; `piles` is the draw-pile
dialog over a fight; `atlas` is the whole house's plan with one wing
surveyed; `rest` is the safe-room board; `event` is a choice event in an
alcove; `gameover` is the end-of-run board.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The run strip at 1280:** can you see the ten Keepsakes and eight Gear as
   distinct medallions -- or two of each and a "+8" chip?
2. **Web controls left:** the settings' colour-palette choices and the piles'
   search field -- the house's enamel and brass, or pill buttons and an
   outlined text input? Panel interiors: a material, or an even plum fill?
3. **The atlas:** are the unsurveyed wings faint ink ghosts of plans, labels
   readable -- or flat brown blotches with labels floating over nothing?
4. **rest:** a crisp, inked blanket fort with the Kid and Companion large
   enough to find -- or fuzzy felt with tiny figures? Option rows with their
   own objects, or four identical long panels?
5. **event:** choices that read as controls (slips, ruled rows), risk and gain
   words readable at 1280 -- or thin lines on a pale page? Moonlight drawn, or
   haze cones?
6. **gameover:** the Final Tricks list a clean ledger, the lists readable at
   1280 -- or a zig-zag of centred names in 10 px lists?

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The fight behind the piles dialog and the map behind settings and
  hud-late; the house's and the pets' artwork; the hung paintings and what
  they depict (they carry no nameplates by the owner's choice).
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" and "Standalone preview": the harness.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe these boards were made by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on hud-late-1280 the Gear group shows two medallions and a +6 chip"
is useful; "the HUD is cramped" tells a builder nothing.
