## THE PAINTINGS FIX (`fix/hang-fit`, 2026-10-16)

The boards were laid out for the Companions' portraits, which share one
shape; Josh's paintings run from 1024x1280 to 948x1659, so at a board's width
a tall one dropped its plate onto whatever stands below it. `ui/hang.js
fitHung` narrows each painting -- never moves it -- until its frame and plate
clear the board's obstacles, and a painting that cannot clear at 72 px gives
its place to the next of its pool that can (at 1280 the tallest canvases do
not fit over the event hall's sconces). A painting's plate wraps to its
frame's width. Gameover's own fit reserves the plate (settled over passes),
lets each frame take the bay's height, and lets the plate run the plaque's
width. Probed with every painting of each pool in each slot at 1600 and 1280
