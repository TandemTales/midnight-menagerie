# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 36: THE LAST WEB CONTROLS AND THE CROWDED HAND

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; the
darks are where this house keeps its colour; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck.

**Five screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `settings` is the
settings dialog over the map; `lobby` is the Kids' treehouse with the party's
password; `shop` is Mr. Moth's market; `piles` is the draw-pile viewer opened
in a fight; `combat-crowd` is the boss fight with nine cards in hand.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **Is anything still a web input?** The settings' seed fields, the lobby's
   password, the piles' search: engraved plates or enamel wells in the house's
   type -- or pale slabs, cream strips, a sans word in a glowing pill.
2. **Does the shop read as a stall or a store?** A price tag on each item with
   one lit press point on the item in hand -- or a grid of identical BUY
   buttons; Keepsakes and Snacks as objects on plates or under glass -- or
   ruled list rows; a curtain with lit folds -- or a gradient.
3. **The hand at nine cards, at 1280:** can you read every card's title? Do
   duplicates stack? Does the fan stay below the fighters' bars, clear of the
   Companion panel and the Nerve dial?
4. **The smaller items:** the settings' wax seal seated, not stuck on the
   frame; SAVE AND QUIT a filled plate; the lobby ribbon's stars clear of the
   letters and its bottom band dressed; the piles tray not clipping the cards;
   one close control.
5. **At 1280, can you read every line meant to be read?** Anything about 9-10
   px, or faint grey italic, is a defect.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The room behind the dialogs, the fight's room and its fighters, the map's
  parchment drawing: not this round's.
- The cards' FACES (art, frame, rules text design) in the shop, the piles and
  the hand -- a card's size and its place in the fan ARE this round's.
- The Kids' and Companions' painted art; the run strip.
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" and "Standalone preview": the harness.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe these boards were made by the same hand as
the samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on combat-crowd-1280 the three Bites overlap so only their art shows"
is useful; "the hand is crowded" tells a builder nothing.
