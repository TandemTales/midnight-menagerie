### PATTERN
The better versions draw each object as itself: crates upright and square with a lit top so a pile reads as a stack, stone that keeps its value down to the base with mouldings and a lit edge, and chair feet whose shadow is soft and belongs to the chair's cast shadow rather than a hard black ellipse. The worse versions either shear the crates into parallelograms, let stone sink into a dark silhouette, or stamp a black disc under a foot. Every difference is small on screen. What the better versions most need next: (1) the upper crates must visibly rest on something, since several still hover a few pixels over the crate below, the top crate of the passage's near-left pile is bigger than its support, and the far purple crates by the end wall float; (2) the lighter stone bench and pedestal need the room's warm candle colour and a darker shaded side so they stop reading slightly grey and evenly lit, and the red fringe on the memory pedestal's panel edge should go; (3) the chair feet need a small soft contact dark of their own, tighter than the cast shadow but never a hard-edged black shape, and the crates want the same painted contact shadow where they meet the floor.
