### PATTERN
Almost every screen pair differs only in the top strip and frame controls: the better version seats each glyph centred in a gold-rimmed, studded mount (and gives Back/Title a crested mount), while the worse leaves the glyph sliding off a bare disc toward the neighbouring number, which is worst on the Steam Deck strip. In the rooms, the better version is the one whose floor objects are lit and modelled (lit candelabra, warm gilt chair frames, a real skull heap, strapped bales, crates that catch light) rather than pale, flat or dark silhouettes; eleven of seventeen rooms are identical or differ only by embers. No fighter stands through furniture in any fight capture of either version. To reach the samples the better versions most need: (1) the remaining near-floor props painted with the same modelled light and readable surface detail as the best ones, since the lampworks crates and pumpkin bales still read as textured boxes; (2) the compact Deck strip given a little more air so the mounted medallions do not press against their numbers; (3) the plain cartouche panels and banners on boards carried to the same gilt, crested finish the round controls now have, so no control or label is left as a flat dark pill.
