# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 42: THE PALE BOARDS AND THE LAST WEB CONTROLS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; **the
darks are where this house keeps its colour**, so a pale, milky or grey board
is a defect; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck.

**Eight screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `map` is a
wing's blueprint; `hud-late` is the map late in a run with ten Keepsakes and
eight Gear in the run strip; `atlas` is the whole house's plan with one wing
surveyed, `atlas-house` with every wing surveyed; `clubhouse` is the Kids'
investigation board; `event` is a choice event in an alcove; `piles` is the
draw-pile dialog over a fight; `settings` is the settings dialog over the map.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The paper and the cork:** aged, darker sheets with burned edges and a
   lit focus -- or a broad pale, even tan or cream field that is the palest
   thing on the screen?
2. **Ink or vector?** The map's districts, the atlas's wing outlines and
   labels: hand-drawn ink with labels in cartouches -- or translucent fills
   with crisp digital outlines, faint hair-lines, labels across boundaries?
   On the map, can you tell where you can go from where you cannot?
3. **Web controls left:** the piles search, the settings seed fields and
   palette choices, the atlas legend, the clubhouse tabs, the event's
   choices -- each drawn as the house's brass and enamel, or a web input, a
   pill, a plain box, a choice with two buttons?
4. **The clubhouse's photographs:** crisp (or deliberately old prints) -- or
   soft, blurred upscales?
5. **At 1280, can you read every line meant to be read** (room tags, the KEY,
   clue notes, settings descriptions, RISK/GAIN lines)? Anything about 9-11 px
   is the defect. In the run strip, is a "+N" covering a token?

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The map's room layout and routes; the house's and the pets' artwork (judge
  how a photograph is presented, not what it shows); the event's hung
  paintings and what they depict; the fight behind the piles dialog.
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" and "Standalone preview": the harness.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe these boards were made by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on atlas-1280 the unsurveyed wings' labels are tan on tan and cross
their own outlines" is useful; "the map is pale" tells a builder nothing.
