### PATTERN
The better versions add drawn, whole objects where the worse have gaps, slabs or fragments: painted books and a globe on the near floor, a charnel stack mid-floor, pumpkins, bales and benches pulled inside the frame instead of sliced by it, and on the screens a gold filigree mount that turns two floating web buttons into a piece of frame ornament like the samples' medallions. Most pairs (10 of 17 rooms, 9 of 16 screens) are identical or differ only by animation, so the gain is local, and no fight screen in either version has a fighter standing through furniture. To reach the samples the better versions next need: (1) near-floor objects on the remaining rooms modelled and lit to the standard of the passages' books and the study globe, with crisp cast shadows tying every foot to the floor (the heart bench's soft blot and the lampworks bench crowding its crates show the risk); (2) the fight room's darks kept warm and saturated at 1280 rather than drifting to grey haze behind the fighters; (3) the same mounted, filigreed treatment carried to the remaining bare controls so nothing on a board reads as a button laid over the painting.
