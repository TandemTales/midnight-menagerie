# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 48: THE BOARDS — REST, EVENT, GAMEOVER, SHOP, AND THE FIGHT'S FLOATING WORDS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; the
darks are where this house keeps its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is
a defect; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck; and **a drawn object beats an empty,
dimmed or shrunken one** — a board is not better for showing less. But in a
dense strip **legibility beats count**: more items only win where each one
still reads.

**Five screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `rest` is the
safe-room board; `event` is a choice event in an alcove; `gameover` is the
end-of-run board; `shop` is Mr. Moth's market; `combat-floaters` is a boss
fight a moment after conditions landed on both sides, with their floating
words on screen.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **rest:** the Blanket Fort painted cloth -- folds, sag, quilting, weighted
   cushions, lantern light into real dark -- or flat vector tartan and a
   polygon beam? Four option rows each with its own object, or four identical
   bars? At 1280, does "Pack up and go on" read?
2. **event:** the moonlight soft shafts that keep the darks, with floor
   patches lying on the boards -- or flat grey wedges and pale quads? The
   parchment on a lectern with its lamp -- or one big rectangle? Is every
   line of its text in readable ink?
3. **gameover:** the Final Tricks as cards or engraved cartouches, every one
   readable -- or a two-column text table? The eight stats readable at 1280?
4. **shop:** the jars and bell jars standing on a lit counter with shadows --
   or floating on gold bars in felt boxes? Descriptions readable at 1280?
5. **combat-floaters:** the words in the house's serif on a ribbon or tag,
   beside each head -- or plain bold type across a face?

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The house's and the pets' artwork; the hung paintings and what they depict
  (they carry no nameplates by the owner's choice), and where they hang.
- On the fight: the room, the creatures' and the Kid's art, the enemy plates,
  the run rail, the hand of cards.
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" and "Standalone preview": the harness.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe these boards were made by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on gameover-1280 the stat strip's SCUFFLES WON and BIG SCARES labels
touch" is useful; "the board is cramped" tells a builder nothing.
