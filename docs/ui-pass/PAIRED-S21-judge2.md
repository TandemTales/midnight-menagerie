### PATTERN
Fifteen of the 33 pairs show no real difference (ten rooms are byte-for-byte the same picture), so the verdict rests on two changes: on the screens, the better version puts gold filigree brackets on the left-frame book and help medallions, which moves them from bare discs toward the samples' rimmed enamel buttons; in the rooms, the better version puts modelled, painted, lit objects on the near and middle floor (globe, painted books, skull stack, extra chair) and pulls benches, pumpkins and candle stands fully inside the frame instead of letting the edge cut them. No fight screen in either version has a fighter standing through furniture. The better versions most need next: (1) the same painted-surface treatment on the remaining near props, which still read as smooth 3D models rather than brushwork (the lampworks crates, the ballroom chairs, the pumpkins); (2) darks that hold colour instead of falling to black along the room sides and far floor (study, heart, crypt and ballroom edges); (3) the web-flat controls that remain on the boards, such as the map's pencil and tool strip and the gameover stat strip, drawn as cartouches and enamel like the samples.
