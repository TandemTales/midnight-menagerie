### PATTERN
The better versions differ in two ways only: on screens, every small round control (strip discs, Title/Back, the left-frame book and help) sits in a gold-rimmed mount of one family instead of a bare dark disc or dull bronze ring; in rooms, near and mid-floor props are lit and modelled (straw courses, lit crates, flames on candelabra, a true heap of skulls, contact shadows) instead of dark or flat. Ten of the seventeen rooms are identical, and three screens differ only by animation. No fighter stands through furniture in any fight capture. What the better versions most need next: (1) the rimmed strip medallions crowd the numbers between them at 1280 ("137", "2", "4" sit tight between rings) and need a little air; (2) the rooms' prop surfaces still read as rendered rather than painted and want brushed detail and firmer contact shadows throughout, not just on the repaired props; (3) the far corners and side walls of several rooms (kitchens, passages, lampworks) still fall close to black and need to hold colour.
