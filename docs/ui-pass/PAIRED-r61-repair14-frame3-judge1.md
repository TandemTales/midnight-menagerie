### PATTERN
The two versions differ only in the help and discoveries controls. One has labelled brass plaques that move from screen to screen: top right, bottom left, bottom right, or above the discard pile. The other has two small icon-only enamel medallions that always sit at the left edge, halfway down. The plaques read better and keep their framing when focused, but they cover the corner candle on the map and atlas screens and poke out from under the Draw Pile and Settings dialogs. The medallions are consistent and never peek out from under a dialog. But when one is focused it turns into a flat, web-style text box that lands on the map's "THE WAY IN" lettering, and at mid-wall it reads as unexplained, in no spot a player would look. Neither matches the samples, which put round enamel buttons with gold rims in a fixed corner of the frame (selectKid's bottom corners). Neither version gives these controls a single fixed corner that keeps clear of candles and dialogs. Both should keep their labels and frames when focused.
