### PATTERN
All four pairs are identical above the near floor; the only change is in the foreground light across the bottom third of the frame. In three of the four rooms the build that keeps the near floor warmer, more saturated and lit to the foot wins. The one exception is graveyard, and there the same rule picks the other letter. None of the differences is large, and neither build is hazier or softer than the other.
