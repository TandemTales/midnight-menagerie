# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 38: THE MAP'S STAMPS AND REGIONS, AND THE ATLAS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; the
darks are where this house keeps its colour; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck.

**Three screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `map` is a
wing's blueprint (the rooms as stamps, the routes, two marked regions, the
boss); `atlas` is the whole house's plan at the start of a run, one wing
surveyed; `atlas-house` is the same plan with every wing surveyed.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **The map's stamps:** do the room nodes have weight and a hierarchy --
   the reachable next row clearly the focal point, passed or far rows a
   lighter ghost -- or are they identical thin grey rings on one flat field?
2. **The map's regions:** drawn ON the paper (hatching, stipple, a dashed
   surveyor's line, a small label) -- or translucent tinted rectangles with a
   felt-tip outline? At 1280, does the boss plate cover a region or a node?
3. **The atlas with one wing surveyed:** can you read the house's shape (each
   unsurveyed wing a crisp pencil outline with a label) and is the one
   surveyed wing a legible inked plan -- not a black blot -- and the focal
   point?
4. **The atlas with every wing surveyed:** seventeen distinct, legible wings,
   or a dense dark tangle?
5. **At 1280, can you read every line meant to be read?** The map's edge help
   text and KEY above all; anything about 9-10 px or faint grey is a defect.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The run strip across the top, and the room behind the parchment.
- WHICH rooms and routes the map has (that is the generated blueprint) --
  only how they are drawn.
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" and "Standalone preview": the harness.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe these sheets were drawn by the same hand as
the samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on atlas-1280 the surveyed Foyer at top left is a black tangle whose
rooms cannot be told apart" is useful; "the map is flat" tells a builder
nothing.
