the run rail and the hand, the fight's room. And rounds 8-18 had judged the
rooms at `--tier high`, while this machine and the Steam Deck both run MEDIUM
(`deck-runs-medium-tier`). So before briefing round 22, the whole game was
photographed again (`survey-2026-09-30.sh`, from `ca8b4a4`, into
`C:/UILOOP/survey2/NOW/`): the fifteen screens at both sizes, and the
seventeen wings' fight rooms, empty, at 1280x800 and the Deck's tier. Two
blind judges scored them with `SURVEY2-judge-prompt.md`, in opposite orders.
Their full judgments are `C:/UILOOP/survey2/judge1.md` and `judge2.md`.

## THE SHARED DEFECTS — both judges, independently

1. **WINGS WITHOUT THEIR NAME.** "The Hedge has no maze, the Bathhouse no
   bath, the Attic no telescope, the Pumpkin grounds only noise cubes", and
   the Lampworks has one lamp. The Hedge's hedges are "knee-high noise blobs
   on a paved plaza"; the Pumpkin Grounds' pumpkins are "orange cubes of
   speckled noise"; the Kennels' beds are "TV static".
2. **A WALL THAT IS A TEXTURE, NOT A BUILT WALL.** Passages' "pale, milky
   lilac cloud texture with thin outline rectangles standing in for panels"
   (the brightest dark in any room); the Kitchens' "pale silver foil sheet"
   with its range drawn as outline circles on it; the Attic's rafters
   "scrawled over a blue cloud texture"; the Sleeping Quarters' electric-blue
   flat; the Lampworks' and the Bathhouse's mottled blue grids.
3. **PIXEL-STEPPED, BLURRED SPRITES** — the Greenhouse plants, the Heart's
   statues, the Graveyard's headstones, the Ballroom's piano: "upscaled
   low-res sprites that both stair-step and blur".
4. **THE HEART'S FLOOR SEAM** — a hard horizontal line across the whole
   floor at y≈620. A bug.
5. **THE BOARDS' WALL PICTURES** behind reward, event, rest and gameover are
   still smeared noise at eye level (round 19 hung portraits in some frames,
   not these).
6. **1280 BUGS**: "Luck +1" runs into the rail's "SNACKS" caption on every run
   screen, and settings' bottom panels are clipped by its footer. Both fixed
   outside the loop on `ui/fix-1280`.
7. **The dialogs' web controls** (piles' selects, settings' switches) and
   the flat void behind them.

Empty rooms show their whole floor, which is under the hand in a fight: "the
floor fills the lower half" is partly the capture, not the room.
