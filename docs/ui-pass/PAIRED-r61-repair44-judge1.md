### PATTERN
The better versions are the ones where an object visibly acts on the floor it stands on: a candle that lights the boards or tiles round its foot, and a chair whose feet meet a dark shadow rather than hovering over a detached one. None of the differences made a dark pale or milky; the lit pools sit on the light tiles and boards and the blacks hold. The differences are small and local, and the rest of each frame is pixel-identical, so no room changes its overall standing. Next, the better versions most need: (1) both effects together in every room, since the foyer's grounded feet and the candle pools currently come in opposite versions; (2) the foot shadows shaped by the object and the light direction rather than as flat black ellipses, and candle pools that fall off softly instead of ending in a straight vertical edge as by the ballroom's left stand; (3) painted, modelled detail on the near objects themselves (chair frames, book spines, stands) so they approach the samples' drawn quality rather than relying on floor light alone.
