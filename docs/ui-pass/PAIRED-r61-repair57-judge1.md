### PATTERN
The better version in every pair is the same treatment: moss that hugs edges and shoulders instead of identical round discs on the face, lantern-lit stone that warms to buff instead of orange, and a drawn fringe of grass where the nearest stones meet the ground instead of a fade to black. It loses only on tomb lids, where the moss is thinned so far that it stops reading at 1x. It most needs three things next:
1. Break the grass fringe up: uneven clump heights, blades overlapping the stone and spilling past its corners, and the lime-bright strips in plots brought down to the surrounding grass value so it stops reading as a comb-toothed band.
2. Restore a readable, irregular cushion of moss on the tomb lids and the nearest arched shoulders, draped over the lip with a lit top edge.
3. Paint the ground in front of the nearest stones (turf, leaves, a cast shadow with an edge) instead of the black pool that both versions still have.
