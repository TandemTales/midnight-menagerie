### PATTERN
The better versions share three traits: stone that takes the room's warm light instead of sitting in it as cold grey, stacked crates that are smaller square boxes scaled to their depth, and a tight dark contact pool under a chair foot instead of a soft haze. All of these are small gains, and the losing versions keep two real virtues, crisper bright edges on the pedestal and larger, more legible single crates. What the better versions most need next:
1. Upper crates that actually touch the crate below. Several still hover with a 2-3 px gap and a sideways offset, and none casts a shadow onto its base.
2. The pedestal's and urn's edges back, as a warm lit rim and a properly dark shaded side, so the warm stone does not soften into the floor; plus a cast shadow at the pedestal foot.
3. Painted detail on these near props (plank grain and nail heads on crates, chips and veining on stone, a soft wider shadow under the chair body to go with the tight foot pools).
