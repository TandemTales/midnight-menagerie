## THE SHARED DEFECTS — both judges named every one, independently

A defect shared by several screens is worth one fix, and these are the ones
to spend a round on first.

1. **THE BOARD WALL'S EMPTY FRAMES.** The room behind reward, event, rest and
   gameover carries a row of gold picture frames around empty black
   rectangles — "holes in the wall, not paintings" — and on reward and event
   the gothic windows are drawn OVER them: "a window cannot overlap a picture
   frame." It is one shared room (the CSS room shell), so one fix buys points
   on four or five screens. Atlas has a related grey stone panel peeking out
   at its left edge; gameover a grey-framed empty wall panel.
2. **CONTROLS THAT ARE STILL WEB FORM ELEMENTS IN GILT.** The settings toggles
   ("a dark disc beside a thin bar with OFF in it"), sliders and seed inputs;
   the pile viewer's five filter selects with chevron circles and its search
   field; the shop's icon-font glyphs for keepsakes and snacks (three snacks
   share one glyph); the rest room's lozenge pills and line icons in rings;
   the event's tiny black chevron discs; the clubhouse's Haunt steppers. Six
   screens. One shared brass-and-enamel control kit lifts all of them.
3. **THE RUN HUD WRAPS TO TWO ROWS AT 1280x800** on both fights: Haunt, the
   seed, Tricks and the gear drop to a second row and double the frame's top
   border. **1280x800 is the Steam Deck.** One compaction covers every in-run
   screen.
4. **THE FIGHT'S ROOM.** Both judges: visible pixel stair-stepping on the
   staircase, rails and columns behind the fight ("an upscaled low-res
   sprite"), and **the Kid and the Companion float with no floor under them**,
   a third the height of the Butler in his mirror, on no shared ground plane.
   The stair-stepping needs a discriminator before anyone draws: the stage
   CALIBRATES its render scale from measured frame times, and these captures
   were taken while two builders held the GPU — so the scale may have been
   trimmed by the contention, not by the tier. Capture with the GPU idle
   first.

Smaller, but seen by both:
- **A grey hump and side diamonds peek out behind every card's top edge** in
  the pile viewer's tray and on the reward rack — "a second card back
  mis-registered". A rendering bug in the card, not a style.
- **"STANDALONE PREVIEW"** sits on four board captures. It is a harness label

- **Two hands**: crisp inked Kids and cards next to soft, airbrushed renders

## Per screen — the worst problem, and the change that buys most of it

- **map** (both +2): the routes are loose, same-weight brush strokes that
  stop short of the nodes and cross one another, so "you cannot trace which
  room leads to which". Draw every route as one continuous uncrossed ink line
  rim to rim, reachable paths bold; nodes on parchment roundels; fade the
  floor plan behind; remove the unexplained watercolour blobs and red ball.
- **settings** (+1.5 to +2): brass levers or two-position engraved plates for
  the toggles; a grooved slider track; engraved-tag seed fields; a solid
  scrim so the screen behind does not show through at the bottom edge.
- **piles** (+1.5 to +2): the filter row as gilt tabs or engraved dial plates
  and the search as an inked parchment slip; a tray tall enough to show whole
  cards; the card-back hump gone.
- **combat-crowd / combat-boss** (+1 to +2): a floor plane with contact
  shadows under the Kid, the pup and the mirror; a one-row HUD at 1280; past
  six cards, a wider fan that keeps title ribbons readable.
- **event** (+1 to +2): the lead-in words after the drop cap are "ghost
  outline type almost invisible on the parchment" — set them in solid ink;
  soften the fold creases off the text; the empty frames behind the windows.
- **rest** (+1 to +2): the Blanket Fort vignette's scale is wrong (a door
  three Kids tall, a fort that is one fuzzy lump) — a table under a draped
  blanket, the Kid at the right size, planks nailed to the door.
- **gameover** (+1): the right ledger crams ~25 rows of 9 px type at 1280 and
  runs into its frame, two keepsakes show a placeholder star; the Worked
  Hardest card shrinks to unreadable at 1280.
- **clubhouse** (+1 to +1.5): the pet photos (an asset question, above); the
  red string runs straight through a photo instead of pin to pin.
- **atlas** (+1): walls drawn as "beaded chains" rather than poché; the
  surveyed wing inked like the sixteen unsurveyed ones.
- **shop, reward, lobby** (+1 each), **opening** (+0.5 to +1).
