# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 24: THE HOUSE'S OWN ROOMS

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; and **a room that is
SOFT, hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step** — a
line should be crisp AND smooth, like ink.

**Six screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms — `sheet-nursery`
(the nursery, the sewing room), `sheet-sleeping` (the dormitory, a bedroom
with its hearth), `sheet-study` (the library, the fireplace study),
`sheet-crypt` (the catacomb, the chapel, the ossuary) — and two fights,
`fight-nursery` and `fight-study`, each also at 1280x800 (the Steam Deck).
Everything was drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **Is each room's subject DRAWN?** A crib has spindles and a canopy; a
   four-poster has posts, a canopy and curtains; a bookcase has books you can
   count, not vertical streaks; an ossuary has skulls and bones you can see.
   A crate, a box with a texture pasted on it, or a streak field is NOT the
   object. Would a viewer told only "the Nursery", "the Sleeping Quarters",
   "the Library", "the Crypt" point at the right room?
2. **Is every wall BUILT?** Courses, panels, mouldings, leaves and joints with
   relief and light — or a mottled cloud texture with lines drawn on it? The
   second is the defect this round exists to remove.
3. **Are the darks dark?** An electric-blue or lavender-washed wall costs
   `palette` and `background`.
4. **Is each room of a wing a different room**, not the same room re-lit,
   and do the Nursery and the Sleeping Quarters stop sharing one template?
4. **Each room of a wing is a room that wing really has**, with the wing's
   palette and arch, and no two rooms of a wing the same room re-lit.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fights: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. A large bare floor on a sheet is the capture.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-crypt's ossuary the niches are a blue blur with no skull you can
point at" is useful; "the crypt is weak" tells a builder nothing.
