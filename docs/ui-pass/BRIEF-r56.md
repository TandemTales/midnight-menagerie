# Round56 — painted surfaces that survive native size

Start from the selected Round55 build recorded in RESULTS-r55.json. Read
BRIEF-r0.md, BRIEF-r55.md and RUBRIC.md, then inspect all four reference
paintings first. This brief supersedes overlapping Round55 priorities. The
first two Round55 reviewers split between IOLITE and ALMANDINE; the third
selected IOLITE, giving it two of three votes. None granted any screen
reference-fit. Their shared objections matter more than
small differences in their scores: condition strips remain thin outlines,
small icons look smooth, letter bevels are uniform, and card rims and disks
look too mechanically even. Change the visible native pixels substantially.
Do not merely add another nearly identical gradient over the same surface.

## Highest-value changes

1. **Give the small objects the same painted materials as their surroundings.**
   Inspect the actual visible rest pillow and sharpening icon implementation
   before editing; do not infer their source from a fallback name. The pillow
   needs fabric volume, irregular folds and warm reflected light; the sharpening
   object needs convincing worn metal or leather according to the object it
   actually depicts. The candle can gain wax detail. Their native medallions
   should match the blanket-fort illustration. A new imagegen edit of the
   existing raster icon is allowed when its current procedural surface cannot
   achieve this. Preserve true alpha, object identity, native size and all live
   labels. Keep replacements rest-scoped; do not alter shared icons elsewhere.
   Code-native editable vector details may remain native where appropriate.
2. **Make condition plaques dimensional inside their existing bounds.** Their
   actual small display needs a visible side return, raised worn lip, recessed
   plum face and localized brass catches. Inspect the final nine-slice result,
   not just a large asset preview: source margins and slice scaling can erase
   the very relief that made a generated asset attractive. Alpha1 margins
   required deterministic crop repairs in two Round55 builds. Keep transparent
   originals and exact crop provenance; never repaint a generated bitmap with
   code. Preserve owned padding1px9px, text size, the measured envelope and local
   owner captions. No new unreserved shadow/outset or added padding.
3. **Put painted variation into live letter faces.** Lavender titles need
   distinct carved planes, selective worn edges and irregular local light that
   remain visible at1280. The cyan/coral condition words can also receive subtle
   material variation while retaining their semantic hue, computed color,
   readable signs, fonts and actual enlarged metrics. The current Strength
   opacity is a real fade phase; do not normalize opacity or change its lifetime.
   Presentation-only live-text markup or a carefully bounded SVG/filter/mask is
   permitted when it produces a real improvement. All strings remain live,
   accessible text; no baked lettering. A minimal inner paint span in condition
   construction is allowed if needed, but leave queue/owner/packing/lifecycle,
   numeric transforms, DOM+Range union and asymmetric reservation unchanged.
   Any such change needs explicit before/after geometry and full existing
   lifecycle/ink checks. Preserve every selector, ID, action and ARIA value.
4. **Finish shop objects locally.** Keep merchant, curtains, three large cards,
   prices and all rules. Cost disks and outer rims need worn edge planes and
   uneven highlights. The large flat shield silhouettes in card wells can gain
   low-contrast painted or embossed depth. Inspect the real card implementation,
   then scope all changes to shop merchandise; never change the combat hand or
   generic card skin. Keep the value/type/rules hierarchy and contrast intact.
5. **Make the remaining small surfaces physical without redoing the layout.**
   Memorial deck rows and number badges need shallow enamel returns and local
   wear, while their quantity bays stay clear. The event bookmark needs folded
   cloth thickness and a small believable contact shadow; retain its red color.
   The illuminated initial can gain restrained engraving. Rails should keep
   their narrow physical lip, quiet faces and opaque row/paper occlusion, with
   localized joint/end wear instead of evenly repeated bright fittings. Keep
   the initial story, first option and complete memorial hierarchy visible.

Prioritize a few strong improvements over touching every selector. Retain an
existing surface when an attempted replacement is visibly worse. IOLITE's quiet
rail grain, JADE's softer pillow folds and ZIRCON's light/dark first-choice bevel
were specific Round55 observations, not blanket authorization to copy sibling
Round56 work. Each builder remains independent.

The third review also identified soft cone-like event window light and a broad,
generic rest wall field. If the high-priority objects are successful, existing
rest/event CSS may gain broken painted light falloff or restrained damask/local
wall detail. Do not replace room art, generate whole scenes, move actors or
expand into combat hand/icon systems. Its event-rail graft was selective inset
carved relief, not repeated arches across the whole rail.
