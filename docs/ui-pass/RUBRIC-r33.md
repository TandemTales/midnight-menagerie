# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 33: THE CONTROLS AND THE SMALL TEXT

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: readable,
accurately drawn objects matter more than matching the samples' hues; the
darks are where this house keeps its colour; 1280x800 is the Steam Deck.

**Four screens, each at the default size and at 1280x800.** `settings` is the
settings dialog over the map; `piles` is the draw-pile viewer opened in a
fight; `shop` is Mr. Moth's market; `hud-late` is the map late in a run, with
ten Keepsakes and eight Gear in the run strip across the top.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **At 1280, can you read every line meant to be read?** Help text under
   each setting, shop descriptions, prices, button lettering, filter values.
   Anything about 9-10 px, or faint lavender-grey italic, is the defect.
2. **Do the controls read as objects of this house or as a web form?**
   Buttons as enamel discs with cartouches, sliders as engraved brass rails
   with a knob, the piles filters as one engraved rail of medallions -- or
   dark stadium pills, styled range inputs, a row of "< All types >"
   spinners.
3. **Shop: one control per row?** Price and BUY locked together into one
   thing, or two neighbouring controls crowding each other.
4. **hud-late's run strip at 1280:** can you tell the Keepsakes and Gear
   apart (each about 30 px or more, a gap between them, in the house's
   palette rather than bright sticker colours)? Are HAUNT and the seed still
   labelled?

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- The room behind the dialogs and the map's parchment drawing (its nodes,
  zones and room labels): not this round's.
- The cards' faces in piles and shop; the Keepsakes' and Gear's underlying
  pictures (a builder may only treat them on the strip -- tint, ring, size).
- The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" and "Standalone preview": the harness.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe these boards were made by the same hand as
the samples? For `worst_problem`, name the screen, the size, the element and
where: "on settings-1280 the help line under Flashes is about 9px grey
italic" is useful; "the text is small" tells a builder nothing.
