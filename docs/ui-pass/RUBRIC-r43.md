# Blind style judgment — rubric, round 43: THE HEDGE, THE ATTIC, THE CRYPT, THE BATHHOUSE

Read `RUBRIC.md` first for the 0-10 scale, the eight fields and the blindness
rules; this file wins where they disagree. Standing rulings: a screen's
COLOUR is not held to the samples' hues — readable, accurately drawn,
well-detailed objects are what matter; the darks are where this house keeps
its colour, so a pale, milky or grey dark is a defect; **a room that is SOFT,
hazy or smeared is a worse defect than one whose lines stair-step**; and **a
drawn object beats an empty, dimmed or muted surface** — a room is not better
for having less in it.

**Five screens.** Four contact sheets of empty fight rooms as a player meets
them -- `sheet-hedge` (the maze, the fountain court, the yew walk),
`sheet-attic` (the attic, the attic library), `sheet-crypt` (the catacomb,
the family vault, the ossuary), `sheet-bathhouse` (the bathhouse, the steam
room, the pool) -- and one fight, `fight-crypt`, also at 1280x800 (the Steam
Deck). Everything was drawn at the Deck's quality tier.

## WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST

1. **Each wing's subject, drawn:**
   - the Hedge: leafy, ragged yew tops with withered gaps and a drawn edge,
     a deep green lawn with leaves -- or ruler-straight noise boxes over a
     milky grey lawn?
   - the Attic: a trunk and box with edges, straps and a lock, whole in frame,
     on a lit floor -- or soft blocks melting into a black bottom quarter?
   - the Crypt: sunken graves with stone kerbs, or sarcophagi, and a vault
     with ribs in relief -- or cardboard half-domes under a flat grey ceiling?
   - the Bathhouse: varied tubs (sizes, metals, a towel, one overturned) with
     crisp feet, window light in shafts -- or one tub cloned a dozen times
     under round spotlight discs?
2. **Its own floor:** does the Attic have a rug of its own, or the red
   diamond rug you may have seen in other rooms?
3. **The near floor** lit and dressed, nothing falling to black; ink, not
   grain; dark, not milky.
4. **Each room of a wing is a room that wing really has**, not the same room
   re-lit.

## WHAT IS NOT A DIFFERENCE ANY BUILDER MADE — do not score it

- On the fight: the run rail, the hand of cards, the Nerve dial, the piles,
  END TURN, the Companion's panel, the Kid's and the creatures' painted art.
  The rail reads "The Foyer · Wing 1" on every fight: the harness's mock run.
- The contact sheets show EMPTY rooms: in a fight the lower third of the
  floor is under the hand. Judge the near floor's dressing and light, not
  that the capture shows it.
- The captions on the sheets.

## `fits_between_samples` and `worst_problem`

`fits`: would a viewer believe this room was painted by the same hand as the
samples? For `worst_problem`, name the sheet, the room, the object and where:
"on sheet-crypt's ossuary the two skeletons lie in flat cardboard half-domes"
is useful; "the materials are weak" tells a builder nothing.
