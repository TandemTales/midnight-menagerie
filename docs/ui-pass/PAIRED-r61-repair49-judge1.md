### PATTERN
The better versions keep their darks dark and their edges firm: cabinet glass that stays deep with the china reading against it, stone whose folds are drawn with a line and a lit rim, and a bust that is one rounded form rather than a striped patch. The worse versions add either a pale veil (the glazing) or surface pattern that does not follow the form (the bust's stripes, the dark band across the ballroom stands' stems), and both read as haze or fault rather than detail at 1280x800. The one place added articulation helped was the small parlor candle stand's socket, so the modelling idea is sound where it stays in the object's own tone. Next the better versions need: (1) the gallery bust given real features and shoulders rather than a smooth egg on a pillow shape; (2) the cabinet glass a single crisp highlight and drawn glazing bars rather than diagonal smears, with the far arches' stair-stepped tracery cleaned up; (3) the candle stands' joints modelled as pale turned collars with a shadow side, never as a dark gap, and the heart statues given faces and hands so they read as figures at the samples' level of drawn detail.
