## WHAT BOTH JUDGES SHARE (their SYSTEM sections)

1. **Hero props as untextured or low-poly primitives with soft blob shadows.**
   J1: "medallion chairs (foyer, ballroom), stick candelabra, tubs,
   doghouses, trunks, statues, the range, the globes ... one prop pass touches
   eight wings". J2: "Kennels, hedge, attic, bathhouse, sleeping, study,
   kitchens and lampworks share both".
2. **Empty dark near floors.** J1 names kennels, pumpkin, study, bathhouse,
   lampworks and passages; J2 names pumpkin, passages, study, kennels,
   lampworks, kitchens and greenhouse.
3. **Softness standing in for detail.** Both name the combat room behind the
   crisp sprites, the opening's blurred hall, the hedge walls and the heart's
   statues.
4. **Red-green colour fringing on hard edges at 1280** (J2: the ballroom
   chairs, the greenhouse columns, the foyer pediments, the kennel roofs).
   **Cause found:** `fx/shaders/grade.js` applies an edge-weighted
   chromatic aberration (`uAberration` 0.0013, growing with the distance
   from the centre). Every room passes through it, and it is worst where
   these props stand, near the frame. That is a shared rule.
5. **On the boards: a flat purple felt panel with a list or object floating
   in it.** Named are shop's lower panels, rest's four option bars,
   gameover's tricks table and settings' identical boxes. Also: drawn vector
   light where a painted one belongs (the event's and rest's beam wedges).
6. **Crowding at 1280.** Named are gameover's eight-stat strip, the run strip
   and settings.

**Not to brief again:** both independent judges ask for every Keepsake and

test found it worse ("crowded", "an unreadable row of coins"). In a dense
